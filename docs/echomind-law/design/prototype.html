What is the change-of-control notice period in the signed agreement?

Searched 38 documents in M-101 Read 6 passages Quotes checked against source
Findings

The signed Master Services Agreement requires Provider to give Client no less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any change of control. Notice must be delivered to Client's General Counsel at the address in Schedule 4.

Conflicting versions. The v4 draft (not signed) proposes sixty (60) days. The signed v3 governs unless and until v4 is executed.

Failure to give notice entitles Client to terminate for cause within ninety (90) days of learning of the change.

§14.2 Change of Control · page 11
Confidential

14. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

14.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

14.2 Provider shall provide no less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any Change of Control to Client in accordance with Clause 22.

14.3 If Provider fails to give notice under Clause 14.2, Client may terminate this Agreement for cause by written notice within ninety (90) days of becoming aware of the Change of Control.
