## 1. Purpose

To define how Ajace AI determines the competence needed to build, deploy and support EchoMind
Enterprise, how that competence is acquired and evidenced, how people are made aware of the quality
policy and of their own contribution to it, and how the organisational knowledge the work depends
on is retained.

## 2. Scope

Everyone working under Ajace AI's control, whether employed, contracted or working as the owner of
the business. It covers the competence needed for the work as it is actually performed today, not a
hypothetical future team.

It also covers a resource that is unusual but material here: **AI coding assistance**. A significant
share of the code in this repository was produced with AI assistance and is declared as such. That
is treated in §7 as a competence and control question, because that is what it is.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Managing Director | Owns this procedure. Determines the competence each role requires. Decides how any shortfall is closed. Maintains the competence matrix and the training record. |
| Lead Engineer | Identifies competence needed by new or changed technology before it enters the product. Keeps the engineering documentation in `docs/` current enough to serve as handover material. |
| Anyone contributing code | Works within their competence, says so when a task is outside it, and reviews and verifies every change they author — including any part generated by an AI assistant. |

Ajace AI currently has one person holding all three roles. Everything in this procedure must
therefore be read as self-administered, which is a weakness stated in §8 rather than hidden.

## 5. Acquiring and evidencing competence (7.2)

Where a competence is needed and not held, it is acquired by one of the routes below, and the route
is recorded in `registers/REG-05_Competence_and_Training_Record.md`.

| Route | When it is appropriate | Evidence it must leave |
|---|---|---|
| Self-directed study of primary documentation | New library, new model, new runtime | The change that follows it, with the rationale recorded in the commit body |
| Structured training or vendor course | Broad area with a safety or compliance edge — GPU platform administration, data protection law | Certificate or attendance record, referenced from the training record |
| Supervised or paired work | Once there is more than one person | Named supervisor and date in the training record |
| Proving it on a bounded piece of work | Any area where the result is measurable | The measurement — for example a golden-eval score before and after, as in commit `4e27109` |

**Evidence of competence for an existing contributor** is drawn from what the work itself left
behind: commit bodies that state the mechanism of a defect and the verification of its fix, design
documentation in `docs/`, and evaluation results in `eval/reports/` and `eval/paper/results/`. This
is legitimate evidence under 7.2(d) — "retain appropriate documented information as evidence of
competence" — provided it is actually referenced. The competence matrix therefore cites it.

Effectiveness of any action taken to acquire competence is evaluated against the work produced
afterwards, not against completion of the training.

## 6. Awareness (7.3)

Everyone working under Ajace AI's control is made aware of:

| ISO 7.3 requirement | How it is met here |
|---|---|
| The quality policy | `QP-01_Quality_Policy.md` is read at induction and on each revision. It is in the repository every contributor already clones. |
| Relevant quality objectives | `QO-01` is reviewed with the person at induction and at each management review. |
| Their contribution to the effectiveness of the QMS, including the benefits of improved performance | Stated concretely, not abstractly: a grounded, attributed answer is the product; a confident ungrounded one is a defect of the highest severity (`QP-01` §2.1). Every contributor is told which controls in the product exist to prevent that, and that they are responsible for not weakening them. |
| The implications of not conforming | Stated concretely: an isolation defect is a compliance event for a customer in a regulated sector, not only a bug. Commit `4e27109` is used as the worked example — a single retrieval path that bypassed the namespace predicate returned 10 out of 10 out-of-namespace chunks under a tenant-scoped query. |

Awareness is recorded as an entry in `registers/REG-05_Competence_and_Training_Record.md` naming the person, the
document version they were made aware of, and the date. It is not recorded in advance.

## 7. AI-assisted development

**The situation, stated accurately.** A material share of the commits in this repository were
produced with AI assistance and declare it through a `Co-Authored-By: Claude …` trailer — for
example `558eaae` and `4e27109`. Ajace AI does not treat this as incidental. It is a resource that
changes what competence means, and it is disclosed rather than obscured.

**The rule.**

> The human author of a commit is accountable for every line in it, whether they typed it or an AI
> assistant generated it. Accountability is discharged by reviewing the change and verifying that it
> does what the commit says it does. An unreviewed or unverified AI-generated change must not be
> committed.

This rule is not aspirational; it restates what the existing evidence shows already happens. The
commit bodies carry the verification, not just the intent:

- `558eaae` — diagnoses a wrong internal port, explains why the failure was silent (the exception
  was swallowed at debug level), and records the verification: "card mode 122 KB WAV, summary mode
  541 KB WAV, and through the public site 83 KB WAV (RIFF header confirmed)".
- `4e27109` — states the bypassed predicate, the measured regression (golden eval 50/52 → 42/52),
  the fix, and the post-fix audit (0/359 out-of-namespace hits; golden eval 49/52).

Both carry a `Co-Authored-By: Claude` trailer. That is the standard: AI assistance plus recorded
human verification.

**Competence implications.** Using an AI assistant does not remove the need for competence in the
area being changed; it raises it, because the contributor must be able to judge output they did not
write. A contributor must not use an AI assistant to work in an area where they could not review the
result unaided. Where they must work in such an area, the competence is acquired first, under §5.

The verification requirement is a mandatory design control and is stated again, with its place in
the design process, in `SOP-05` §7.

## 8. Organisational knowledge (7.1.6)

ISO 9001:2015 7.1.6 requires the organisation to determine the knowledge necessary for the operation
of its processes, to maintain it, and to make it available. For Ajace AI this is not a
box-ticking clause. It is one of the most serious risks the organisation carries.

**Knowledge that exists and is written down.** The following is real, current and available to
anyone with the repository:

| Knowledge | Where it is held | Size |
|---|---|---|
| What the platform is and does | `docs/CAPABILITIES.md` | 284 lines |
| Retrieval pipeline end to end | `docs/RAG_FLOW.md` | 281 lines |
| Chunking and embedding rationale | `docs/RAG_AND_CHUNKING_EXPLAINED.md` | 315 lines |
| Per-message chat path and LLM call accounting | `docs/CHAT_AND_RAG_FLOW.md` | 273 lines |
| Session and summary handling | `docs/CHAT_SESSION_AND_SUMMARY_FLOW.md` | — |
| Conversational AI and wake-word behaviour | `docs/CONVERSATION_AI_AND_WAKE_WORD_FLOW.md` | 181 lines |
| Transcript storage | `docs/TRANSCRIPT_STORAGE_FLOW.md` | 171 lines |
| Live-transcription WebSocket protocol | `backend/app/transcribe/PROTOCOL.md` | 43 lines |
| Self-audit of the RAG pipeline | `docs/RAG_AUDIT.md` (dated February 2026) | 183 lines |
| End-user operation | `docs/USER_MANUAL.md`, 16 chapters | 1257 lines |
| Offline and public deployment | `OFFLINE_DEPLOYMENT.md`, `docs/PUBLIC_DEPLOYMENT.md` | — |
| Why the infrastructure is configured as it is | Inline comments at `docker-compose.yml:7-11`, `41-44`, `69-72`, `108-112` | — |
| Why individual changes were made, and how they were verified | Commit bodies, e.g. `558eaae`, `4e27109` | 202 commits |

**Knowledge that exists only in one person's head.** This is the gap, and it is large. The written
material above describes *what the system does*. It does not fully capture the operational knowledge
needed to keep it running: which failure modes present as which symptoms, which recovery action to
take for each, what "normal" looks like on the reference hardware, what has already been tried and
rejected for a given problem, and the reasoning behind decisions made before the commit-body
discipline was adopted (only 80 of 202 commits use conventional prefixes; the convention was adopted
partway through the project's life).

**Maintaining and making it available.** The following are the standing requirements:

1. A change that alters observable behaviour updates the affected document in `docs/` in the same
   commit as the code.
2. A commit body states the rationale and the verification, in the form already demonstrated by
   `558eaae` and `4e27109`. This is the primary mechanism by which knowledge survives the person.
3. Where a failure mode is diagnosed and recovered, the diagnosis and the recovery are written down
   — in the relevant document in `docs/`, not only in the commit — so that a second person could
   perform the recovery.
4. Knowledge needed from outside the organisation (model documentation, NVIDIA platform
   documentation, the ISO standard text) is identified in
   `registers/REG-02_External_Documents_and_Providers.md` with the version in use.

**Knowledge required for a change in circumstances** (7.1.6, second paragraph) is determined at the
same time as the change itself, under `SOP-02` §9. The two changes in circumstance that would demand
new knowledge immediately are: onboarding a second person, and moving off the GB10 reference
platform.

## 10. Current state and gaps

**No competence matrix exists.** `registers/REG-05_Competence_and_Training_Record.md` is created by this QMS and
is empty of assessed entries until the Managing Director completes it. The competence areas in §4
are determined from the work in the repository; the assessment of who holds them is not yet made.

**No training records exist.** There is no `registers/REG-05_Competence_and_Training_Record.md` content, no
certificates referenced anywhere in the repository, and no record of any training having been taken.
This does not mean no learning occurred — the repository is evidence that a great deal did — it
means none of it was recorded as required by 7.2(d).

**No induction material exists.** There is nothing in the repository that a new contributor could
read to be brought up to the required awareness under 7.3. Until one exists, `QP-01`, this
procedure, `SOP-01` §9 and `SOP-05` §7 are the reading list, and the induction record is a signed
entry in the training record.

**Bus factor is 1.** The organisation has one developer, working under two Git identities (`RW2523`
and `2523`) with a single email address, across 202 commits from 2026-02-05 to `ff29843` on
2026-09-21. Every specialised competence in §4 is held by that one person. There is no second person
who could:

- diagnose a GPU or driver-level failure on the reference hardware;
- recover a deployment after a model-serving failure;
- judge whether an AI-generated change is correct;
- continue any customer engagement.

This is recorded in `registers/REG-03_Risk_Register.md` under `SOP-02` §7 and is, on that scale, a
high-impact risk with no current treatment beyond the written documentation listed in §8.

**Awareness has not been formally delivered or recorded**, because the quality policy `QP-01` is
itself still `DRAFT — not yet approved`. Awareness activity begins when the policy is approved.

**Self-assessment is the only assessment available.** With one person, the determination of
competence, the judgement that it is sufficient, and the record of it are all made by the same
person. An auditor should treat competence evidence here as self-declared and weigh the objective
evidence — commit bodies, evaluation results, working deployment — more heavily than the
declaration. Ajace AI states this openly rather than presenting the matrix as independently
verified.

All gaps above are carried into `ISO9001_Gap_Analysis.md`.
