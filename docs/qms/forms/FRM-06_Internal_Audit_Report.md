## When to use this form

For every audit event in the programme at `SOP-13` §4.1, and for any unscheduled audit. Issued to
the Managing Director within 10 working days of the closing meeting. Also used for an interim
**self-assessment**, which is titled as such in §1 and is not a conforming internal audit — see
`SOP-13` §5.

## Completion notes

- **§1 is the first section for a reason.** An audit report whose independence position is buried is
  misleading. Answer the "own work" question honestly; the QMS already records the consequence.
- **Conformity and effectiveness are different conclusions.** A QMS can be fully conformant on paper
  and ineffective in practice, and the reverse. Answer both.
- **A finding needs all three parts.** "Configuration management is weak" is an opinion. "Clause
  8.6: release `____` has no recorded source revision; `FRM-03` for it shows an empty SHA field" is
  a finding.
- **Do not classify a nonconformity as an OFI to be kind.** The classification drives the response;
  softening it removes the response.
- **§8 closes on verified effectiveness, not on action completed** — see `SOP-14` §8.
- Sampling must be stated in §5. An unstated sample cannot be judged by the next auditor.
