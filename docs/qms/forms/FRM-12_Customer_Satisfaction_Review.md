which is not the same as whether the delivery met the specification.

## Completion notes

- Record the customer's words, not a paraphrase that sounds better. The value of this form is that
  it carries information the organisation would not otherwise hear.
- A "satisfied" customer who describes a workaround they have adopted has told you about a defect.
  Raise it under SOP-10.
- Negative feedback with no corresponding entry in `REG-04` is a sign the form is being used to
  record impressions rather than to drive action.
