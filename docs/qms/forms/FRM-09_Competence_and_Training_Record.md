## When to use this form

One record per person, created on engagement and reviewed at least annually and on any change of
role. Clause 7.2 requires the organisation to determine the necessary competence, ensure the person
is competent on the basis of education, training or experience, take action where they are not, and
**retain documented information as evidence of competence**. This form is that evidence.

## Completion notes

- **Competences are determined from the role, then evidenced** — not listed from what the person
  happens to have. Start from what the work requires: the product runs offline in regulated sectors,
  so the required set usually includes data isolation and residency, secure configuration, the
  evaluation methodology, container and GPU deployment, and the QMS procedures the role owns.
- **"Evidence held" must be checkable.** A qualification, a completed course with a date, or
  specific delivered work referenced by path or commit. "Many years of experience" is not evidence.
- **§3 is not attendance.** Effectiveness is shown by what the person can now do that they could not
  before — a task performed correctly, a review passed, a defect class that stopped recurring.
- **Record gaps honestly in §5, including the owner's own.** A one-person organisation has
  competence gaps by arithmetic; the QMS value is in naming them and deciding what to do, not in
  claiming completeness.
- **Where a gap cannot be closed internally**, the action in §6 is to engage an external party —
  as with the internal-audit impartiality position in `SOP-13` §5.
- This record contains personal data. Retain per `SOP-01` §8 (duration of engagement + 3 years) and
  keep it out of any publicly readable repository.
