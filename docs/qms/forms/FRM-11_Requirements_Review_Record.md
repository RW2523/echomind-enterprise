**When to use.** Before committing to supply anything to a customer — a deployment, a customisation,
a pilot or a trial. ISO 9001:2015 8.2.3.2 makes the *record* of this review mandatory, not optional.
Complete it again whenever requirements change (8.2.4).

## Completion notes

- **"Requirements not stated but necessary"** is where most disputes originate. Spend the time here.
- **Acceptance criteria** written in §5 become the design inputs in `FRM-01` and the verification
  target in `FRM-03`. If they are vague here, nothing downstream can be verified.
- If the customer states a requirement verbally, 8.2.3.1 requires it to be confirmed before
  acceptance — record where that confirmation lives.
