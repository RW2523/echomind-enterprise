    feat(llm): port chat to Qwen3-30B-A3B NVFP4 on TensorRT-LLM (driver fix confirmed)

    (CVD ordinal masking — verified: CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 now yields
    device_count=1, and spawned/forked CUDA workers initialize cleanly). TRT-LLM
    1.2.0rc6 serves nvidia/Qwen3-30B-A3B-FP4 again with NVFP4 CUTLASS kernels.
    
    - trtllm: un-gated (profile removed), restart: unless-stopped; regression
      playbook documented in the service comment.
    - backend + voice: LLM_BASE_URL/LLM_URL -> trtllm:8355, model
      nvidia/Qwen3-30B-A3B-FP4 (thinking disabled via chat_template_kwargs as
      before). Ollama stays for embeddings + as a documented one-line rollback.
    - README: architecture/model tables updated to match.
    
    Measured on the box: decode ~37 tok/s steady; prefill ~3k tok/s (3,633-token
    prompt end-to-end in 1.2 s) vs ~340 tok/s on ollama — ~10x faster RAG TTFT at
    30B quality. Golden eval: 48/52 maintained, doc-precision 0.98, retrieval
    median 6.2s -> 5.4s, refusal median 4.6s -> 1.3s. GPU: ~37 GB (weights + KV
    at TRTLLM_KV_FRACTION=0.5) alongside voice ASR + backend; 56 GB headroom.
