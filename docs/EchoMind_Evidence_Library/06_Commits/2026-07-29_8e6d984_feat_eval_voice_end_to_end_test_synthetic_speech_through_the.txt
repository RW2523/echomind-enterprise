    feat(eval): voice end-to-end test — synthetic speech through the live WS loop
    
    Synthesizes a spoken question with Piper (/speak), feeds it into the voice
    WebSocket as real-time 20 ms PCM frames, and measures the full pipeline (VAD →
    Nemotron partials → semantic endpoint → Parakeet final → intent routing →
    LLM/RAG → phrase-chunked Piper TTS). Reports the heard transcript, the reply,
    and end-of-speech-relative latencies (asr_final / first reply / first audio).
    
    Measured with Qwen3-30B-A3B-FP4 on TRT-LLM: word-perfect STT on both turns,
    first audio +0.41 s (casual) and +0.64 s (full RAG turn; 30B answer streaming
    from +1.14 s).
