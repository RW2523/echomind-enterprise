## Remaining risks

- A restating sentence with no citation of its own and no explicit attribution ("The termination notice period is 60
  days." before a cited quote) stays unsupported by design: adjacency alone also clears contradicting sentences ("… for
  the Supplier only").
- When the model declines with the bare "The documents in this matter do not answer that.", there is no specific absence
  to show; the generic text remains.
- The date calculator reads common English drafting only (begins/commences/effective, initial term of N months/years,
  renews for N, at least N days before the term ends, the Effective Date); anything else gives no calculation rather than
  a wrong one. Notice-delivery and deemed-receipt clauses are not applied; business days are not calculated.
- The research page does not yet show the calculation (saved research turns have no field for it, and a reopened
  answer would differ from the live one); the chat card and the research API carry it.
- `relocate` trusts a quote found word for word in exactly one document; a model quoting the right words for the wrong
  proposition is still caught only by the attribution and misattribution checks, as before.
