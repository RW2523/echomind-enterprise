Lease of Unit 12 Riverside Works (DEMO)

THIS LEASE is made on this 24 June 2016.

BETWEEN:

Pembroke Estates Ltd of 12 High Street, Pembroke, acting by its authorised representative, Mr. A. Thompson, and

Harbourview Studios Ltd of 12 Riverside Works, acting by its authorised representative, Ms. L. Carter.

1. PREMISES

The lessor hereby lets to the tenant, and the tenant hereby takes from the lessor, the premises known as Unit 12, Riverside Works, situated in Pembroke, for the term of 15 years from 24 June 2016.

2. RENT

The tenant shall pay to the lessor the sum of GBP 96,000 per annum in accordance with the terms of this Lease.

3. PAYMENT OF RENT

3.1 The rent shall be payable in quarterly instalments, each instalment being the sum of GBP 24,000.

4. RENT REVIEW

The rent shall be subject to an upward-only open-market rent review on 24 June 2026. The parties shall, in accordance with the Landlord and Tenant (Covenants) Act 1995, carry out the review process with due diligence and in good faith.

5. REPAIRS AND INSURANCE

The tenant shall keep the premises in a state of repair and shall maintain all necessary insurance in respect of the premises.

6. UTILITIES

The tenant shall be responsible for all utility charges relating to the premises, including but not limited to water, electricity, and gas.

7. ASSIGNMENT AND UNDERLETTING

The tenant shall not assign or underlet the premises without the prior written consent of the lessor.

8. REPAIRING AND INSURING OBLIGATIONS

The tenant shall bear all costs of repairing and maintaining the premises and shall keep the premises insured against all reasonable risks.

9. BREAK CLAUSE

The tenant shall have the right to break this Lease on 24 June 2027, provided that the tenant gives at least six months' written notice to the lessor.

10. RENT REVIEW PROPOSAL

On 1 May 2026, the lessor proposed a rent increase to GBP 128,000 per annum. The tenant's surveyor, in a note dated 3 June 2026, provided comparable evidence indicating a range of GBP 108,000 to GBP 112,000 per annum.

11. PROPOSED NEW LEASE

In light of the above, the parties have agreed to draft heads of terms for a new 10-year lease. The proposed rent under the new lease is GBP 110,000 per annum, with a rent-free period of 6 months.

12. MISCELLANEOUS

This Lease shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any dispute arising under this Lease shall be resolved in the courts of England and Wales.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease on the day and year first above written.
