Dear Sir/Madam,  

This letter serves as formal notice of the rent review proposal by Pembroke Estates Ltd, as per the terms of the lease dated 24 June 2016, for Unit 12, Riverside Works.  

Pursuant to Clause 4 of the lease, the rent review is to be conducted on 24 June 2026, which marks the first opportunity for an upward-only open-market rent review. In accordance with the lease terms, we have conducted a thorough assessment of the current market conditions and have determined that the appropriate rent for Unit 12, Riverside Works, should be GBP 128,000 per annum, effective from 24 June 2026.  

This proposed rent is based on a comprehensive analysis of the open market, including comparable lettings and current rental trends in the area. We have taken into account the value of the property, the condition of the premises, and the current demand for similar units in the locality. The proposed rent of GBP 128,000 reflects the current market rate and is in line with the upward-only nature of the rent review clause.  

We note that the tenant’s surveyor has provided a report dated 3 June 2026, which suggests a range of GBP 108,000 to GBP 112,000. However, we remain firm in our assessment that the market value of the property warrants a rent of GBP 128,000 per annum. We trust that this figure is both reasonable and reflective of the current market conditions.  

As the lease requires, we are now inviting the tenant to consider this proposal and to provide a response. We are prepared to engage in further discussions should the tenant wish to negotiate the terms of the rent review. However, we wish to make it clear that this proposal is final and is based on the current market conditions as of 1 May 2026.  

In addition, we wish to remind the tenant of their obligations under Clause 8 of the lease, which requires them to maintain the premises in good repair and to ensure that all insurance obligations are met. We trust that these obligations have been fulfilled and that the property remains in a condition suitable for the proposed rent.  

Furthermore, we note that the tenant has the right to serve notice to exit the lease under Clause 9, which allows for a break option on 24 June 2027, provided that at least six months’ written notice is given. However, we would encourage the tenant to consider the long-term benefits of continuing the lease at the proposed rent.  

We are prepared to discuss the proposal further and to explore any potential arrangements that may be of mutual benefit. However, we wish to reiterate that the proposed rent of GBP 128,000 is the figure we are prepared to accept and that we expect a formal response from the tenant within a reasonable timeframe.  

Should the tenant wish to propose an alternative rent, we would be happy to consider such a proposal, provided that it is supported by sufficient evidence and is in line with the open-market review process.  

We look forward to your prompt response.
