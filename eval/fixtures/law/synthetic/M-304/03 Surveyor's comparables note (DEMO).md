Surveyor's comparables note (DEMO)  
3 June 2026  

This note is prepared by [Tenant's Surveyor Name] for the purpose of providing comparative evidence in relation to the proposed rent review of the lease of Unit 12, Riverside Works, under the lease dated 24 June 2016 between Pembroke Estates Ltd and Harbourview Studios Ltd. The note is dated 3 June 2026 and is intended to support the tenant’s position that the rent should be set within the range of GBP 108,000 to GBP 112,000 per annum.  

The following four comparable lettings have been reviewed and are considered to be relevant to the valuation of Unit 12, Riverside Works, for the purposes of the rent review. These comparables are selected based on their similarity in terms of location, size, condition, and use, and are assessed to be representative of the current market rates for similar premises in the area.  

1. Unit 14, Riverside Works – Let on 15 March 2026 for GBP 110,000 per annum. This unit is of similar size and condition to Unit 12 and is located within the same industrial complex. The tenant has a long lease term and the rent reflects the current market rate for the area.  

2. Unit 22, Riverside Works – Let on 12 April 2026 for GBP 108,000 per annum. This unit is a smaller space than Unit 12 but is in the same building and has similar access and infrastructure. The rent is considered to be in line with the current market for smaller units in the area.  

3. Unit 5, Riverside Works – Let on 21 May 2026 for GBP 112,000 per annum. This unit is of comparable size and condition to Unit 12 and is located in the same building. The rent is considered to reflect the current market rate for such units in the area.  

4. Unit 8, Riverside Works – Let on 30 May 2026 for GBP 110,000 per annum. This unit is of similar size and condition to Unit 12 and is located in the same complex. The rent is consistent with the current market for similar premises.  

These four comparable lettings indicate that the current market rate for similar units in the Riverside Works complex is between GBP 108,000 and GBP 112,000 per annum. The lowest of the four comparables is GBP 108,000, and the highest is GBP 112,000. The average of the four is GBP 110,000 per annum.  

The tenant’s surveyor considers that the proposed rent of GBP 128,000 per annum, as set out in the rent review proposal dated 1 May 2026, is significantly higher than the current market rate for similar units. The surveyor believes that the rent should be set within the range of GBP 108,000 to GBP 112,000 per annum, based on the evidence provided by the four comparable lettings.  

The surveyor has also considered the potential for a rent-free period of six months as proposed in the heads of terms for a new 10-year lease. While the surveyor acknowledges that such a concession may be a factor in the negotiation, the surveyor’s view remains that the rent should be based on the current market rate, not on the terms of a potential new lease.  

In conclusion, the surveyor’s assessment is that the market rent for Unit 12, Riverside Works, as of 3 June 2026, is within the range of GBP 108,000 to GBP 112,000 per annum. This is supported by the four comparable lettings reviewed. The surveyor recommends that the rent be set at a figure within this range, and that the proposed rent of GBP 128,000 per annum be revised accordingly.
