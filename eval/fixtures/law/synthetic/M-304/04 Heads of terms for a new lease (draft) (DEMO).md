Heads of terms for a new lease (draft) (DEMO)

1. This document sets out the proposed terms for a new 10-year lease of Unit 12, Riverside Works, which is currently occupied by Harbourview Studios Ltd under a lease dated 24 June 2016. The proposed new lease is subject to the approval of all parties and shall be formalised in a lease agreement to be drawn up in accordance with the terms set out herein.

2. The term of the proposed new lease shall be for a period of 10 years, commencing on 24 June 2027, and shall be subject to the tenant's right to break the lease under the terms of the existing lease, provided that the tenant gives at least six months’ written notice prior to the proposed break date.

3. The rent for the proposed new lease shall be GBP 110,000 per annum, payable in quarterly instalments. The rent shall be reviewed in accordance with the provisions of the existing lease, with an upward-only open-market rent review scheduled for 24 June 2026, as previously proposed by Pembroke Estates Ltd.

4. The tenant shall be entitled to a 6-month rent-free period at the commencement of the proposed new lease. This rent-free period shall be applied to the first six months of the lease term, commencing on 24 June 2027.

5. The tenant shall be subject to the same full repairing and insuring obligations as set out in Clause 8 of the existing lease. These obligations shall remain in effect for the entire term of the proposed new lease, unless otherwise agreed in writing by both parties.

6. The proposed new lease shall incorporate the tenant’s right to break the lease on 24 June 2027, provided that the tenant gives at least six months’ written notice prior to that date. This break clause shall be subject to the terms and conditions set out in Clause 9 of the existing lease.

7. The rent review mechanism under the existing lease, which provides for an upward-only open-market rent review on 24 June 2026, shall remain in effect for the proposed new lease. The rent review shall be conducted in accordance with the procedures set out in the existing lease, and the proposed rent of GBP 110,000 per annum shall be the basis for the review.

8. The tenant’s surveyor has provided a report dated 3 June 2026, which includes comparable evidence suggesting a rent range of GBP 108,000 to GBP 112,000 per annum. The proposed rent of GBP 110,000 per annum is within this range and is considered to be commercially reasonable and reflective of the current market conditions.

9. The proposed new lease shall be subject to all the terms and conditions of the existing lease, including but not limited to the tenant’s obligations under Clause 8, the break clause under Clause 9, and the rent review provisions under Clause 4. Any variations to these terms shall be subject to mutual agreement between the parties.

10. The proposed new lease shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales, and any disputes arising under this lease shall be resolved in accordance with the applicable laws and procedures of England and Wales.

11. The proposed new lease shall be subject to the approval of both parties and shall be formalised in a lease agreement to be drawn up in accordance with the terms set out in this document. The parties agree to negotiate in good faith and to proceed with the formalisation of the lease as soon as practicable.

12. The parties acknowledge that this document is a draft and is not a legally binding agreement. The terms set out in this document are subject to change upon further negotiation and agreement between the parties.

13. This document is dated 3 June 2026 and is issued for the purpose of facilitating discussions between the parties regarding the proposed new lease.
