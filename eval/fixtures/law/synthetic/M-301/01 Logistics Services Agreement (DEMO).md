Logistics Services Agreement (DEMO)  
This Logistics Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 3 February 2025 by and between Northwind Logistics Ltd, with its principal office at 123 Business Street, London, England (the "Customer"), and Calder Freight Ltd, with its principal office at 456 Transport Way, Manchester, England (the "Carrier").  

1. **Scope of Services**  
The Carrier agrees to provide logistics services to the Customer, including the delivery of goods from the Port of Felixstowe to the Customer’s three warehouses.  

2. **Term**  
This Agreement shall commence on 3 February 2025 and continue for a period of three (3) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms hereof.  

3. **Payment Terms**  
The Customer shall pay the Carrier a monthly fee of GBP 140,000 for the services rendered under this Agreement. Payment is due within 45 days of the invoice date.  

4. **Performance Standards**  
The Carrier shall maintain an on-time delivery key performance indicator (KPI) of 98% per calendar month.  

5. **Service Credits**  
In the event the Carrier fails to meet the on-time delivery KPI, the Customer shall be entitled to service credits calculated as 5% of the monthly fee for each full percentage point below the 98% KPI, up to a maximum of 25% of the monthly fee.  

6. **Limitation of Liability**  
The Carrier’s total liability in any contract year shall not exceed GBP 400,000. The Carrier shall not be liable for any loss of profit, including but not limited to lost sales or revenue.  

7. **Notice of Claim**  
The Customer shall notify the Carrier of any claim within 30 days after the end of the month in which the failure occurred.  

8. **Force Majeure**  
This Agreement includes force majeure provisions covering the closure of the Port of Felixstowe. However, the Carrier shall not be excused from liability for delays caused by congestion or labour shortages at the Carrier’s subcontractors.  

9. **Governing Law**  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.  

10. **Dispute Resolution**  
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be resolved through negotiation between the parties for a period of 20 business days. If no resolution is reached, the dispute shall be referred to the High Court of England and Wales.  

11. **Payment Schedule**  
The Carrier shall be paid GBP 140,000 per month for the services rendered.  

12. **Force Majeure**  
The Carrier shall not be liable for delays caused by force majeure events, including but not limited to port closures.  

13. **Performance Monitoring**  
The Customer shall monitor the Carrier’s performance on a monthly basis and ensure compliance with the KPI of 98%.  

14. **Liability Cap**  
The Carrier’s liability for any claim arising under this Agreement shall not exceed GBP 400,000 in any contract year.  

15. **Exclusion of Loss of Profit**  
The Carrier shall not be liable for any loss of profit, including but not limited to lost sales or revenue.  

16. **Notice Period**  
The Customer must notify the Carrier of any claim within 30 days of the end of the month in which the failure occurred.  

17. **Force Majeure Definition**  
Force majeure shall include, but not be limited to, the closure of the Port of Felixstowe.  

18. **Payment Terms**  
The Customer shall pay the Carrier within 45 days of the invoice date.  

19. **Amendments**  
This Agreement may be amended only in writing and signed by both parties.  

20. **Entire Agreement**  
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements or understandings.
