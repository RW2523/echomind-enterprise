Notice of claim (DEMO)  
Northwind Logistics Ltd  
28 July 2026  

To: Calder Freight Ltd  
Attention: Legal Department  

This Notice of Claim is issued by Northwind Logistics Ltd (the Customer) under Clause 12.1 of the Logistics Services Agreement dated 3 February 2025, between the Customer and Calder Freight Ltd (the Carrier). This notice is submitted in accordance with the terms and conditions of the Agreement and is intended to formally assert the Customer’s rights and remedies arising from the Carrier’s failure to meet the agreed Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for the months of April, May, and June 2026.  

On 28 July 2026, the Customer hereby notifies the Carrier that the KPI of 98% on-time delivery was not met for the months of April, May, and June 2026. The actual on-time delivery percentages for these months were 91.5%, 93%, and 95%, respectively. These results fall significantly below the agreed KPI of 98%, and the Customer is entitled to service credits under Clause 6.3 of the Agreement.  

The Customer calculates the service credits as follows: for each full percentage point below the KPI, the Carrier is liable for 5% of the monthly fee. The monthly fee under the Agreement is GBP 140,000. For April 2026, the KPI was 98%, and the actual delivery was 91.5%, a shortfall of 6.5 percentage points. For May 2026, the shortfall was 5 percentage points, and for June 2026, the shortfall was 3 percentage points. The total shortfall across the three months is 14.5 percentage points.  

Under Clause 6.3, the service credits are calculated as 5% of the monthly fee for each full percentage point below the KPI, with a maximum cap of 25% of the monthly fee. The calculation for each month is as follows:  

- April 2026: 6 full percentage points below KPI × 5% × GBP 140,000 = GBP 42,000  
- May 2026: 5 full percentage points below KPI × 5% × GBP 140,000 = GBP 35,000  
- June 2026: 3 full percentage points below KPI × 5% × GBP 140,000 = GBP 21,000  

Total service credits: GBP 42,000 + GBP 35,000 + GBP 21,000 = GBP 98,000.  

However, the Customer has elected to claim GBP 112,000 in service credits, which represents 8 full percentage points below the KPI across the three months. This calculation is based on the total shortfall of 14.5 percentage points, with the Customer electing to apply the service credit rate for 8 full percentage points.  

In addition to the service credits, the Customer asserts a claim for lost sales of GBP 260,000, arising from the failure to meet the KPI. This loss is attributed to the delay in delivery, which caused Northwind Logistics Ltd to lose sales opportunities with its customers. The Customer submits that this loss is directly attributable to the Carrier’s failure to meet the KPI and is not excluded under the terms of the Agreement.  

The Customer further asserts that the Carrier’s reliance on force majeure is not applicable in this case. Clause 14 of the Agreement specifically excludes congestion and labour shortages at the Carrier’s subcontractors from the definition of force majeure. The Customer maintains that the delays in delivery were not due to any event beyond the Carrier’s control and that the Carrier is fully liable for the failure to meet the KPI.  

The Customer also disputes the Carrier’s claim that the notice of claim was submitted late. Clause 12.1 of the Agreement requires the Customer to notify any claim within 30 days after the end of the month in which the failure occurred. The Customer submitted this notice on 28 July 2026, which is within the required 30-day period following the end of June 2026.  

The Customer hereby demands that the Carrier pay the service credits of GBP 112,000 and the lost sales of GBP 260,000 within 45 days of the date of this notice. If the Carrier fails to respond or pay in full, the Customer reserves the right to pursue all available remedies under the Agreement and applicable law.
