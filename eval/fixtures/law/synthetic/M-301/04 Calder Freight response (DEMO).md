Dear Sir/Madam,

We refer to your letter dated 28 July 2026, in which you have claimed service credits and compensation for lost sales arising from alleged failures in the delivery of goods under the Logistics Services Agreement dated 3 February 2025 between Northwind Logistics Ltd (the Customer) and Calder Freight Ltd (the Carrier).

We regret to inform you that we are unable to accept your claim. We have carefully reviewed the relevant clauses of the agreement and find that your claim is not supported by the terms of the contract.

Firstly, the alleged failures in on-time delivery in April, May and June 2026 were caused by port congestion at Felixstowe, which we have determined to be a force majeure event under clause 14 of the agreement. Clause 14 explicitly includes port closure as a force majeure event, and while congestion and labour shortages at the Carrier’s subcontractors are not covered, the port closure itself is. The congestion at the port of Felixstowe, which occurred during the relevant months, is a force majeure event within the meaning of the agreement. As such, the Carrier is not liable for any delays or failures in delivery during this period.

Secondly, your claim for lost sales of GBP 260,000 is excluded under clause 11.4 of the agreement, which states that the Carrier shall not be liable for any loss of profit. This clause clearly and unambiguously excludes any claim for lost profits, including lost sales. Therefore, your claim for GBP 260,000 is not recoverable under the terms of the agreement.

Thirdly, we note that your notice of claim for the April and May 2026 failures was submitted on 28 July 2026, which is beyond the 30-day notice period specified in clause 12.1. Clause 12.1 requires that any claim must be notified within 30 days after the end of the month in which the failure occurred. The failure in April 2026 occurred in April, and the 30-day notice period would have expired on 30 May 2026. Similarly, the failure in May 2026 would have required notice by 30 June 2026. Your notice of 28 July 2026 is therefore outside the required timeframe and is not valid under the agreement.

We further note that the service credits you have claimed amount to GBP 112,000, which is based on the calculation of 5% of the monthly fee for each full percentage point below the 98% KPI. However, as the failures in April, May and June 2026 were caused by a force majeure event, the service credits are not applicable for those months. Furthermore, the total liability of the Carrier under clause 11.2 is capped at GBP 400,000 per contract year. Even if we were to consider the service credits for the months not covered by force majeure, the total liability would not exceed the cap.

We are prepared to negotiate in good faith and resolve any disputes in accordance with the terms of the agreement. However, we must reiterate that your claim is not supported by the contractual terms and is therefore not acceptable.
