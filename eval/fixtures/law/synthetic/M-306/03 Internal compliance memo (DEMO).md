Internal compliance memo (DEMO)  
6 October 2026  

To: Compliance Team  
From: Internal Compliance Department  
Subject: Precautionary Recall of Harvest Seeded Loaf Batches 4471 to 4479  

This memo is issued to inform the Compliance Team of the recommendation to initiate a precautionary recall of Harvest Seeded Loaf batches 4471 to 4479. This action is being proposed in light of a recent consumer report of an allergic reaction to sesame, which is not listed on the product label. Additionally, the Food Labelling Authority has requested specific documentation related to this product, including the product specification, supplier allergen declarations, labelling approvals, and complaint records for the past 12 months.  

The product specification for the Harvest Seeded Loaf, version 5 dated 12 March 2026, indicates that sesame may be present as a trace allergen due to shared bakery equipment. However, the printed label does not include this information. This discrepancy raises concerns about the accuracy of the product's allergen information and the potential risk to consumers with sesame allergies.  

On 6 October 2026, the Compliance Team reviewed the product's history and identified three prior complaints related to the Harvest Seeded Loaf. These complaints, although not linked to sesame, suggest that the product may not meet the expected standards of consumer safety and transparency. The current situation, combined with the recent report of a sesame-related allergic reaction, warrants immediate action.  

The Food Labelling Authority has requested that Meridian Foods plc provide the following documentation within 21 days of the date of this letter:  
- Product specification for the Harvest Seeded Loaf  
- Supplier's allergen declaration  
- Labelling approvals for the product  
- Complaint records for the last 12 months  

Given the urgency of the Authority’s request and the potential risk to public health, the Compliance Team recommends the following course of action:  

1. A precautionary recall of all batches of the Harvest Seeded Loaf produced between 1 January 2026 and 30 September 2026, specifically batches 4471 to 4479.  
2. Immediate notification to all relevant stakeholders, including retailers, distributors, and consumers, regarding the recall.  
3. A thorough review of the product's labelling and allergen control measures, including an audit of the bakery line to determine the source of the sesame contamination.  
4. Submission of the requested documentation to the Food Labelling Authority by 22 October 2026.  

The Compliance Team further recommends that the company conduct an internal investigation into the cause of the discrepancy between the product specification and the printed label. This investigation should include a review of the production and quality control processes for the Harvest Seeded Loaf.  

The three prior complaints related to the Harvest Seeded Loaf, while not directly linked to sesame, highlight the need for a comprehensive review of the product's safety and labelling practices. The Compliance Team is also recommending that the company consider implementing additional allergen control measures to prevent similar issues in the future.  

In light of the above, the Compliance Team strongly advises that the precautionary recall of Harvest Seeded Loaf batches 4471 to 4479 be initiated immediately. This action is necessary to protect consumer health, maintain the company's reputation, and comply with the requirements of the Food Labelling Authority.  

This memo is intended for internal use only and is not to be shared with external parties without prior approval.  

Sincerely,  
Internal Compliance Department  
Meridian Foods plc
