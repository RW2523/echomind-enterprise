Re: Request for Information and Corrective Action Regarding Harvest Seeded Loaf  

Dear Sir/Madam,  

Meridian Foods plc acknowledges receipt of your letter dated 1 October 2026, regarding the Harvest Seeded Loaf and the consumer report of a reaction to sesame. We take this matter very seriously and are fully cooperating with your investigation.  

We confirm that we have received your request for the following information within 21 days:  

1. The product specification for the Harvest Seeded Loaf.  
2. The supplier’s allergen declaration.  
3. Labelling approvals for the product.  
4. Complaint records for the last 12 months.  

We are pleased to confirm that we have compiled and will promptly forward the requested documents to your office. These materials will be sent by 21 days from the date of your original request, as required.  

The product specification for the Harvest Seeded Loaf (version 5, 12 March 2026) does include a note that the product may contain sesame due to shared bakery equipment. However, the printed label omits this information, which we acknowledge is a serious oversight. We are currently reviewing the labelling process to determine how this omission occurred.  

We have also reviewed the internal compliance memo dated 6 October 2026, which recommends a precautionary recall of batches 4471 to 4479. We agree that this step is necessary and have initiated the recall process immediately. We have informed our distribution partners and are working to ensure that all affected products are removed from the market as quickly as possible.  

In addition, the memo notes three earlier complaints related to the Harvest Seeded Loaf. We have reviewed these complaints and are conducting a full internal investigation into the root cause of the issues. We have also contacted the individuals involved to gather further details and ensure that their concerns are fully addressed.  

As part of our corrective action plan, we are implementing the following measures:  

- A complete review of all allergen-related labelling protocols.  
- Additional training for our labelling and quality assurance teams.  
- A new internal audit process to ensure compliance with all labelling regulations.  
- A revised supplier verification process to confirm that all allergen declarations are accurate and up to date.  

We are committed to ensuring that such errors do not occur in the future and are taking all necessary steps to prevent recurrence.  

We understand the importance of accurate food labelling and the potential risks to consumer health. We appreciate the opportunity to address this matter and will keep you fully informed of our progress.  

Please do not hesitate to contact us if you require any further information or clarification.
