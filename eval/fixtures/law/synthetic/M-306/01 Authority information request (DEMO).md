Subject: Request for Information Regarding Harvest Seeded Loaf

Dear Sir/Madam,

We are writing to you in relation to the Harvest Seeded Loaf produced by your company, Meridian Foods plc. On 1 October 2026, the Food Labelling Authority received a consumer report indicating an adverse reaction to sesame in this product. The label on the Harvest Seeded Loaf does not list sesame as an allergen, despite the product specification indicating that sesame may be present as a trace allergen from the bakery line.

In order to assess the compliance of your product with current labelling regulations, we are formally requesting the following information within 21 days of the date of this letter:

1. The most recent version of the product specification for the Harvest Seeded Loaf, including all relevant details regarding ingredients, processing methods, and potential allergen presence.  
2. The supplier's allergen declaration for the ingredients used in the Harvest Seeded Loaf, including any documentation that confirms the presence or absence of sesame in the raw materials.  
3. All labelling approvals for the Harvest Seeded Loaf, including any internal reviews, external audits, or regulatory clearances related to the product's packaging and label.  
4. All complaint records related to the Harvest Seeded Loaf for the past 12 months, including details of the consumer report received on 1 October 2026 and any other complaints received during that period.  

We note that the product specification for the Harvest Seeded Loaf, version 5 dated 12 March 2026, indicates that sesame may be present as a trace allergen due to shared production equipment. However, the printed label does not include sesame as an allergen, which may constitute a breach of labelling requirements under Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011.

Furthermore, we have received an internal compliance memo dated 6 October 2026 from Meridian Foods plc, which recommends a precautionary recall of batches 4471 to 4479 of the Harvest Seeded Loaf. The memo also notes three previous complaints related to this product, which we are not currently aware of. We request that you confirm whether these complaints have been properly documented and investigated.

Please ensure that all requested information is provided in a clear, organised format, and that it is accompanied by any supporting documentation. If you require further clarification regarding this request, please contact the Food Labelling Authority at the address above.

This request must be fulfilled within 21 days of the date of this letter. Failure to comply may result in further regulatory action, including but not limited to formal enforcement proceedings.
