Product specification v5 — Harvest Seeded Loaf (DEMO)

1. Product Name: Harvest Seeded Loaf  
2. Product Code: HLS-2026-05  
3. Manufacturer: Meridian Foods plc  
4. Date of Specification: 12 March 2026  
5. Version: 5  

6. Description:  
The Harvest Seeded Loaf is a premium seeded bread product designed for consumers seeking a wholesome, nutritious option. The loaf contains a blend of seeds, including sunflower, flax, and sesame, and is marketed as a healthy, all-natural product. It is available in 400g and 800g sizes and is distributed across major retail chains and online platforms.

7. Ingredients:  
- Wheat flour  
- Water  
- Yeast  
- Salt  
- Sunflower seeds  
- Flax seeds  
- Sesame seeds  
- Vegetable oil  
- Malt extract  

8. Allergen Information:  
The product contains wheat, sunflower seeds, flax seeds, and sesame seeds. The specification includes a warning that the product may contain traces of sesame due to shared production lines. This statement is included in the specification as a precautionary measure to inform consumers of potential cross-contamination risks.

9. Label Copy:  
The label of the Harvest Seeded Loaf includes the following text:  
"Contains: Wheat, Sunflower Seeds, Flax Seeds. May contain traces of Sesame."  

10. Allergen Statement:  
The specification explicitly states that the product may contain traces of sesame, as per the Food Standards Agency guidelines. This information is included to ensure transparency and consumer safety. The statement is based on the assessment of the production environment, which includes shared equipment and facilities where sesame-containing products are also processed.

11. Production and Quality Assurance:  
The Harvest Seeded Loaf is produced at Meridian Foods plc’s facility in Manchester. The production process involves multiple stages, including mixing, proofing, baking, and packaging. Quality assurance protocols are in place to monitor and manage allergen risks. However, the specification does not include a detailed allergen control plan, and no specific measures are outlined for preventing cross-contamination with sesame.

12. Labelling Approval:  
The label for the Harvest Seeded Loaf was approved by Meridian Foods plc’s internal quality assurance team on 12 March 2026. The approval process did not include a review of the allergen statement by the Food Labelling Authority. The label was printed and distributed without further external review.

13. Complaint Records:  
Meridian Foods plc has received three complaints related to the Harvest Seeded Loaf in the last 12 months. The complaints were submitted by consumers who reported allergic reactions after consuming the product. The complaints were logged in the company’s internal system, but no immediate action was taken beyond standard customer service responses.

14. Internal Compliance Memo:  
On 6 October 2026, an internal compliance memo was issued by the Quality Assurance Department. The memo noted that the Harvest Seeded Loaf label omits the sesame allergen warning, despite the product specification stating that the loaf may contain traces of sesame. The memo recommended a precautionary recall of batches 4471 to 4479, citing the potential risk to consumers with sesame allergies.

15. Conclusion:  
The product specification v5 of 12 March 2026 for the Harvest Seeded Loaf includes the allergen statement regarding sesame. However, the label omits this information, which may expose consumers to unnecessary health risks. The discrepancy between the product specification and the label raises concerns about compliance with food labelling regulations. The internal compliance memo of 6 October 2026 highlights the need for immediate corrective action to address this issue.
