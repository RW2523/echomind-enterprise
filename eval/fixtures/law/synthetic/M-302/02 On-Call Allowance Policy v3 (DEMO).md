On-Call Allowance Policy v3 (DEMO)

1. This On-Call Allowance Policy v3 (DEMO) is effective from 1 January 2024 and applies to all employees of Brightwater Clinics Ltd who are required to perform on-call duties. This policy outlines the terms and conditions of on-call allowance, including the rates of payment, procedures for submitting claims, and mechanisms for resolving disputes.

2. On-call duties are defined as any period during which an employee is available to respond to emergencies or urgent clinical needs outside of their regular working hours. Employees who are required to perform such duties shall be compensated in accordance with this policy.

3. The on-call allowance shall be calculated based on the number of nights and weekend days on which the employee is on call. The following rates shall apply:

   a. GBP 220 for each night on call, provided the employee is available for at least 12 hours and is prepared to respond to emergencies during that time.

   b. GBP 90 for each weekend day on call, provided the employee is available for at least 12 hours and is prepared to respond to emergencies during that time.

4. Employees who are on call shall submit a claim for on-call allowance by the 10th of each month. Claims must be submitted using the designated online portal and must include the following information:

   a. The employee’s full name and employee ID.

   b. The dates of the on-call periods.

   c. The number of nights and weekend days on call.

   d. Any additional notes or explanations, if applicable.

5. Claims submitted after the 10th of the month will be considered late and may be subject to review and approval by the HR department. Late claims will not be processed unless there are exceptional circumstances, as determined by the HR manager.

6. The HR department shall review all claims and confirm the number of nights and weekend days on call. The HR department may request additional documentation or clarification if the claim is incomplete or unclear.

7. The on-call allowance shall be paid in the month following the on-call period. Payments shall be made via direct deposit to the employee’s bank account on the 25th of each month.

8. Disputes regarding the calculation or payment of on-call allowance shall be resolved through the internal grievance procedure of Brightwater Clinics Ltd. Employees who wish to dispute a decision regarding their on-call allowance must submit a written grievance to the HR department within 14 days of receiving the payment or notice of non-payment.

9. The HR department shall investigate all grievances and provide a written response within 14 days of receiving the grievance. If the employee is dissatisfied with the HR department’s response, they may escalate the matter to the Chief Operations Officer (COO) for further review.

10. The COO shall review the grievance and provide a final decision within 14 days of receiving the escalation. This decision shall be binding and final.

11. This policy is subject to change at any time by Brightwater Clinics Ltd. Any changes to this policy shall be communicated to all affected employees in writing, with the effective date clearly stated.

12. This policy is effective from 1 January 2024 and supersedes all previous versions of the On-Call Allowance Policy.

13. Employees are required to familiarize themselves with this policy and to follow its procedures when submitting on-call allowance claims. Failure to comply with this policy may result in delays or denial of payment.

14. Brightwater Clinics Ltd reserves the right to audit on-call allowance claims at any time. Employees may be required to provide additional documentation or evidence to support their claims.

15. This policy is intended to ensure fair and consistent treatment of all employees who perform on-call duties. It is not intended to create or imply any contractual obligations beyond those set forth in the employment contract.

16. This policy is not intended to limit or override any other terms or conditions of employment, including but not limited to the terms of the employment contract, the notice period, or any restrictive covenants.

17. The terms of this policy shall be interpreted in accordance with the laws of the United Kingdom.

18. This policy is issued by Brightwater Clinics Ltd and is subject to the internal policies and procedures of the company.

19. The HR department is the responsible party for the administration and enforcement of this policy.

20. This policy is a demonstration document and is not intended for use in any legal proceedings or as a binding agreement.
