HR investigation notes (DEMO)

1. This document records the findings of the HR investigation into the grievance raised by Dr Amir Rahman, a consultant radiologist employed by Brightwater Clinics Ltd under a contract dated 1 September 2022.

2. The grievance concerns two matters: (a) an allegation of unpaid on-call allowance of GBP 18,400 for the year 2025, and (b) a claim that a rota change was implemented in March 2026 without proper consultation.

3. The employment contract between Brightwater Clinics Ltd and Dr Rahman includes the following relevant clauses:
   - Clause 4: Annual salary of GBP 118,000.
   - Clause 7: On-call duties are to be compensated under the On-Call Allowance Policy.
   - Clause 12: Either party must provide three months’ notice of termination.
   - Clause 15: A six-month non-compete restriction applies if Dr Rahman works for a competing clinic within 10 miles of Brightwater Clinics Ltd.

4. The On-Call Allowance Policy (version 3, effective 1 January 2024) specifies the following rates:
   - GBP 220 for each night on call.
   - GBP 90 for each weekend day on call.

5. The grievance raised by Dr Rahman claims that he was owed GBP 18,400 in unpaid on-call allowance for the year 2025. The HR investigation reviewed the records of on-call shifts and the applicable allowance rates.

6. The investigation found that Dr Rahman had 64 instances of on-call duty in 2025. Based on the policy rate of GBP 220 per night, the total amount due for these shifts is GBP 14,080.

7. The discrepancy between the claimed GBP 18,400 and the calculated GBP 14,080 arises from an overestimation of the number of on-call shifts. The HR investigation confirmed that only 64 nights were recorded as on-call shifts for the year 2025, and no additional shifts were found in the records.

8. The second part of the grievance concerns a rota change implemented in March 2026. Dr Rahman alleged that the change was made without consultation. The HR investigation reviewed internal communications and found that the rota change was communicated via an email sent to all staff on 2 March 2026.

9. The email dated 2 March 2026 informed staff of the updated rota, including changes to on-call schedules. The email was sent to all employees and included a summary of the changes. There is no evidence that Dr Rahman was excluded from the communication or that the change was made without proper consultation.

10. The HR investigation has concluded that the rota change was properly communicated and that the process followed the company’s standard procedures for updating schedules. No further action is required in this regard.

11. The calculation of GBP 14,080 for 64 nights of on-call duty is based on the policy rate of GBP 220 per night, as outlined in the On-Call Allowance Policy (version 3, 1 January 2024). This amount has been verified against the records maintained by the HR department.

12. The HR investigation notes that the grievance regarding the unpaid on-call allowance was based on an incorrect calculation of the number of on-call shifts. The correct amount due is GBP 14,080, not GBP 18,400.

13. The HR department has advised Brightwater Clinics Ltd that the issue of unpaid on-call allowance should be resolved by paying the correct amount of GBP 14,080 to Dr Rahman. The company is also advised to ensure that all future on-call allowance calculations are accurately recorded and communicated.

14. The HR investigation further notes that the rota change on 2 March 2026 was properly documented and communicated to all relevant staff. There is no evidence of procedural irregularity in this matter.

15. This investigation was conducted in accordance with the company’s internal procedures for handling employee grievances. The findings are presented in good faith and are based on the available evidence.
