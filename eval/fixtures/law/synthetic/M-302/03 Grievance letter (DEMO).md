Subject: Formal Grievance Regarding Unpaid On-Call Allowance and Rota Change  

Dear Sir/Madam,  

I am writing to formally raise a grievance in relation to my employment with Brightwater Clinics Ltd. This letter is submitted in accordance with the company’s grievance procedure and is dated 19 June 2026.  

Firstly, I wish to bring to your attention the issue of unpaid on-call allowance for the year 2025. According to the On-Call Allowance Policy (version 3, 1 January 2024), I am entitled to GBP 220 for each night on call and GBP 90 for each weekend day. During the year 2025, I was required to perform on-call duties on a total of 83 nights and 12 weekend days, as recorded in the clinic’s rota system. This equates to a total of GBP 18,400 in unpaid on-call allowance.  

The discrepancy between the amount I have calculated and the amount I have been paid is significant. The HR investigation notes, which were shared with me on 19 June 2026, state that there were 64 unpaid nights, which would amount to GBP 14,080. However, this figure does not reflect the actual number of nights I was on call during 2025. I have reviewed the rota records and can confirm that I was on call for 83 nights, not 64. The difference of 19 nights amounts to GBP 4,180, which, when added to the weekend days, results in a total of GBP 18,400.  

I have raised this matter with the HR department on multiple occasions, but no resolution has been reached. I believe that the On-Call Allowance Policy is clear in its terms, and I am entitled to the full amount of GBP 18,400 for the unpaid on-call allowance for 2025.  

Secondly, I wish to formally object to the rota change implemented in March 2026. The change was communicated to staff via email on 2 March 2026, and I was not consulted prior to its implementation. This is a breach of the company’s internal procedures, which stipulate that any changes to the rota must be discussed with affected staff members beforehand.  

The rota change significantly impacted my working schedule and caused considerable inconvenience. I was not given the opportunity to provide feedback or raise concerns about the change before it was implemented. This lack of consultation is not in line with the terms of my employment contract, which is dated 1 September 2022, and which includes a clause regarding consultation on matters affecting my work.  

I have been a loyal and dedicated employee of Brightwater Clinics Ltd and have always adhered to the terms of my contract. I believe that my concerns are valid and should be addressed in a timely and appropriate manner.  

In light of the above, I request that Brightwater Clinics Ltd investigate this matter thoroughly and provide me with a written response within 14 days of the date of this letter. I also request that the unpaid on-call allowance of GBP 18,400 be paid in full, and that the rota change be reviewed in accordance with the company’s internal procedures.  

I trust that this matter will be resolved promptly and amicably. Should I not receive a satisfactory response, I may have no choice but to pursue further action through the appropriate channels.
