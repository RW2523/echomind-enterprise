Employment contract — Dr A. Rahman (DEMO)  

This Employment Contract is made on this 1 September 2022 by and between Brightwater Clinics Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales, with its registered office at 123 Health Lane, Brightwater, and Dr Amir Rahman, a consultant radiologist, residing at 456 Radiology Drive, Brightwater.  

1. This Contract is effective from 1 September 2022 and shall continue in force unless terminated in accordance with the terms herein.  
2. The employment of Dr Amir Rahman shall be on a full-time basis, subject to the terms and conditions of this Contract.  
3. Dr Rahman shall perform the duties of a consultant radiologist as reasonably required by Brightwater Clinics Ltd, including but not limited to diagnostic imaging, patient consultations, and administrative responsibilities.  
4. Dr Rahman’s annual salary shall be GBP 118,000, payable in equal monthly instalments on the last working day of each month.  
5. Dr Rahman shall be entitled to all statutory and company benefits, including but not limited to annual leave, sick leave, and pension contributions.  
6. Dr Rahman shall be subject to the On-Call Allowance Policy, which provides for compensation for on-call duties.  
7. The On-Call Allowance Policy (version 3, 1 January 2024) shall apply to Dr Rahman’s on-call duties, with compensation of GBP 220 for each night on call and GBP 90 for each weekend day.  
8. Brightwater Clinics Ltd shall maintain a fair and transparent process for managing on-call rotas and shall consult with Dr Rahman before making any changes to his on-call schedule.  
9. Either party may terminate this Contract by giving three months’ written notice to the other.  
10. Dr Rahman shall not, during the period of his employment and for a six-month period thereafter, engage in any employment or consultancy with a competing clinic within 10 miles of any Brightwater Clinics Ltd premises.  
11. Dr Rahman shall not, during the period of his employment and for a six-month period thereafter, disclose any confidential information of Brightwater Clinics Ltd to any third party.  
12. Brightwater Clinics Ltd shall have the right to terminate this Contract immediately for cause, including but not limited to gross misconduct, breach of contract, or failure to perform duties.  
13. Any dispute arising under this Contract shall be resolved through the internal grievance procedure of Brightwater Clinics Ltd.  
14. Dr Rahman shall be entitled to a grievance procedure as outlined in the company’s internal policies.  
15. Breach of the non-compete clause shall entitle Brightwater Clinics Ltd to seek injunctive relief and/or damages.  
16. This Contract may be amended only by written agreement between the parties.  
17. Any amendments to the On-Call Allowance Policy shall be communicated to Dr Rahman in writing and shall not affect any entitlements accrued prior to the amendment.  
18. This Contract constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, or understandings, whether written or oral.
