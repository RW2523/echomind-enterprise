Subject: Proposed Amendments to SaaS Agreement with Lumen Software Ltd

Dear [Lumen Software Ltd. Legal Team],

I hope this message finds you well. As discussed, I am writing to formally communicate Orbis Bank plc’s proposed amendments to the draft SaaS Agreement dated 8 September 2026 for the provision of the Ledgerline platform by Lumen Software Ltd.

We have reviewed the terms of the draft agreement and have identified several key areas where we believe the agreement should be adjusted to better align with our operational and compliance requirements. These changes are essential to ensure that the service level, data security, and liability frameworks are in line with our internal policies and regulatory obligations.

Firstly, we request that the availability guarantee be increased from 99.9% to 99.95% on a monthly basis. This adjustment is critical to our operational continuity and customer satisfaction. We also propose that the service credits for downtime be increased to reflect this higher standard of service, with a maximum of 10% of the monthly fee applicable in the event of a breach of the 99.95% availability commitment.

Secondly, we would like to revise the data breach notification requirement from 72 hours to 24 hours, as outlined in the Data Processing Addendum. This change is necessary to ensure that we can respond to any incident in a timely manner and comply with applicable data protection laws.

In addition, we propose that the limitation of liability clause be amended to remove the cap on liability for data protection breaches. While we acknowledge the current cap of the fees paid in the previous 12 months, we believe that in the case of a data protection breach, the liability should be unlimited to reflect the potential severity of such events and to ensure that Lumen Software Ltd is fully accountable for any harm caused.

We also note that the security schedule requires annual penetration tests and ISO 27001 certification. We agree with these requirements and confirm that we will ensure that all necessary documentation and test results are provided in accordance with the schedule.

Please be advised that the above amendments are non-negotiable and must be incorporated into the final agreement. We remain open to discussing other terms, but these specific changes are essential for us to proceed with the agreement.

We kindly request that you provide your comments and any proposed adjustments by 22 September 2026. If we do not receive a response by this date, we will assume that you are in agreement with our proposed amendments and will proceed accordingly.

Should you have any questions or require further clarification on the above points, please do not hesitate to contact me directly.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.
