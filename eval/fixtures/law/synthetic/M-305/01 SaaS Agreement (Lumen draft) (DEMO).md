SaaS Agreement (Lumen draft) (DEMO)

This SaaS Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into on 8 September 2026 by and between Lumen Software Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales with its registered office at 123 Tech Street, London, United Kingdom (the "Provider"), and Orbis Bank plc, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales with its registered office at 456 Finance Road, Manchester, United Kingdom (the "Customer").

1. **Services**  
The Provider shall provide the Customer with access to the Ledgerline platform (the "Service") for the duration of this Agreement. The Service shall be delivered in accordance with the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement and the attached schedules.

2. **Fees**  
The Customer shall pay the Provider a subscription fee of GBP 360,000 per annum, payable in equal quarterly installments. All payments shall be made in British pounds sterling and in accordance with the payment terms set forth in this Agreement.

3. **Service Levels**  
The Provider shall maintain a monthly Service Availability of 99.9%. In the event the Service Availability falls below 99.9% during any month, the Provider shall issue a service credit of up to 10% of the monthly subscription fee. The service credit shall be applied to the Customer’s next invoice.

4. **Data Protection**  
The Provider shall comply with the Data Processing Addendum attached hereto as Schedule A. In the event of a data breach, the Provider shall notify the Customer within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. The Provider shall also maintain all data processing activities in accordance with the applicable data protection laws.

5. **Liability**  
The liability of each party under this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees paid by the Customer to the Provider in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This limitation shall not apply to claims arising from the Provider’s gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of the Data Processing Addendum.

6. **Term and Termination**  
This Agreement shall commence on 8 September 2026 and shall continue for a period of 12 months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms of this Agreement. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon 30 days' written notice to the other. Upon termination, the Customer shall pay any outstanding fees for services rendered up to the date of termination.

7. **Security Requirements**  
The Provider shall maintain the security of the Service in accordance with the security schedule attached hereto as Schedule B. The Provider shall conduct annual penetration tests and shall maintain ISO 27001 certification throughout the term of this Agreement.

8. **Governing Law**  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively in the courts of England and Wales.
