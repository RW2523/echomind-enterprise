Security schedule (DEMO)

1. This Security Schedule (the "Schedule") forms part of the SaaS Agreement dated 8 September 2026 between Lumen Software Ltd ("Lumen") and Orbis Bank plc ("Orbis"). This Schedule sets forth the security requirements and obligations of Lumen in relation to the provision of the Ledgerline platform to Orbis.

2. Lumen shall maintain ISO 27001 certification for its information security management system ("ISMS") and shall provide proof of such certification to Orbis upon request. Lumen shall ensure that its ISMS is reviewed and updated at least annually to reflect changes in its operations, technology, and regulatory environment.

3. Lumen shall conduct annual penetration testing of the Ledgerline platform. The penetration tests shall be performed by an independent third-party security firm with appropriate qualifications and shall be reported to Orbis in writing. The report shall include a detailed analysis of vulnerabilities identified, remediation steps taken, and any unresolved issues.

4. All data stored, processed, or transmitted by the Ledgerline platform shall be encrypted using industry-standard encryption protocols. Encryption shall be applied to both data at rest and data in transit. Lumen shall ensure that encryption keys are securely managed and stored, with access restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. Lumen shall implement and maintain robust access control mechanisms to ensure that only authorized personnel have access to the Ledgerline platform. Access shall be granted based on the principle of least privilege, and user access rights shall be reviewed and updated regularly. Lumen shall maintain audit logs of all access and activity on the platform for a minimum of 12 months.

6. In the event of a data breach or unauthorized access to data stored or processed by the Ledgerline platform, Lumen shall notify Orbis within 24 hours of becoming aware of the incident. The notification shall include details of the breach, the nature of the affected data, and the steps being taken to mitigate the risk. Lumen shall also provide Orbis with a detailed incident response plan and cooperate fully with any investigation conducted by Orbis or relevant regulatory authorities.

7. Lumen shall maintain an incident response plan that is reviewed and updated at least annually. The plan shall outline procedures for detecting, reporting, and responding to security incidents, including data breaches. Lumen shall conduct regular drills to test the effectiveness of the incident response plan and shall provide Orbis with a summary of the results of such drills.

8. Lumen shall ensure that all personnel with access to the Ledgerline platform are subject to a thorough background check and shall provide training on information security policies and procedures. Lumen shall maintain a policy of continuous security awareness training for all employees, including regular updates on emerging threats and best practices.

9. Lumen shall comply with all applicable data protection laws and regulations, including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK Data Protection Act 2018. Lumen shall implement technical and organizational measures to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the data processed by the Ledgerline platform.

10. Lumen shall maintain ISO 27001 certification for the duration of the SaaS Agreement and shall notify Orbis in writing of any changes to its certification status. If Lumen's ISO 27001 certification is revoked or suspended, Lumen shall immediately notify Orbis and take all necessary steps to restore compliance.

11. Lumen shall provide Orbis with a written statement confirming compliance with the terms of this Schedule upon request. The statement shall include details of Lumen's ISO 27001 certification, the results of the annual penetration tests, and any other relevant security measures implemented by Lumen.

12. This Schedule shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes arising under this Schedule shall be resolved in the courts of England and Wales.
