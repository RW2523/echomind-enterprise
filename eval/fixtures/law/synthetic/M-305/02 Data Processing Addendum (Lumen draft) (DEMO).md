Data Processing Addendum (Lumen draft) (DEMO)

1. This Data Processing Addendum (the "Addendum") is made and entered into as of 8 September 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Lumen Software Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales with its registered office at 123 Tech Street, London, England (the "Data Processor"), and Orbis Bank plc, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales with its registered office at 456 Finance Road, London, England (the "Data Controller").

2. The parties agree that this Addendum forms part of the SaaS Agreement dated 8 September 2026 between the parties (the "Agreement"), and shall be governed by the terms of the Agreement, except as modified by the terms of this Addendum.

3. The Data Processor shall process personal data on behalf of the Data Controller in accordance with the Agreement and this Addendum. The processing of personal data shall be carried out for the purpose of providing the services described in the Agreement, including but not limited to the operation and maintenance of the Ledgerline platform.

4. The Data Processor shall not engage any third party to process personal data on behalf of the Data Controller without the prior written consent of the Data Controller. Any such third party shall be subject to the same data protection obligations as the Data Processor under this Addendum.

5. The Data Processor shall notify the Data Controller of any proposed engagement of a third party at least 30 days prior to the proposed engagement date. The Data Controller shall have the right to object to such engagement within 14 days of receiving such notice.

6. In the event of a data breach involving personal data processed by the Data Processor, the Data Processor shall notify the Data Controller without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. The notification shall include details of the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, the likely consequences of the breach, and the measures taken or proposed to be taken to mitigate the risk of such breach.

7. The Data Processor shall provide the Data Controller with reasonable access to its facilities and records for the purpose of audit, inspection, or other verification of compliance with the terms of this Addendum. Such audits shall be conducted no more than once per calendar year unless otherwise agreed by the parties in writing.

8. The Data Processor shall maintain and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk of the processing. These measures shall include, but are not limited to, the following: encryption of personal data in transit and at rest, regular testing of security measures, and the implementation of access controls.

9. The Data Processor shall conduct annual penetration tests of its systems and infrastructure to ensure the security of the personal data it processes. The results of such tests shall be made available to the Data Controller upon request.

10. The Data Processor shall maintain ISO 27001 certification for its information security management system. The Data Processor shall provide the Data Controller with a copy of its current certification upon request.

11. The Data Processor shall ensure that all personnel who have access to personal data are subject to confidentiality obligations and are trained in data protection and security procedures.

12. This Addendum shall remain in effect for the duration of the Agreement and shall continue to apply for a period of 12 months following the termination of the Agreement, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the parties.

13. Any amendments to this Addendum shall be made in writing and signed by both parties.
