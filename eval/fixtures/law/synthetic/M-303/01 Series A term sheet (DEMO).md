Series A term sheet (DEMO)  
This Series A term sheet (the "Term Sheet") is entered into as of 4 September 2026, by and among Kestrel Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and Aldermoor Ventures LP (the "Investor").  

1. **Investment Amount and Valuation**  
The Investor shall invest USD 8,000,000 (the "Investment Amount") in the Company in exchange for equity securities of the Company. The pre-money valuation of the Company is USD 24,000,000 (the "Pre-Money Valuation").  

2. **Liquidation Preference**  
The Investor shall have a 1x non-participating liquidation preference with respect to the Investment Amount.  

3. **Board of Directors**  
The Company’s board of directors shall consist of five (5) members. The initial composition of the board shall be as follows: two (2) members designated by the founders of the Company, one (1) member designated by the Investor, and two (2) independent members.  

4. **Protective Provisions**  
The Investor shall have protective provisions with respect to the Series A Preferred Stock, which shall require the affirmative vote of the holders of a majority of the Series A Preferred Stock in order to take the following actions: (a) amending the Company’s certificate of incorporation or bylaws; (b) authorizing, creating, or increasing the authorized shares of any class of capital stock of the Company; (c) entering into any material transaction with an affiliate of the Company; (d) declaring or paying any dividends or distributions on common stock; (e) making any material change to the business of the Company; and (f) any other matter that the Investor deems material.  

5. **Option Pool Top-Up**  
The Company shall top up the option pool to a total of 12% of the post-money capitalization of the Company immediately following the closing of the Investment. The option pool shall be funded from the proceeds of the Investment.  

6. **Pro Rata Rights**  
The Investor shall have pro rata rights with respect to future financings of the Company, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Term Sheet.  

7. **Closing Date**  
The closing of the Investment (the "Closing") shall occur on 30 October 2026, subject to the satisfaction of all conditions set forth in this Term Sheet and the definitive agreements to be entered into by the parties.  

8. **Governing Law**  
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.  

9. **Entire Agreement**  
This Term Sheet constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and negotiations, whether written or oral.  

10. **Non-Binding Nature**  
This Term Sheet is non-binding except for the provisions set forth in Sections 1 through 10, which shall be binding upon the parties upon execution. All other terms and conditions shall be subject to negotiation and execution of definitive agreements.
