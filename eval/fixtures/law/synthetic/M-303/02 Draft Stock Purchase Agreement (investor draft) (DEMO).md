Draft Stock Purchase Agreement (investor draft) (DEMO)  
This Draft Stock Purchase Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of 20 September 2026, by and among Kestrel Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), and Aldermoor Ventures LP (the “Investor”).  

1. Purchase and Sale.  
The Investor agrees to purchase, and the Company agrees to issue and sell, 1,000,000 shares of Series A Preferred Stock of the Company, par value $0.01 per share, at a purchase price of USD 8,000,000, which shall be paid in cash upon the Closing (as defined below).  

2. Closing.  
The closing of the purchase and sale of the Series A Preferred Stock (the “Closing”) shall take place on 15 November 2026, at the offices of the Investor or such other location as the parties may agree, or such other date as the parties may mutually determine in writing.  

3. Option Pool Top-Up.  
As a condition precedent to the Closing, the Company shall, prior to the Closing, increase the size of the Company’s option pool to 10% of the total outstanding capitalization of the Company on a post-money basis following the Closing. The Company shall provide the Investor with a written confirmation of such increase prior to the Closing.  

4. Representations and Warranties of the Company.  
The Company represents and warrants to the Investor as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date as follows:  
(a) Organization and Authority. The Company is duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. The Company has full power and authority to execute, deliver, and perform its obligations under this Agreement.  
(b) Capitalization. The Company’s capitalization as of the date hereof is as follows: the founders own 70% of the outstanding capital stock of the Company, the angel investors own 15%, and the option pool is 15% (of which 9% has been granted).  
(c) No Conflicts. The execution and delivery of this Agreement and the performance of the Company’s obligations hereunder do not conflict with, or constitute a breach of, any agreement, instrument, or understanding to which the Company is a party or by which it is bound.  

5. Closing Conditions.  
The obligations of the Investor under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction, in the Investor’s sole discretion, of the following conditions:  
(a) The Company shall have delivered to the Investor a certificate of good standing for the Company, dated no earlier than 10 days prior to the Closing Date.  
(b) The Company shall have delivered to the Investor a written confirmation that the option pool has been increased to 10% of the total outstanding capitalization of the Company on a post-money basis following the Closing.  
(c) The Company shall have delivered to the Investor a fully executed copy of this Agreement and such other documents as the Investor may reasonably request.  

6. Governing Law.  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
