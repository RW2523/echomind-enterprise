Board consent approving the financing (draft) (DEMO)

WHEREAS, Kestrel Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), is engaged in the business of developing and commercializing advanced robotics technology;

WHEREAS, the Company is seeking to raise a Series A round of financing in the amount of USD 8,000,000 (the "Series A Financing");

WHEREAS, the Series A Financing is to be led by Aldermoor Ventures LP (the "Investor") at a pre-money valuation of USD 24,000,000;

WHEREAS, the terms of the Series A Financing are set forth in the term sheet dated 4 September 2026 (the "Term Sheet"), which includes, among other terms, a 1x non-participating liquidation preference;

WHEREAS, the Company’s current capitalization is as follows: founders 70%, angel investors 15%, and an option pool of 15% (of which 9% has been granted);

WHEREAS, the Term Sheet provides for an option pool top-up to 12% of the post-money capitalization of the Company prior to the closing of the Series A Financing;

WHEREAS, the Company’s board of directors (the "Board") is required to approve the Series A Financing in accordance with the Company’s bylaws and Delaware law;

NOW, THEREFORE, the undersigned members of the Board, being duly authorized and acting in accordance with the bylaws of the Company, do hereby consent in writing to the following actions:

1. The Board hereby approves the Series A Financing on the terms set forth in the Term Sheet dated 4 September 2026, including but not limited to the following material terms:

   a. The Series A Financing shall be in the amount of USD 8,000,000;

   b. The pre-money valuation of the Company shall be USD 24,000,000;

   c. The liquidation preference shall be 1x non-participating;

   d. The Board shall consist of five (5) members: two (2) founders, one (1) designee of Aldermoor Ventures LP, and two (2) independent directors;

   e. Protective provisions shall be applicable to the Series A investors upon the majority of the Series A shares;

   f. The option pool shall be topped up to 12% of the post-money capitalization of the Company prior to the closing of the Series A Financing;

   g. The Series A Financing shall include pro rata rights for the Investor;

   h. The closing of the Series A Financing is targeted for 30 October 2026.

2. The Board hereby authorizes the execution and delivery of the Stock Purchase Agreement (the "SPA") and all related documents, including but not limited to the definitive term sheet, the subscription agreement, and all necessary disclosures, in accordance with the Term Sheet and the representations and warranties of the Company.

3. The Board hereby confirms that the current capitalization of the Company is as follows: founders 70%, angel investors 15%, and an option pool of 15% (of which 9% has been granted), and that the Series A Financing shall not be dilutive to the existing shareholders except as provided in the Term Sheet.

4. The Board hereby approves the proposed closing date of 30 October 2026 for the Series A Financing, and further authorizes the Company’s management to proceed with the necessary steps to close the financing on such date.

5. The Board hereby confirms that the terms of the Series A Financing are consistent with the Term Sheet and that the draft Stock Purchase Agreement dated 20 September 2026, which contains an inconsistency regarding the option pool top-up, shall be revised to reflect the terms set forth in the Term Sheet.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the undersigned, being members of the Board of Directors of Kestrel Robotics, Inc., have executed this written consent as of the 4th day of September 2026.
