Capitalisation table summary (DEMO)

This Capitalisation Table Summary (the "Summary") is prepared as of 20 September 2026, in connection with the proposed Series A financing of Kestrel Robotics, Inc. (the "Company"), a Delaware corporation. This Summary reflects the current capitalisation of the Company prior to the Series A financing and is intended to provide a clear overview of the ownership structure as of the date hereof.

1. The Company’s capitalisation prior to the Series A financing is as follows:

   a. Founders: 70% of the outstanding shares of the Company.

   b. Angel Investors: 15% of the outstanding shares of the Company.

   c. Option Pool: 15% of the outstanding shares of the Company, of which 9% have been granted to employees and service providers.

2. The founders’ 70% ownership stake is held by the two founding shareholders of the Company, who are the original equity holders and have retained their ownership through the Company’s early-stage development.

3. The angel investors’ 15% ownership stake is held by a group of individual investors who provided early-stage funding to the Company. Their shares were issued in a private placement in 2024.

4. The option pool represents the portion of the Company’s equity reserved for employees, directors, and consultants. The option pool is currently 15% of the outstanding shares, with 9% of that amount already issued as options to key employees and service providers.

5. The current capitalisation structure reflects the Company’s pre-Series A ownership distribution, with no Series A preferred shares outstanding. The Company’s outstanding shares are all common shares, with the founders and angel investors holding their respective percentages in common stock.

6. The Company’s current capitalisation does not include any preferred shares, as the Series A financing has not yet closed. The Series A preferred shares will be issued upon the closing of the financing, subject to the terms of the definitive agreements, including the Stock Purchase Agreement.

7. The proposed Series A financing will be led by Aldermoor Ventures LP, with a pre-money valuation of USD 24,000,000. The Company is raising USD 8,000,000 in the Series A round, resulting in a post-money valuation of USD 32,000,000.

8. The term sheet dated 4 September 2026 provides for a 1x non-participating liquidation preference for the Series A investors. The Company’s board of directors will consist of five members: two founders, one designee of Aldermoor Ventures LP, and two independent directors.

9. Protective provisions will be in place for the Series A investors, requiring their approval for certain material decisions, including but not limited to the sale of the Company, the issuance of additional securities, and the entry into material contracts.

10. The Company is required to top up the option pool to 12% of the post-money capitalisation prior to the closing of the Series A. However, the draft Stock Purchase Agreement dated 20 September 2026, prepared by Aldermoor’s counsel, states that the option pool will be topped up to 10%, which is inconsistent with the term sheet.

11. The closing of the Series A is currently targeted for 30 October 2026, although the draft Stock Purchase Agreement provides for a closing date of 15 November 2026, which may require renegotiation.

12. This Summary is provided for informational purposes only and is not intended to be a binding document. The actual capitalisation structure may change as a result of negotiations, amendments to the term sheet, or changes in the definitive agreements.

13. The Company’s current capitalisation, as set forth in this Summary, reflects the ownership percentages as of 20 September 2026. The founders hold 70% of the outstanding shares, the angel investors hold 15%, and the option pool represents 15% of the outstanding shares, with 9% already issued.

14. This Summary does not include any Series A preferred shares or any other securities that will be issued upon the closing of the Series A financing. The Series A financing will result in a dilution of the existing shareholders’ ownership percentages, as the Company will issue additional shares to Aldermoor Ventures LP.

15. The Company and its advisors are working to resolve the discrepancies in the draft Stock Purchase Agreement, including the option pool top-up percentage and the closing date, in order to ensure that the Series A financing proceeds in accordance with the term sheet dated 4 September 2026.

16. This Capitalisation Table Summary is a demonstration document and is not intended to be used for any legal or financial purpose other than for the purposes of this software demonstration.
