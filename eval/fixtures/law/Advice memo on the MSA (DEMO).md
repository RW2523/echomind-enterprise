## 1. Payment

1.1 Clause 4.2 of the Master Services Agreement provides that "Invoices are payable net sixty (60) days from the invoice date." The client should diarise payment accordingly.

## 2. Data protection

2.1 Under clause 6.1 of the Master Services Agreement, the Provider must notify a personal data breach "within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of it." This is consistent with Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

## 3. Liability

3.1 Clause 8.1 of the Master Services Agreement caps each party's aggregate liability, and clause 8.3 carves out fraud and wilful misconduct.

3.2 Clause 18.3 of the Master Services Agreement entitles the client to service credits for any outage. See also paragraph 1.1 above.

## 4. Authorities
