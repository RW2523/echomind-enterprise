# CONTRACT REVIEW PLAYBOOK (DEMO): VENDOR AND SAAS AGREEMENTS

> FICTIONAL DEMO FIXTURE. An invented firm playbook for testing EchoMind Law. Not real guidance and not legal advice.

**Owner:** Commercial Contracts Group · **Version:** 2026.1 · **Applies to:** vendor, SaaS and services agreements where our client is the customer.

## How to use this playbook

For each clause, compare the counterparty's draft with our **Standard position**. If the draft is weaker, propose the standard. If the counterparty refuses, you may accept the **Fallback position** without escalation. Anything weaker than the fallback needs the escalation shown.

## P1. Payment Terms

- **Standard position:** push for Net 45 payment terms from the invoice date.
- **Fallback position:** Net 30 is acceptable.
- **Late-payment interest:** we accept a maximum of 1.5% per month on undisputed overdue amounts. Anything higher must be redlined down.
- **Escalation:** any advance-payment requirement over three (3) months goes to the client relationship partner.

## P2. Limitation of Liability

- **Standard position:** mutual cap of at least the greater of USD 500,000 or twelve (12) months' fees.
- **Fallback position:** a cap of twelve (12) months' fees, with carve-outs for confidentiality, data protection and IP indemnity.
- **Escalation:** if the liability cap drops below six (6) months' fees, the deal requires Deal Desk approval before signature.

## P3. Data Protection (regulated data)

- **Breach notification:** for contracts involving regulated data, the counterparty must report a personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.
- **Return or destruction:** the counterparty must return or destroy our client's data within 45 days after termination, and certify destruction in writing.
- **Escalation:** any refusal of audit rights goes to the Privacy Lead.

## P4. Term and Renewal

- **Standard position:** no automatic renewal; renewal only by written agreement.
- **Fallback position:** automatic renewal is acceptable if either party may give notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the term. A longer notice period is outside the fallback and must be negotiated down to 60 days.

## P5. Change of Control

- **Standard position:** the vendor must give at least 60 days' prior written notice of a change of control, and our client may terminate without penalty.
- **Fallback position:** 30 days' notice with a termination right.

## P6. Governing Law

- **Standard position:** the law of our client's home jurisdiction.
- **Fallback position:** Delaware or New York law, with courts of the same state.
