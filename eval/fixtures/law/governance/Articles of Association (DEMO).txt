ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION of Tidewater Holdings Ltd (fictional)

1. Share capital
1.1 The share capital of the company consists of ordinary shares and A preference shares.

2. Directors
2.1 The number of directors shall not be less than two.
2.2 The quorum for board meetings is two directors.
2.3 Not less than seven (7) days' notice shall be given of every board meeting.
