Thanks Tomasz. Noting for the file:

- MSA 9.4 as amended by the First Amendment (eff. 7/1/2023): written notice within 48 hours of discovery, phone calls expressly don't count. If they found it early Sunday morning, 48 hours runs out about now (Tuesday morning).
- BAA 2.4: breach report no later than 10 business days after discovery, i.e. by Friday 3/21 if discovery was 3/9. But the BAA says the shorter MSA period still applies (7.4).
- Our own HIPAA clock (164.404) - need Lionel's view on when it starts for us. Ostrander is an independent contractor under the BAA, not our agent.
