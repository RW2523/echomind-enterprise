Simon

Thank you for the interim schedule. I attach it with our preliminary comments in the right hand column. Headline points:

1. Our client's position is that the lease ends on the break date, 24 June 2026, under the notice served on 28 August. Sofia is dealing with Pellow Grange on that and I won't repeat it here. On that footing the schedule is in any event premature - your client's remedy is a terminal schedule after the end of the term.

2. Items 14, 15 and 16 (removal of the mezzanine, the shopfront and the mezz services) are not dilapidations at all. They are reinstatement items and the obligation to reinstate the 2022 works only arises if the landlord serves notice under clause 6.1 of the licence for alterations dated 7 March 2022 not less than six months before the end of the term. No such notice has been served. If the break is effective that means by 24 December 2025. Your schedule cites "Licence cl. 5.3" for these items - clause 5.3 is the covenant to make good damage caused by the works, which is something different.

3. Item 15 in particular - reinstating the original timber shopfront would put back a worn out 2016 shopfront that was in poor condition when it came out. We do not accept that any landlord acting reasonably would require it, and the costing is speculative.

4. Arithmetic - your sub-total of £82,400.00 does not agree with the items. I make items 1-17 £85,350.00; it looks as though item 12 (£2,950.00) has dropped out of the sum. Not that the higher figure helps your client but rev 2 should be right.

5. The prelims at 10% and fees at 12% are both on the high side for works of this scale and we'll want to see the basis.

6. We'd like to re-inspect jointly with you, ideally with access to the roof for the AC condensers (item 12). Store manager is Bethan Lloyd - she can do any weekday morning.
