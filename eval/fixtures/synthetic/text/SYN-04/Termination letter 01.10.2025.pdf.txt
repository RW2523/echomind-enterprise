Re: Termination of Employment

Dear Daniela:

This letter confirms our conversation today that your employment with Brackenridge Analytics, Inc. (the "Company") ends effective today, January 10, 2025 (the "Separation Date").

On November 4, 2024 you were placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (the "PIP"), which ended on January 3, 2025. At the final review the Company determined that you did not meet Objectives 1, 2 and 4 of the PIP. Taking into account your performance during and before the PIP period, the Company has decided to end your employment.

1. Final Pay. In accordance with California Labor Code Sections 201 and 227.3, your final pay has been issued to you today by direct deposit, and your final pay statement is enclosed. Your final pay includes (a) your base salary for January 6 through January 10, 2025 ($3,480.77), and (b) payment for 92.00 hours (11.5 days) of accrued and unused paid time off at your final rate of pay ($8,005.77), for total gross final pay of $11,486.54, less applicable withholdings. This is in addition to your regular pay for the pay period ending January 4, 2025, which was also paid today.

2. Expenses. Please submit any outstanding business expenses, with receipts, by February 10, 2025, through BrackNet or by email to Lucas Ferreira, Payroll and Benefits Manager (payroll@brackenridge-analytics.example). Approved expenses will be reimbursed in accordance with California Labor Code Section 2802 and Employee Handbook Policy 7.4.

3. Benefits. Your medical, dental and vision coverage will continue through January 31, 2025. You will receive a COBRA election notice by mail from the Company's COBRA administrator explaining your right to continue coverage, the cost and how to elect. You will have sixty (60) days from the later of the date coverage ends or the date of the notice to elect. You may also be eligible for Cal-COBRA after federal COBRA ends. Your vested 401(k) account balance remains yours, and the plan administrator will send you information about your options.

4. Stock Options. As of the Separation Date, 19,500 of the 24,000 options granted to you under Grant No. 2021-0417 are vested. Unvested options were forfeited on the Separation Date. Under your Stock Option Grant Notice you may exercise your vested options during the ninety (90) day period following the Separation Date, that is, until April 10, 2025. Please contact equity@brackenridge-analytics.example with any questions about exercise.

5. 2024 Annual Bonus. Under Section 5(a) of the 2024 Annual Bonus Plan, a participant must be employed by the Company on the payment date, March 14, 2025, to earn a 2024 bonus. Because your employment ends before that date, you are not eligible for a 2024 bonus.

6. Continuing Obligations. Your obligations under Sections 4 (Confidential Information and Inventions) and 5 (Non-Solicitation of Employees) of your Employment Agreement dated August 18, 2021 continue after the Separation Date. Section 9 of that agreement (Arbitration of Disputes) also remains in effect.

7. Company Property. We have collected your Company laptop and badge today. If you have any other Company property or documents, including any Company information on personal devices or accounts, please return them to the People team by January 17, 2025.

8. Unemployment Insurance. Enclosed are the EDD pamphlet "For Your Benefit" (DE 2320) and a Notice to Employee as to Change in Relationship. You may apply for unemployment insurance benefits with the Employment Development Department.

If you have questions about your final pay or benefits, please contact Marisol Gutierrez-Vance at (408) 555-0131 or marisol.gutierrez-vance@brackenridge-analytics.example.

We thank you for your contributions to Brackenridge and wish you well.
