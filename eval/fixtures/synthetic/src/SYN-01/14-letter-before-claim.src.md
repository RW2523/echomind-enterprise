**Varnholt Aerostructures Limited - Supply and Installation Agreement dated 14 March 2023 - Letter of Claim, Dispute Notice and notice of set-off**

1. Introduction

1.1 We act for Varnholt Aerostructures Limited ("Varnholt"). This letter concerns the Supply and Installation Agreement between Varnholt and Quellcrest Robotics Ltd ("Quellcrest") dated 14 March 2023, as varied by Variation Agreement No. 1 dated 9 January 2024 ("VA1") and Variation Agreement No. 2 dated 22 July 2024 ("VA2") (together, "the Agreement"). Capitalised terms have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 This letter is a letter of claim under the Pre-Action Protocol for Construction and Engineering Disputes. It is also (a) a Dispute Notice under clause 21.1 of the Agreement and (b) written notice of set-off under clause 7.5 of the Agreement.

2. The Agreement

2.1 Under the Agreement Quellcrest is to supply, install, test and commission eight robotic drilling and fastening cells (the "Cells") at Varnholt's Sinfin Lane Works, Derby. Following VA1 the Contract Price is £6,550,000 (exclusive of VAT): £810,000 for each of Cells 1 to 6 and £845,000 for each of Cells 7 and 8.

2.2 Clause 9 and Schedule 4 provide that, if Acceptance of a Cell has not occurred by its Planned Acceptance Date, Quellcrest shall pay Liquidated Damages of 0.5% of the Cell Price for each full week from the Planned Acceptance Date until Acceptance, subject to a maximum of 10% of the Cell Price for each Cell (clause 9.5). Varnholt may deduct Liquidated Damages from sums due to Quellcrest under clause 7.5 (clause 9.6).

2.3 The Planned Acceptance Dates are those in Schedule 3 as replaced by VA1. By VA2 the Planned Acceptance Dates of Cells 5 to 8 were extended by four weeks. The Planned Acceptance Date of Cell 4 was not changed by VA2 and remains 31 May 2024.

3. The delays

3.1 Cells 1, 2 and 3 were Accepted on or before their Planned Acceptance Dates. Every one of Cells 4 to 8 has been late.

3.2 Cell 4 failed its SAT on 13 August 2024 (cycle time 24.1 s against a requirement of 22.0 s, three swage collar defects and an aborted continuous run). Its end effector has been returned to Coventry and, as at the date of this letter, Quellcrest has not offered a date for the repeat SAT. Cell 4 has not been Accepted.

3.3 Cells 5, 6, 7 and 8 were Accepted on 20 September, 27 September, 25 September and 1 October 2024 respectively.

4.3 Liquidated Damages for Cell 4 continue to accrue at £4,050 for each further full week until Acceptance, up to the maximum of £81,000, which will be reached after 20 full weeks, on 18 October 2024, if Cell 4 has not been Accepted by then.

4.4 Accordingly, as at the date of this letter, Quellcrest is liable to pay Liquidated Damages of £196,325, together with such further Liquidated Damages as accrue on Cell 4.

5. Quellcrest's claim for an extension of time (Bay 3)

5.1 By letter ref. QRL/VAR/EOT/003, attached to an email sent by Mr Whitlow at 18:52 on 15 May 2024, Quellcrest claimed an extension of time for Cells 4, 5 and 6 on the ground that Bay 3 did not comply with the Site Readiness Requirements in Schedule 5. Varnholt rejected that claim on 16 May 2024 and maintains its rejection, for the following reasons.

5.2 Bay 3 complied with Schedule 5. The slab was poured on 18 March 2024, 42 days before the Site Readiness Date, and achieved its specified strength. Schedule 5 contains no relative humidity limit, and the drawing referred to in paragraph 2.2(f) (QR-FD-0412 rev B) required only that the slab be "dry". The humidity limit and the change of anchor type first appeared in rev C of that drawing, issued by Quellcrest on 7 May 2024, after handover. Bay 3 was handed over by Bay Handover Certificate HC-B3-01 on 29 April 2024.

5.3 Further and in any event, the notice was not given in time. Clause 11.3 makes written notice within 14 days after the date on which Quellcrest became aware of the Buyer Delay a condition precedent to any extension. Quellcrest was aware of the slab moisture readings, and that it would not install anchors because of them, by 30 April 2024 at the latest: Mr Whitlow telephoned Mr Anand of Varnholt about them that afternoon, and Ms Kowalczyk's email of 2 May 2024 records that Mr Whitlow had called her about them on 30 April and urges that the contract notice be sent within "14 days". The 14-day period therefore expired on 14 May 2024. The email of 15 May 2024 was sent after 5.30 pm and is deemed received on 16 May 2024 under clause 22.3.

5.4 Further, under clause 22.4 a notice under clause 11 must be given to the address in clause 22.2 and is not validly given by email to the Contract Manager alone. The notice was emailed to Ms Thorne and Mr Anand, with a copy to Ms Kowalczyk. It was not sent to Varnholt's notice address by email, and Quellcrest is put to proof of any other service.

5.5 Finally, any delay caused by Bay 3 could only have affected Cell 4. Cells 5 and 6 did not pass FAT until 3 June and 17 June 2024 respectively, because Quellcrest's sub-supplier delivered their spindle drive units late (as recorded in VA2). By clause 11.7, no extension is available for Cells 5 and 6 in those circumstances.

6. Notice of set-off

6.1 Quellcrest's invoice QR-INV-2024-131 (Milestone 4 for Cells 1, 2 and 3) was received by Varnholt on 5 August 2024. Pursuant to clause 7.5 of the Agreement, Varnholt hereby gives notice that it sets off against the sum claimed in that invoice the Liquidated Damages of £196,325 set out in paragraph 4 above, calculated as shown in the table, and that it will set off such further Liquidated Damages as accrue in respect of Cell 4.

6.2 Varnholt will account to Quellcrest for any balance of that invoice once the Liquidated Damages for Cell 4 have been finally determined.

7. Other matters

7.1 Varnholt reserves its right under clause 8.7 to recover its reasonable costs of attending and supporting the failed SAT of Cell 4 on 13 August 2024 and any repeat SAT, and all its rights under clause 19 in respect of Cell 4.

7.2 Varnholt requires Quellcrest to confirm within 7 days the date on which the repeat SAT of Cell 4 will take place.

8. Senior management meeting

8.1 Under clause 21.2, the Managing Directors are to meet within 15 Business Days after the date of this Dispute Notice, that is by 25 October 2024. Mr Graham Petrakis, Varnholt's Managing Director, is available at Sinfin Lane Works on 22 or 24 October 2024. Please let us know which date Mr Fenn (and, if Quellcrest wishes, Ms Kowalczyk) will attend.

9. What Varnholt requires

9.1 Varnholt requires Quellcrest to:

(a) confirm that it accepts liability for Liquidated Damages of £196,325 as at the date of this letter, and for further Liquidated Damages on Cell 4 as they accrue;

(b) confirm that it accepts the set-off notified in paragraph 6; and

(c) withdraw its claim for an extension of time in respect of Bay 3.

9.2 Under the Protocol we expect a full response within 28 days of receipt of this letter, that is by 1 November 2024. If the matter is not resolved, Varnholt intends to issue proceedings in the Technology and Construction Court in accordance with clause 21.5 without further notice, and will seek its costs and interest.
