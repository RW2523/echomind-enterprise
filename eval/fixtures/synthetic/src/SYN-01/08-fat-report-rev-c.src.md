## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This report records the factory acceptance tests ("FAT") of the eight robotic drilling and fastening cells supplied to Varnholt Aerostructures Limited ("Varnholt" or "the Buyer") under the Supply and Installation Agreement dated 14 March 2023, as varied by Variation Agreement No. 1 dated 9 January 2024 (Cells 7 and 8), clause 8 and Schedule 1 Part C.

1.2 All FATs were carried out in Test Bay T2 at the Supplier's Coventry facility. Each FAT was witnessed by the Buyer's Quality Lead, Mr T. Wierzbicki. The Buyer's Contract Manager, Ms R. Thorne, also attended the FATs of Cells 1, 4, 7 (first test and re-test) and 8 (re-test).

1.3 This report does not cover site acceptance tests (SAT), which are recorded separately on form QRL-F-SAT-02.

## 2. References

2.1 Supply and Installation Agreement, clause 8 (Testing) and Schedule 1 Part B (performance requirements) and Part C (Test Specification).

2.2 Variation Agreement No. 1, Annex (Cells 7 and 8: B-series fixtures, 14 m track, three fastener diameters).

2.3 Test procedure QRL-TP-117 rev 3; laser tracker calibration certificate CAL-LT-2023-0912 (valid to 30/09/2024).

2.4 Non-conformance reports NCR-031 (Cell 7) and NCR-033 (Cell 8).

## 3. Test set-up

3.1 Test coupon: 2024-T3 aluminium alloy, 6 mm + 4 mm stack, 50 holes per coupon (4.8 mm and 6.35 mm; for Cells 7 and 8 also 4.0 mm), drilled, countersunk and fastened by the Cell under test.

3.2 Positional accuracy measured as the true position of each hole against nominal, by laser tracker; the result reported is the worst hole on the coupon. Repeatability measured over 30 repeat cycles to a reference hole. Cycle time is the mean over 120 consecutive fasteners (drill, countersink, insert, swage).

3.3 Ambient temperature in T2 was held at 20°C ± 2°C during all tests.

## 4. Acceptance criteria (Schedule 1 Part B)

| Ref | Parameter | Requirement |

## 7. Non-conformances

7.1 NCR-031 (Cell 7) and NCR-033 (Cell 8). Root cause: the thermal compensation parameters of the robot controller had not been re-set for the 14 m track, and the joint between the second and third new-generation track sections was out of alignment by 0.08 mm. Corrective action: track joint re-shimmed and re-surveyed; compensation tables re-generated over the full 14 m; laser tracker volumetric check repeated. Preventive action: track survey added to the pre-FAT checklist (QRL-TP-117 rev 4).

7.2 Both NCRs were closed on re-test. No NCRs were raised on Cells 1 to 6.

## 8. Conclusion

8.1 All eight Cells have passed FAT in accordance with clause 8 and Schedule 1 Part C of the Agreement, and FAT Certificates FATC-01 to FATC-08 have been issued by the Buyer.

8.2 Cells 1 to 6 passed at the first attempt. Cells 7 and 8 passed on re-test on 22/07/2024 and 29/07/2024 respectively.
