## 9. Traps (deliberate inconsistencies) and which document prevails

1. **Agreement date.** Word executed version (01) blank "________ 2023"; the date 14 March 2023 is only handwritten on the scanned execution pages (03). The scan prevails: the SIA is dated 14 March 2023. (02's header date 27/02/2023 is the draft date, not the contract date.)
2. **Draft v3 vs executed.** Draft: cap 150% at cl. 19.2; LDs 1% per week, cap 15%; step-in rights at cl. 15 so every later clause is one higher (limitation 19, termination 20; warranty 16). Executed: cap 100% at cl. 18.2; LDs 0.5% / 10% at cl. 9 and Sch 4; no step-in; warranty cl. 15; termination cl. 19. The executed SIA prevails (entire agreement, cl. 24). "Clause 19" means Termination in the executed SIA.
3. **Planned Acceptance Dates.** Original Sch 3 (01): Cells 4–6 on 24 May, 7 Jun, 21 Jun 2024. VA1 (04) replaced Sch 3: 31 May, 14 Jun, 28 Jun 2024 and added Cells 7–8 (12 Jul, 26 Jul). VA1 prevails, then VA2 extends Cells 5–8.
4. **Contract Price.** 01 says GBP 4,860,000; after VA1 it is GBP 6,550,000 (so the cl. 18.2 cap is GBP 6,550,000). VA1 prevails.
5. **VA2 four vs six weeks.** Typed "four (4) weeks" crossed through; handwritten "six (6) weeks - amended GW / RT", initialled by both signatories. Six weeks prevails (05; confirmed by 15 and 16). The LBC (14), the file note (17) and the board minutes' estimate (18) use four weeks. Correct PADs: 26 Jul, 9 Aug, 23 Aug, 6 Sep 2024. Thorne's internal email of 29 Aug 2024 (07) says every one of Cells 4–8 is already past its date; on the six-week dates Cell 8 (6 Sep) was not yet late.
6. **LBC LD total.** 14 claims GBP 196,325; correct figure on the same date is GBP 163,225 (overstated by 33,100); final correct figure after Cell 4's Acceptance on 8 Nov 2024 is GBP 171,325, Cell 4 capped at 81,000.
7. **Set-off clause number.** Ferris's email (07) relies on "clause 7.6"; set-off is cl. 7.5; 7.6 is contractual interest. The SIA (01) prevails; the LBC (14) cites 7.5 correctly.
8. **Invoice 131 percentage.** 11 applies 25% (607,500 + 121,500 = 729,000). Schedule 2 (01, as replaced by 04) fixes M4 at 20%: 486,000 + 97,200 = 583,200. Schedule 2 prevails. Nobody in the file notices: the ledger (12/13), the internal emails (07), the board minutes (18) and Brannock Steele (15) all use 729,000; the 532,675 "balance" is built on it. The source of the error is the 25/25/25/25 quotation split noted in 02.
9. **Invoice 131 due date.** Invoice: "30 days from date of invoice", due 01/09/2024. Cl. 7.3: 30 days after receipt (received 05/08/2024) → 04/09/2024. Contract prevails; Brannock Steele's interest from 2 Sep 2024 starts too early.
10. **US dates.** Kowalczyk's "4/30" = 30 Apr, "05/01" = 1 May, "05/06/2024" = 6 May 2024 (UK bank holiday Monday; Thorne's reply confirms) — not 5 Jan / 5 Jun. FAT report rev C approval "08/02/2024" by Kowalczyk = 2 Aug 2024, not 8 Feb 2024 (the report records July tests).
11. **Statutory vs contractual interest.** 15 claims 1998 Act interest at 13.25%; cl. 7.6 provides 3% over base and declares itself a substantial remedy. Arguable; 15 ignores it.
12. **Deemed Bay 3 compliance.** WS draft (16) says Quellcrest gave no notice of non-compliance within three Business Days; the email chain (06) shows Whitlow's notice on 2 May, the third Business Day. The emails prevail (Harper Quinn's comment in 16 flags it).
13. **EOT notice timing and service** — see section 7: arguable; Varnholt's better view is late (awareness 30 Apr; deadline 14 May; deemed received 16 May; not served under cl. 22.4).
14. **Bay 3 extension for Cells 5 and 6.** Brannock Steele claims five weeks for Cells 4–6; Cells 5–6 were held up by Quellcrest's own spindle delay (FAT 3 and 17 Jun), so cl. 11.7 limits any extension to Cell 4.
15. **Delay damages vs LDs.** The board minutes (18) and file note (17) record a GBP 410,000 lost-production estimate; cl. 9.4 makes LDs the sole remedy for delay and cl. 18.5 excludes lost production. Not recoverable.
