Gary, Brenda,

We do not accept that Bay 3 is non-compliant. Briefly:
- Sch 5 para 2.2 does not specify any RH limit. "Dry" means what it says - no standing water or wet areas - and the slab is dry.
- The slab is C32/40 (28-day cubes 41.5 N/mm2), poured 18 March, i.e. 42 days before handover against the 28 days required.
- QR-FD-0412 rev B note 6 says "slab to be dry" and nothing more. If Quellcrest wanted an RH limit it should have been on the drawing.
- 3 of 5 pull-outs failing points at least as much to the anchor or the installation as to the slab. Please send the test records and confirm the anchor type used.

Craig will get our flooring contractor (Pennick) in to take independent readings and look at the pull-out locations.

Brenda - 05/06 would be Monday 6 May, which is a bank holiday here. Can we do Tuesday 7 May, 2pm UK / 9am Eastern instead?
