## BACKGROUND

(A) The Buyer wishes to automate the drilling and fastening of its A-series rib family at Sinfin Lane Works, Derby.

(B) The Supplier has agreed to design, manufacture, supply, install, test and commission six robotic drilling and fastening cells on the terms of this Agreement.
