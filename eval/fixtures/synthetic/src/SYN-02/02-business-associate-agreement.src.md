# BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

This BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT (this "BAA") is entered into effective as of February 1, 2022 (the "BAA Effective Date") by and between Larkspur Health Partners, Inc., a New York corporation ("Covered Entity"), and Ostrander Billing Solutions, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Business Associate").

WHEREAS, Covered Entity and Business Associate are parties to a Master Services Agreement, Contract No. LHP-OBS-2022-01, dated as of February 1, 2022 (as amended from time to time, the "Underlying Agreement"), under which Business Associate performs revenue-cycle management and billing services for Covered Entity;

WHEREAS, in performing those services Business Associate creates, receives, maintains and transmits Protected Health Information on behalf of Covered Entity; and

WHEREAS, the parties intend to comply with the requirements of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act, and the regulations at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164 (collectively, the "HIPAA Rules"), including the requirement at 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e) and 164.504(e) that a covered entity obtain satisfactory assurances from its business associates;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 General. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this BAA have the meanings given to them in the HIPAA Rules, including the terms Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Health Care Operations, Minimum Necessary, Notice of Privacy Practices, Required by Law, Subcontractor and Use.

1.2 Specific Definitions. For purposes of this BAA:

(a) "Breach" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.

(b) "Business Associate" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 and, in reference to the party to this BAA, means Ostrander Billing Solutions, LLC.

(c) "Covered Entity" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 and, in reference to the party to this BAA, means Larkspur Health Partners, Inc., including each of its clinics.

(d) "Discovery" or "discovered" means, with respect to a Breach or Security Incident, the first day on which the Breach or Security Incident is known to Business Associate or, by exercising reasonable diligence, would have been known to Business Associate, including to any person, other than the person committing the Breach, who is an employee, officer or other agent of Business Associate, consistent with 45 C.F.R. § 164.410(a)(2).

(e) "Electronic Protected Health Information" or "ePHI" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, limited to information created, received, maintained or transmitted by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity.

(f) "Individual" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 and includes a person who qualifies as a personal representative under 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(g).

(g) "Protected Health Information" or "PHI" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, limited to information created, received, maintained or transmitted by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity.

(h) "Secretary" means the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services or the Secretary's designee.

(i) "Security Incident" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.304.

(j) "Unsecured Protected Health Information" or "Unsecured PHI" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402, being PHI that is not rendered unusable, unreadable or indecipherable to unauthorized persons through the use of a technology or methodology specified by the Secretary in guidance.

(k) "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a day on which banks in the State of New York are authorized or required by law to close.

2. OBLIGATIONS AND ACTIVITIES OF BUSINESS ASSOCIATE

2.1 Permitted Use and Disclosure Only. Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this BAA or as Required by Law.

2.2 Safeguards. Business Associate shall use appropriate safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 with respect to ePHI, to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as provided for by this BAA. Without limiting the foregoing, Business Associate shall implement the encryption, access control and vulnerability management requirements of Section 9 and Exhibit C of the Underlying Agreement with respect to all ePHI.

2.3 Reporting of Non-Permitted Uses and Disclosures and Security Incidents. Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity in writing any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this BAA of which it becomes aware, and any Security Incident of which it becomes aware, without unreasonable delay. The parties agree that this Section 2.3 constitutes notice by Business Associate of the ongoing existence and occurrence of attempted but unsuccessful Security Incidents (such as pings, port scans and denied log-on attempts) that do not result in unauthorized access, use or disclosure of ePHI, for which no further notice is required.

2.4 Reporting of Breaches of Unsecured PHI. Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity in writing any Breach of Unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and in no case later than ten (10) business days after discovery of the Breach. A Breach shall be treated as discovered by Business Associate as provided in Section 1.2(d). Business Associate shall not delay a report under this Section 2.4 on the ground that it has not yet completed its risk assessment or investigation. The report shall include, to the extent possible, and Business Associate shall supplement it as information becomes available:

(a) the identification of each Individual whose Unsecured PHI has been, or is reasonably believed by Business Associate to have been, accessed, acquired, used or disclosed;

(b) a brief description of what happened, including the date of the Breach and the date of discovery of the Breach;

(c) a description of the types of Unsecured PHI involved, such as full name, Social Security number, date of birth, home address, account number, diagnosis, procedure or insurance information;

(d) a description of what Business Associate is doing to investigate the Breach, to mitigate harm to Individuals and to protect against further Breaches;

(e) whether the Unsecured PHI was encrypted, and if so the method, and the status of the encryption keys;

(f) the identity of any Subcontractor involved; and

(g) any other information Covered Entity reasonably requests to meet its obligations under 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.404 through 164.408 and applicable state law.

2.5 Presumption of Breach. Any acquisition, access, use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by the HIPAA Rules is presumed to be a Breach unless Covered Entity, after consultation with Business Associate, determines that there is a low probability that the PHI has been compromised based on the risk assessment described in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.

2.6 Mitigation. Business Associate shall mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect known to Business Associate of a use or disclosure of PHI by Business Associate or its Subcontractors in violation of this BAA.

2.7 Subcontractors. In accordance with 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), Business Associate shall ensure that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate agrees in writing to the same restrictions, conditions and requirements that apply to Business Associate under this BAA.

2.8 Access. Within ten (10) Business Days after a request by Covered Entity, Business Associate shall make PHI maintained in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity as necessary for Covered Entity to meet its obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524.

2.9 Amendment. Within ten (10) Business Days after a request by Covered Entity, Business Associate shall make any amendment to PHI in a Designated Record Set that Covered Entity directs or agrees to under 45 C.F.R. § 164.526.

2.10 Accounting of Disclosures. Business Associate shall maintain and, within ten (10) Business Days after request, make available the information required for Covered Entity to provide an accounting of disclosures under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528.

2.11 Compliance with Subpart E. To the extent Business Associate carries out an obligation of Covered Entity under Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, Business Associate shall comply with the requirements of Subpart E that apply to Covered Entity in the performance of that obligation.

2.12 Books and Records. Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary for purposes of determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules, and shall promptly notify Covered Entity of any such request unless prohibited by law.

2.13 Cooperation in Investigations. Business Associate shall cooperate fully and promptly with Covered Entity, and with any forensic examiner or counsel engaged by or on behalf of Covered Entity, in investigating any Security Incident or Breach, including by providing access to relevant logs, system images, configuration records, personnel and the reports of any forensic investigator engaged by Business Associate, and by answering reasonable written questions within five (5) Business Days.

3. PERMITTED USES AND DISCLOSURES BY BUSINESS ASSOCIATE

3.1 Services. Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the services set out in the Underlying Agreement, provided that such use or disclosure would not violate Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if done by Covered Entity.

3.2 Minimum Necessary. Business Associate shall request, use and disclose only the minimum necessary PHI to accomplish the intended purpose, in accordance with Covered Entity's minimum necessary policies.

3.3 Management and Administration. Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities, and may disclose PHI for those purposes if the disclosure is Required by Law, or if Business Associate obtains reasonable written assurances from the recipient that the PHI will remain confidential and be used or further disclosed only as Required by Law or for the purpose for which it was disclosed, and that the recipient will notify Business Associate of any breach of confidentiality.

3.4 No Data Aggregation or De-identification. Business Associate shall not aggregate PHI with the data of other clients of Business Associate, and shall not de-identify PHI for its own purposes, without the prior written consent of Covered Entity.

3.5 No Sale or Marketing. Business Associate shall not sell PHI or use or disclose PHI for marketing purposes.

4. OBLIGATIONS OF COVERED ENTITY

4.1 Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any limitation in its Notice of Privacy Practices to the extent such limitation may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

4.2 Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any change in, or revocation of, an Individual's permission to use or disclose PHI, to the extent such change may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

4.3 Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any restriction on the use or disclosure of PHI to which Covered Entity has agreed under 45 C.F.R. § 164.522, to the extent such restriction may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

4.4 Covered Entity shall not request Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if done by Covered Entity.

5. BREACH NOTIFICATION AND COSTS

5.1 Control of Notifications. Covered Entity shall determine, in its sole discretion, whether notification of a Breach to Individuals, the Secretary, the media or any state authority is required or appropriate, and the content, timing and method of any such notification. Business Associate shall not notify any Individual, Governmental Authority or the media of a Breach involving PHI without the prior written approval of Covered Entity, except as Required by Law.

5.2 Reimbursement of Notification Costs. To the extent a Breach of Unsecured PHI arises from the acts or omissions of Business Associate or any of its Subcontractors, Business Associate shall reimburse Covered Entity for the reasonable costs incurred by Covered Entity in providing the notifications required by 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.404 through 164.408 and by applicable state law, including the costs of printing, postage, substitute notice, media notice and a call center for responding to Individuals' inquiries. This Section 5.2 is in addition to, and not in limitation of, Business Associate's obligations under Section 11 of the Underlying Agreement.

5.3 Cooperation. Business Associate shall cooperate with Covered Entity in preparing any notification, and shall provide such information about the Breach as Covered Entity reasonably requests to prepare notifications that comply with the HIPAA Rules and applicable state law.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 Term. This BAA is effective as of the BAA Effective Date and shall remain in effect until the termination or expiry of the Underlying Agreement and the return or destruction of all PHI in accordance with Section 6.3.

6.2 Termination for Cause. Upon Covered Entity's determination that Business Associate has violated a material term of this BAA, Covered Entity may (a) give Business Associate written notice and an opportunity to cure the violation within thirty (30) days, and terminate this BAA and the Underlying Agreement if Business Associate does not cure the violation within that time; or (b) terminate this BAA and the Underlying Agreement immediately if cure is not possible.

6.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Business Associate shall return to Covered Entity or, at Covered Entity's direction, destroy all PHI that Business Associate or its Subcontractors maintain in any form, and shall retain no copies. If return or destruction is infeasible, Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity of the conditions that make it infeasible, extend the protections of this BAA to such PHI, and limit further uses and disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible.

6.4 Survival. The obligations of Business Associate under Sections 2.3, 2.4, 2.13, 5 and 6.3 survive termination of this BAA.

7. MISCELLANEOUS

7.1 Regulatory References. A reference in this BAA to a section of the HIPAA Rules means the section as in effect or as amended.

7.2 Amendment. The parties shall take such action as is necessary to amend this BAA from time to time to comply with the requirements of the HIPAA Rules. Any other amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.

7.3 Interpretation. Any ambiguity in this BAA shall be resolved to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

7.4 Relationship to Underlying Agreement. This BAA is incorporated into and forms part of the Underlying Agreement. In the event of a conflict between this BAA and the Underlying Agreement with respect to the use, disclosure or safeguarding of PHI, this BAA shall control; provided, however, that where the Underlying Agreement imposes on Business Associate obligations that are more stringent than, or more protective of PHI than, the obligations in this BAA (including any shorter period for giving notice of a Security Incident or Breach), those obligations shall apply in addition to this BAA and shall not be deemed to conflict with it. Nothing in this BAA limits any remedy of Covered Entity under the Underlying Agreement.

7.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries. Nothing in this BAA confers any rights, remedies or obligations on any person other than the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

7.6 Independent Contractor. Business Associate is an independent contractor and is not an agent of Covered Entity for any purpose, including for purposes of 45 C.F.R. § 164.410.

7.7 Governing Law. This BAA is governed by the laws of the State of New York, to the extent not preempted by the HIPAA Rules or other federal law.

7.8 Notices. Reports under Sections 2.3 and 2.4 shall be delivered to Covered Entity's Privacy Officer by email at privacy@larkspurhealth.example and in accordance with Section 20 of the Underlying Agreement. All other notices under this BAA shall be given in accordance with Section 20 of the Underlying Agreement.

7.9 Counterparts. This BAA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
