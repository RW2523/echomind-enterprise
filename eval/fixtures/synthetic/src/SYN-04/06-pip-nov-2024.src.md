## 1. Purpose

This Performance Improvement Plan ("PIP") identifies specific areas where your performance has not met the expectations for a Senior Data Scientist, sets out the improvement required, and describes the support the Company will provide during the PIP period. The goal of this PIP is to help you succeed in your role.

## 2. Background

As discussed in our 1:1 on September 12, 2024 (verbal warning), and again in your 2024 mid-year check-in, there have been ongoing concerns about the pace of your delivery on Ledgerline work, your adoption of the Atlas platform stack, and your responsiveness to the team. Since September 23, 2024 your primary responsibility has been the Ledgerline query-layer migration and data validation work, which is on the critical path for our Q1 2025 finance-customer releases. In the October sprints you missed two sprint commitments (LDG-407 and LDG-409), and status on your tickets was often not updated until asked. Atlas onboarding modules assigned to the whole team in August remain incomplete.

## 3. Areas for Improvement and Objectives

Objective 1 - Ledgerline delivery.
Deliver LDG-412 (finance dashboard query-layer migration) to code-complete and merged by November 22, 2024, and LDG-418 (data validation suite for the migrated query layer) by December 13, 2024, each meeting the acceptance criteria in the ticket.
Measure: tickets merged by the due dates with no open blocking review comments.

Objective 2 - Atlas platform adoption.
Complete Atlas onboarding modules 1 through 4 by December 6, 2024, and ship one feature pipeline (the Ledgerline-to-Atlas revenue feature backfill) to the Atlas staging environment by December 20, 2024.
Measure: module completion recorded in the learning portal; pipeline running in staging and reviewed by the Atlas tech lead.

Objective 3 - Communication.
Post a written weekly status update in the #ds-team channel every Friday by 5:00 p.m. PT covering progress, risks and next steps, and attend the daily stand-up (or post an asynchronous update when out).
Measure: weekly updates posted on time for each week of the PIP period.

Objective 4 - Collaboration and code review.
Respond to code reviews assigned to you within two (2) business days.
Measure: average review response time of two business days or less over the PIP period, based on repository data.

## 4. Support Provided

- Weekly 30-minute pairing session on the Atlas stack with Kevin Strand, Atlas tech lead.
- Access to the Atlas staging environment and the Atlas onboarding learning path (to be requested by your manager in the first week of the PIP).
- Biweekly check-in meetings with your manager on the dates listed above; your HR Business Partner may attend.
- Your manager will review ticket scope with you at each check-in and agree any changes to due dates in writing.

## 5. Check-ins and Review

At each check-in we will review progress against each objective and record it in BrackNet. At the final review on or about January 3, 2025, your manager and your HR Business Partner will assess whether each objective has been met. Possible outcomes include successful completion of the PIP, an extension of the PIP, or further action.

## 6. Consequences

Failure to meet the objectives of this PIP, or to sustain the required level of performance after the PIP period, may result in further disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. This PIP is issued under Employee Handbook Policies 6.1 and 6.3. Nothing in this PIP changes the at-will nature of your employment, as described in Employee Handbook Policy 2.1 and Section 7 of your Employment Agreement, and nothing in this PIP guarantees employment for the PIP period.
