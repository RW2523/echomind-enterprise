Termination of Continuous Service: If the Optionholder's Continuous Service terminates for any reason other than Cause, death or Disability, the Optionholder may exercise the vested portion of the Option for ninety (90) days after the date of termination (but not later than the Expiration Date). If Continuous Service terminates because of the Optionholder's death or Disability, the vested portion may be exercised for twelve (12) months after termination. If Continuous Service is terminated for Cause, the Option terminates immediately. The unvested portion of the Option is forfeited on the date Continuous Service terminates.

Incentive Stock Option Treatment: The Optionholder understands that, to retain treatment as an Incentive Stock Option, the Option generally must be exercised within three (3) months after termination of employment (one year in the case of Disability), and that the portion of an Incentive Stock Option that first becomes exercisable in any calendar year with an aggregate Fair Market Value (determined at the Date of Grant) of more than $100,000 is treated as a Nonstatutory Stock Option. Any extension of the post-termination exercise period may cause the Option to cease to qualify as an Incentive Stock Option.

Method of Exercise: The Option may be exercised by delivering a Notice of Exercise to the Company's stock administrator (equity@brackenridge-analytics.example) together with payment of the Exercise Price by cash or check or, if permitted by the Company, by any other method permitted by the Plan, and satisfaction of any tax withholding obligations.

Additional Terms and Acknowledgments: The Optionholder acknowledges receipt of, and understands and agrees to, this Grant Notice, the Option Agreement and the Plan. The Optionholder acknowledges that this Grant Notice, the Option Agreement and the Plan set out the entire understanding between the Optionholder and the Company regarding the Option and supersede all prior oral and written agreements on that subject, except for the Optionholder's offer letter and employment agreement to the extent they describe the Option. Nothing in this Grant Notice confers any right to continued employment.
