# SYN-04 bible — Okafor-Reyes v Brackenridge Analytics

California employment matter: internal age-discrimination complaint, PIP, termination, counsel's demand letter,
separation agreement negotiation. Calder & Wynne LLP acts for the EMPLOYER (Brackenridge Analytics, Inc.).
Every company, person, address, email domain and figure is invented. This file is the answer key; the documents are
`01`–`15` in this folder.

## 1. Summary of the matter

Daniela Okafor-Reyes (born 1983; 41 at the time of her complaint) joined Brackenridge Analytics, Inc. as a Senior Data
Scientist on September 7, 2021 at USD 168,000 a year (raised to USD 181,000 from April 1, 2023, a raise documented only
in the payroll register). On October 21, 2024 she emailed HR a formal complaint that her manager Trevor Albright made
age-related remarks and gave the lead role on the new Atlas platform to a 29-year-old colleague. Seven days later
(October 28, 2024) Albright emailed his VP proposing to "restructure" the team so that her role "goes away", noting it
would free her 2024 bonus accrual. The VP balked at the timing; Albright replied that it was "a performance conversation
anyway" and asked to go "straight to a PIP"; the VP then ruled out any restructure "full stop" while the HR matter was
open. A 60-day PIP was issued November 4, 2024. HR's investigation (case HR-INV-2024-031) closed
December 6, 2024 as "unsubstantiated". In December 2024 payroll deducted 16 PTO hours ("PTO correction -16.00 hrs")
for November 25–26, 2024 at Albright's request; she says she worked those days. She was terminated January 10, 2025
for not meeting the PIP and paid 92.00 hours (11.5 days) of PTO. Her counsel Ostrowski & Lam LLP sent a demand on
February 3, 2025 (retaliation, age discrimination, 2024 bonus, waiting-time penalties; USD 475,000 within 21 days).
Calder & Wynne sent a separation agreement (v1, February 14, 2025); Ostrowski & Lam returned a mark-up (v2 redline,
February 21, 2025) which is unsigned.
