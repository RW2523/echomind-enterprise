**Merrow Arcade, Bath - change of company name and of address for notices**

We are writing to all tenants of Merrow Arcade to notify you of two changes affecting your landlord.

**1. Change of name**

On 6 November 2023 Carrow Hill Estates Limited changed its name to Carrow Hill Property Holdings Limited. The company number (05928814) is unchanged. The change of name does not affect the identity of your landlord or the terms of your lease, which continue in full force. Invoices issued by our managing agents since January 2024 have shown the new name.

**2. Change of registered office and address for notices**

With effect from 1 June 2024 our registered office and head office will move from Carrow House, 11 Ashgrove Terrace, Bath BA2 6LP to:

9 Tolcarne Place
Bristol BS1 6QE

For the purposes of the notices clause in your lease (clause 21 in the case of the Merrow Arcade unit leases), please note that with effect from 1 June 2024 all notices to be served on the landlord must be sent to:

The Company Secretary
Carrow Hill Property Holdings Limited
9 Tolcarne Place
Bristol BS1 6QE

We will not be retaining any presence at Carrow House after 31 May 2024 and post sent there after that date may not reach us.

**3. Payments and day-to-day management**

Our bank details are unchanged and rent and other payments should continue to be made to the existing account. Day-to-day management of Merrow Arcade remains with Quarrington Lake Property Management, 2nd Floor, 41 Cheyne Parade, Bath BA1 1HS (01632 960587), to whom all service charge, insurance and maintenance queries should continue to be addressed.

Please pass a copy of this letter to your property and legal teams and update your records accordingly. If you have any questions, please contact me on the number above.
