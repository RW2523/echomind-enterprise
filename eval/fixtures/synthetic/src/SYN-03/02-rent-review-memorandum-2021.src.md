# MEMORANDUM OF RENT REVIEW

Premises: Unit 4, Merrow Arcade, 18-24 Kingsgate Street, Bath BA1 2JX

Lease: dated 24 June 2016 and made between (1) Carrow Hill Estates Limited and (2) Ashdown & Pryce Retail Limited for a term of 15 years from and including 24 June 2016, as varied by a Deed of Variation dated 15 October 2020 ("the Lease")

Landlord: CARROW HILL ESTATES LIMITED (company number 05928814) of Carrow House, 11 Ashgrove Terrace, Bath BA2 6LP

Tenant: ASHDOWN & PRYCE RETAIL LIMITED (company number 07419385) of Pryce House, 6 Fennimore Way, Reading RG1 8TQ

Review Date: 24 June 2021

1. The Landlord and the Tenant record, in accordance with paragraph 7 of Schedule 5 to the Lease, that the Annual Rent payable under the Lease with effect from and including the Review Date of 24 June 2021 has been agreed at NINETY FOUR THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY POUNDS (£94,250) per annum exclusive of VAT, being the Open Market Rent agreed between the parties' surveyors.

2. The Annual Rent is payable by equal quarterly instalments of £23,562.50 in advance on the usual quarter days.

3. The Tenant has paid the instalments of the Annual Rent due on 24 June 2021, 29 September 2021 and 25 December 2021 at the rate payable before the Review Date, namely £21,625.00 per quarter. The Balancing Sum under paragraph 6.2 of Schedule 5 to the Lease is therefore:

| Quarter day | Paid | Due at revised rent | Shortfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 June 2021 | £21,625.00 | £23,562.50 | £1,937.50 |
| 29 September 2021 | £21,625.00 | £23,562.50 | £1,937.50 |
| 25 December 2021 | £21,625.00 | £23,562.50 | £1,937.50 |
| Total | | | £5,812.50 |

4. The Tenant shall pay the Balancing Sum of £5,812.50 to the Landlord within fourteen days of the date of this memorandum. Provided that it is so paid, the Landlord waives the interest which would otherwise be payable on the Balancing Sum under paragraph 6.2 of Schedule 5 to the Lease.

5. The next Review Date under the Lease is 24 June 2026.

6. This memorandum does not affect the Tenant's option to determine the Lease on 24 June 2026 under clause 9 of the Lease (as varied), and the Open Market Rent agreed for the Review Date does not bind either party on the next Review Date.

7. Save as recorded in this memorandum the Lease continues in full force and effect. This memorandum is signed in duplicate, one part to be retained by each party, and each party bears its own costs.

Dated 11 January 2022

Signed for and on behalf of CARROW HILL ESTATES LIMITED
