# LICENCE FOR ALTERATIONS

relating to Unit 4, Merrow Arcade, 18-24 Kingsgate Street, Bath BA1 2JX

THIS LICENCE is dated 7 March 2022

PARTIES

(1) CARROW HILL ESTATES LIMITED (company number 05928814) whose registered office is at Carrow House, 11 Ashgrove Terrace, Bath BA2 6LP ("the Landlord"); and

(2) ASHDOWN & PRYCE RETAIL LIMITED (company number 07419385) whose registered office is at Pryce House, 6 Fennimore Way, Reading RG1 8TQ ("the Tenant").

BACKGROUND

(A) This Licence is supplemental to the Lease.

(B) The Landlord is entitled to the reversion immediately expectant on the term granted by the Lease and the Tenant is entitled to the residue of that term.

(C) Under paragraphs 7.1 and 7.2 of Schedule 3 to the Lease the Tenant may not alter the shopfront of the Premises or install a mezzanine floor without the consent of the Landlord.

(D) The Tenant has applied for consent to carry out the Works and the Landlord has agreed to give its consent on the terms of this Licence.

AGREED TERMS

<<<NUMBERING ON>>>

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Licence the following definitions apply:

"Approved Plans" means drawings AP-MA4-101 revision C (shopfront elevation and section), AP-MA4-102 revision B (mezzanine general arrangement) and AP-MA4-103 revision A (mezzanine sections and staircase) prepared by Fenwright Design Ltd, together with the specification dated 14 January 2022, copies of which are annexed to this Licence;

"Consents" means all planning permissions, listed building and conservation area consents (if required), advertisement consents, building regulations approvals and other consents, licences and approvals required by law for the Works;

"end of the Term" means the end of the Term (as defined in the Lease) however it ends, including by determination under clause 9 of the Lease, by effluxion of time, by forfeiture or by surrender;

"the Lease" means the lease of the Premises dated 24 June 2016 and made between (1) the Landlord and (2) the Tenant, as varied by a deed of variation dated 15 October 2020, and includes any document supplemental to it;

"Pre-Works Layout" means the layout and finishes of the Premises before the Works, as shown on drawing AP-MA4-100 (existing layout) annexed to this Licence;

"the Works" means the Shopfront Works and the Mezzanine Works described in the Schedule; and

"Works Period" means the period of six months from the date of this Licence.

1.2 Words and expressions defined in the Lease have the same meanings in this Licence unless the context requires otherwise.

1.3 References to clauses and to the Schedule are to clauses of and the Schedule to this Licence unless stated otherwise.

2. LICENCE

2.1 In consideration of the Tenant's covenants in this Licence, the Landlord grants the Tenant consent to carry out the Works in accordance with the Approved Plans and the terms of this Licence.

2.2 This Licence does not authorise any works other than the Works, nor any variation of the Works from the Approved Plans without the prior written consent of the Landlord (not to be unreasonably withheld for minor variations).

2.3 The consent in this Licence is limited to the consent of the Landlord as landlord and does not imply that any Consent will be granted.

3. CONSENTS

3.1 Before starting the Works the Tenant shall obtain all Consents and give copies of them to the Landlord.

3.2 The Tenant shall not apply for any Consent without first giving the Landlord a copy of the proposed application, and shall comply with all conditions of every Consent.

4. CARRYING OUT THE WORKS

4.1 The Tenant shall carry out the Works:

(a) in a good and workmanlike manner, using good quality materials and in accordance with the Approved Plans and the Consents;

(b) using contractors approved by the Landlord (such approval not to be unreasonably withheld);

(c) so as to cause as little disturbance as reasonably possible to the Landlord and to the other tenants and occupiers of the Centre and their customers; and

(d) outside the opening hours of the Centre in the case of any noisy works or works affecting the malls.

4.2 The Tenant shall complete the Works within the Works Period.

4.3 The Tenant shall be the client for the purposes of the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 and shall comply with them.

4.4 The Tenant shall permit the Landlord and the Landlord's Surveyor to enter the Premises to inspect the progress of the Works on reasonable notice.

4.5 The Tenant shall notify the Landlord in writing within five Working Days after the Works are completed.

5. TENANT'S FURTHER COVENANTS

5.1 The Tenant shall indemnify the Landlord against all liabilities, costs, claims and demands arising from the carrying out of the Works or the presence of the Works in the Premises.

5.2 The Tenant shall ensure that its contractors maintain contractors' all risks insurance and public liability insurance of not less than £5,000,000 for any one event during the carrying out of the Works.

5.3 The Tenant shall make good to the reasonable satisfaction of the Landlord any damage to the Retained Parts, the Common Parts or any other part of the Centre caused by the carrying out of the Works.

5.4 The Tenant shall pay on demand any increase in the premium for the insurance of the Centre attributable to the Works.

5.5 Within one month after completion of the Works the Tenant shall give the Landlord a complete set of as-built drawings of the Works and a copy of the health and safety file.

6. REINSTATEMENT

6.1 If the Landlord so requires by written notice given to the Tenant not less than six months before the end of the Term, the Tenant shall before the end of the Term remove the Works (or such part of them as the notice specifies) and reinstate the Premises to the Pre-Works Layout, making good all damage caused by the removal, to the reasonable satisfaction of the Landlord.

6.2 If the Landlord does not give notice under clause 6.1, the Works shall remain in the Premises at the end of the Term and the Tenant shall yield them up in the state of repair and condition required by the Lease.

6.3 Any reinstatement works shall be carried out in accordance with the covenants in the Lease and with all Consents required for them.

7. RENT REVIEW

7.1 The Works shall be treated as improvements carried out by the Tenant otherwise than in pursuance of an obligation to the Landlord, and shall be disregarded on any review of the Annual Rent under Schedule 5 to the Lease.

8. LEASE COVENANTS

8.1 From completion of the Works, the Works shall form part of the Premises for the purposes of the Tenant's covenants in the Lease (including the covenants to repair and decorate in paragraphs 4 and 5 of Schedule 3 to the Lease).

8.2 The covenants in this Licence are enforceable as if they were contained in the Lease, and any breach of them shall be a breach of the Tenant's covenants in the Lease.

8.3 Save as varied by this Licence, the Lease remains in full force and effect, and nothing in this Licence waives any breach of the Tenant's covenants in the Lease.

9. COSTS

9.1 The Tenant shall pay on the date of this Licence the Landlord's legal and surveyors' costs in connection with the application for consent and this Licence, agreed at £2,350 plus VAT.

10. THIRD PARTY RIGHTS

10.1 A person who is not a party to this Licence shall have no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any of its terms.

11. GOVERNING LAW

11.1 This Licence and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by the law of England and Wales.

## SCHEDULE - THE WORKS

### Part 1 - Shopfront Works

1. Removal of the existing timber-framed shopfront, stallriser and fascia installed at the grant of the Lease.

2. Installation of a new shopfront comprising a polyester powder-coated aluminium frame (colour RAL 7022 matt), full-height toughened and laminated glazing, a pair of automatic sliding doors with presence sensors, and a new stallriser in honed limestone, all as shown on drawing AP-MA4-101 revision C.

3. Installation of a new fascia with individually mounted letters and a projecting sign, each internally illuminated by LED, subject to advertisement consent.

4. Associated electrical works to the shopfront and the sliding doors, connected to the Tenant's distribution board.

### Part 2 - Mezzanine Works

1. Installation of a demountable steel-framed mezzanine floor of approximately 68 square metres at the rear of the sales floor, independent of the structure of the Centre and bearing on its own column pad foundations set into the floor screed, as shown on drawings AP-MA4-102 revision B and AP-MA4-103 revision A.

2. Installation of a steel staircase with timber treads and a glazed balustrade to the open edge of the mezzanine.

3. Extension of the Tenant's lighting and small power circuits to the mezzanine and the underside of the mezzanine deck.

4. Extension of the sprinkler system serving the Premises below the mezzanine deck, carried out by the Landlord's approved sprinkler contractor, and alteration of the fire alarm and emergency lighting to suit.

5. Making good of all finishes affected by the Works.
