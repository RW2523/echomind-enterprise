Scope

This statement applies to Rosemary Seasoning Blend RS-440 blended and packed at the Prairie Mills facility in Hutchinson, Kansas, and supplied to the customer named above in lined totes. It covers the nine major food allergens recognized under U.S. law.

Statement

This product does not contain sesame. This product is manufactured in a facility that also processes sesame, milk and soy.

No major food allergen is an ingredient of this product, and, based on the documentation provided by our raw material suppliers, no ingredient of this product, including the natural flavor, is derived from a major food allergen.

Allergen controls

Prairie Mills maintains a written allergen control program at its Hutchinson facility. The program includes production scheduling, segregated storage of allergen-containing raw materials, color-coded tools, and cleaning between production runs. Finished product is not routinely tested for allergens.

Use of this statement

This statement is based on the information available to Prairie Mills on the date of issue, including supplier documentation for each raw material. It is provided to assist the customer in its own allergen management and labeling decisions. It is not a certificate of analysis and does not replace the customer's own supplier verification. Prairie Mills will notify the customer in writing of any change that affects the allergen information in this statement.
