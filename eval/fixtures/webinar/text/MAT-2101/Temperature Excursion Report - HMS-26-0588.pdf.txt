3. Logger data

Source: in-load continuous data logger travelling with the Shipment (placed by Brennan), downloaded at the Newark DC on June 6, 2026 and reviewed by QA on June 7, 2026.

Finding: the logger record shows the Goods above 8 °C for a continuous period of 5 hours 40 minutes on June 2, 2026.

Peak temperature recorded: 11.4 °C.

This is an Excursion as defined in the carrier agreement (more than 30 consecutive minutes outside 2 °C to 8 °C).

4. Actions taken

June 6, 2026: all 22 pallets placed in quarantine (location Q-3, locked cold cage) by E. Ruiz. QA notified at 9:55 a.m.

June 7, 2026: logger record reviewed by T. Akers. Excursion confirmed.

June 8-9, 2026: manufacturers' stability guidance requested and reviewed for each product line. The guidance does not support release of product exposed to this Excursion for distribution to hospitals and pharmacies.

June 9, 2026: Legal (P. Lowell) informed; notice of claim sent to Brennan.

5. Disposition

Decision: REJECT - DESTROY ALL 22 PALLETS.

The product may not be released, returned to stock or sold. Destruction to be carried out through Hollis's licensed pharmaceutical waste vendor. Product to remain in quarantine at Q-3 until Legal releases it for destruction, so that the carrier may inspect it if requested.

6. Root cause (carrier)

To be confirmed by Brennan. Brennan's dispatch told Receiving on June 3, 2026 that the load had a "reefer problem". The Shipment arrived four days after its Pickup Time on a different trailer from the one on the advance shipping notice.
