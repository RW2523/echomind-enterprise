Re: Offer of Employment - Respiratory Therapist II, Lakeshore Medical Center

Dear Ms. Ortega:

On behalf of Ridgeline Health Partners, Inc. ("Ridgeline"), I am pleased to offer you the position of Respiratory Therapist II in the Respiratory Care Department at Lakeshore Medical Center. This letter sets out the principal terms of our offer.

Position and reporting. You will report to me as Director, Respiratory Care. You will work a rotation of day and night shifts set by the department schedule. Your duties are described in the enclosed job description and include ventilator management, airway care, arterial blood gas sampling, and rapid response and code team coverage.

Start date. Your first day of employment will be Monday, February 6, 2023, beginning with Ridgeline's two-day new-hire orientation.

Pay and classification. You will be paid $38.00 per hour, less applicable withholdings and deductions, on Ridgeline's biweekly payroll. Your position is classified as non-exempt under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act and the Illinois Minimum Wage Law. This means that you are eligible for overtime at one and one-half times (1.5x) your regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek. Ridgeline's workweek begins at 12:00 a.m. on Sunday. You must record all time worked accurately in the Kronos timekeeping system, and you may not work "off the clock" for any reason.

On-call shifts. As a Respiratory Therapist II, you will be eligible for on-call shifts under Ridgeline's On-Call Compensation Policy HR-214, as in effect from time to time. That policy sets the on-call stipends, the response standards that apply while you are on call, and how call-back time is paid. A copy of the current policy is enclosed.

Benefits. You will be eligible to enroll in Ridgeline's medical, dental and vision plans effective the first day of the month following your start date, and in Ridgeline's retirement savings plan in accordance with its terms. You will accrue paid time off under Ridgeline's PTO policy for non-exempt staff.

Conditions of employment. This offer is contingent on (a) your holding an active, unrestricted Illinois respiratory care practitioner license and the NBRC Registered Respiratory Therapist (RRT) credential; (b) satisfactory completion of a background check and pre-employment health screening, conducted in accordance with applicable law; and (c) your providing documents that establish your identity and authorization to work in the United States within three (3) business days after your start date.

At-will employment. Your employment with Ridgeline is at will. Either you or Ridgeline may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. Nothing in this letter or in any Ridgeline policy creates a contract of employment for any particular period. The at-will nature of your employment may be changed only by a written agreement signed by Ridgeline's Chief People Officer.

Entire offer. This letter sets out the terms of our offer and supersedes any earlier discussions. Ridgeline's policies, including Policy HR-214, may be revised from time to time in accordance with their terms.

To accept this offer, please sign and date this letter where indicated below and return it to me no later than Friday, January 27, 2023.

We are delighted at the prospect of your joining the Lakeshore respiratory care team. Please call me at (312) 555-0143 or email vesposito@ridgelinehealth.example with any questions.
