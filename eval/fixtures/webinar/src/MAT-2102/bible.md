## 2. The documents' facts

**Offer letter** (January 23, 2023, from Victor Esposito to Maria Ortega): position Respiratory Therapist II;
start date February 6, 2023; **$38.00 per hour, non-exempt (eligible for overtime at 1.5x for hours over 40 in a
workweek)**; eligible for on-call shifts under policy HR-214; at-will employment; signed by Ortega January 25, 2023.

**On-Call Compensation Policy HR-214** (revision 3, effective **January 1, 2024**, approved by the Chief People
Officer):
- 3.1 Weeknight on-call shift = 6:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m., Monday–Thursday nights. **Stipend $95.00 per weeknight.**
- 3.2 Weekend/holiday on-call day = 24 hours from 7:00 a.m. **Stipend $240.00 per weekend or holiday day.**
- 3.3 While on call, the employee must answer a page within ten (10) minutes and be able to report within forty-five
  (45) minutes; otherwise free to use the time for their own purposes (sleep at home, family, errands within range).
- 4.1 Call-back: time actually worked when called in is paid at the regular rate, and at one and one-half times the
  regular rate for hours over forty (40) in the workweek; **minimum two (2) hours per call-back.**
- 4.2 The stipend is paid in addition to call-back pay.
- 5.1 On-call shifts are recorded in the timekeeping system with code ONC-WN (weeknight) or ONC-WE (weekend/holiday).

**Demand letter** (October 6, 2026, Daniel Gold of Reyes & Gold to Andrea Lim):
- Claims that between October 1, 2023 and September 30, 2026 Ms. Ortega was on call **214 weeknights** and **31
  weekend days**; that on-call time is compensable "engaged to wait" time under the FLSA and the Illinois Minimum Wage
  Law.
- Claims "**$41,200.00 in on-call stipends calculated at Ridgeline's own policy rates**" (this figure is WRONG: at the
  policy rates 214 × $95.00 = $20,330.00 and 31 × $240.00 = $7,440.00, total **$27,770.00**).
- Claims unpaid overtime on **126 call-back hours at $57.00 per hour = $7,182.00** (this one is arithmetically right:
  1.5 × $38.00 = $57.00).
- Claims liquidated damages equal to the unpaid amounts and attorneys' fees. **Total demand $96,764.00** (= 2 ×
  ($41,200.00 + $7,182.00)). Asks for a response by **November 5, 2026**, and proposes a tolling agreement.

**Timesheet summary** (Kronos export, prepared by Payroll on October 15, 2026): by quarter, Q4 2023 to Q3 2026, the
on-call shifts recorded for Ortega: weeknights total **214**, weekend days total **31**, call-back hours total
**126**. (Make a table of 12 quarters whose columns add up to these totals.)

**Payroll register extract** (same period, by quarter): weeknight stipends PAID for **198** nights ($18,810.00),
weekend stipends paid for **31** days ($7,440.00), total stipends paid **$26,250.00**; call-back hours paid **126**
(88 hours at 1.5x = $5,016.00; 38 hours at the regular rate = $1,444.00, those were weeks under 40 hours). Q2 2025
shows 4 weeknights paid against 20 recorded (the gap of 16).

**HR investigation memo** (October 20, 2026, Janet Kowalski to Andrea Lim, privileged, at counsel's request):
- **16 weeknight on-call shifts between April 6 and June 28, 2025 were not paid** because the ONC-WN code was missing
  from the Lakeshore respiratory-care schedule template after a system upgrade on April 3, 2025; fixed July 1, 2025.
  Unpaid stipends **16 × $95.00 = $1,520.00**. Eleven other employees affected (not Ortega's claim).
- Ortega averaged 0.6 call-backs per on-call night; she was free to stay at home, sleep and handle personal matters;
  no complaints before the demand letter.
- Recommends paying $1,520.00 plus interest to Ortega and the eleven others' unpaid amounts, and auditing all
  schedule templates.

**Tolling agreement** (signed **October 28, 2026** by Maria Ortega, Daniel Gold and Andrea Lim for Ridgeline): tolls
all limitations periods for Ortega's wage claims from **October 6, 2026 through January 31, 2027**; either party may end
it earlier on **fifteen (15) days' written notice**; no admission of liability; Illinois law.

## 3. Planted issues (what the demo shows)

1. **The claimed stipend figure does not hold at the policy rates:** 214 × $95 + 31 × $240 = **$27,770.00**, not $41,200.00.
2. **What was actually unpaid:** recorded 214 vs paid 198 weeknights → 16 unpaid = **$1,520.00** (the HR memo explains why).
3. The overtime figure ($7,182.00 for 126 hours at $57.00) is arithmetically consistent with the $38.00 regular rate,
   but 38 of those hours fell in weeks under 40 hours (payroll register).
