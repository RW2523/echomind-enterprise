1. PURPOSE

1.1 This policy explains when Ridgeline Health Partners, Inc. ("Ridgeline") may schedule employees to be on call, the conditions that apply while an employee is on call, and how on-call time and call-back work are paid.

1.2 The policy is intended to ensure that patient care departments have qualified staff available outside regular hours, that employees who take on-call shifts are paid accurately and consistently, and that Ridgeline complies with the Fair Labor Standards Act ("FLSA"), the Illinois Minimum Wage Law and the Illinois Wage Payment and Collection Act.

2. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

2.1 This policy applies to non-exempt employees in departments that maintain an on-call schedule, including Respiratory Care, Surgical Services, Imaging, Cardiac Catheterization and Laboratory Services. It does not apply to exempt employees, to physicians and advanced practice providers, or to agency and traveler staff.

2.2 In this policy:

(a) "On call" means a period during which an employee is not working but has agreed to be available to answer a page and, if needed, to return to work.

(b) "Weeknight on-call shift" has the meaning given in section 3.1.

(c) "Weekend or holiday on-call day" has the meaning given in section 3.2.

(d) "Call-back" means an occasion on which an employee who is on call is directed to report to the facility, or to log in remotely where the department permits remote work, to perform work.

(e) "Recognized holiday" means New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day or Christmas Day.

(f) "Workweek" means the fixed period of seven consecutive days beginning at 12:00 a.m. on Sunday.

(g) "Kronos" means Ridgeline's timekeeping and scheduling system, and "schedule template" means the department schedule pattern in Kronos from which shifts, and the pay codes attached to them, are generated.

3. ON-CALL SHIFTS AND STIPENDS

3.1 A weeknight on-call shift runs from 6:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday nights. An employee scheduled for a weeknight on-call shift receives a stipend of $95.00 per weeknight. Friday night coverage is provided by in-house staff under each department's staffing plan and is not an on-call shift under this policy.

3.2 A weekend or holiday on-call day is a period of twenty-four (24) hours beginning at 7:00 a.m. on a Saturday, a Sunday or a recognized holiday. An employee scheduled for a weekend or holiday on-call day receives a stipend of $240.00 per weekend or holiday day.

3.3 While on call, an employee must answer a page within ten (10) minutes and must be able to report to the facility within forty-five (45) minutes of being called back. Subject to those two requirements, the employee is otherwise free to use on-call time for his or her own purposes, for example to sleep at home, to spend time with family, or to run errands within range of the facility. An employee who is on call is not required to remain on Ridgeline premises, to wear a uniform or to perform any work unless called back.

3.4 Department directors publish on-call schedules at least four (4) weeks in advance. Employees may trade on-call shifts with a qualified colleague with the approval of the charge therapist or supervisor. The employee who takes the shift receives the stipend.

3.5 An employee will not be scheduled for more than eight (8) on-call shifts in a four-week schedule period without his or her agreement.

3.6 The stipend is paid for the employee's availability. It is paid for each scheduled on-call shift whether or not the employee is called back.

3.7 An employee who does not meet the response standards in section 3.3 may forfeit the stipend for that shift and may be subject to corrective action under Policy HR-305.

4. CALL-BACK PAY

4.1 Time actually worked by an employee who is called back is paid at the employee's regular rate, and at one and one-half (1.5) times the regular rate for hours worked over forty (40) in the workweek. Each call-back is paid for a minimum of two (2) hours, even if the work takes less time. Call-back time begins when the employee arrives at the facility (or logs in remotely) and ends when the employee leaves (or logs off).

4.2 The on-call stipend is paid in addition to call-back pay. A call-back does not reduce or replace the stipend for the shift.

4.3 If a call-back continues into the employee's next scheduled shift, the time from the start of the scheduled shift is paid as part of that shift and not as call-back time.

4.4 A second page received while the employee is still at the facility on a call-back is part of the same call-back.

5. TIMEKEEPING AND PAYROLL

5.1 On-call shifts are recorded in Kronos with code ONC-WN for a weeknight on-call shift or ONC-WE for a weekend or holiday on-call day. The code is attached to the shift by the department's schedule template, and the stipend is generated for payroll from that code.

5.2 An employee who is called back must punch in and out at a Kronos terminal, or record the time in the Kronos mobile application when working remotely, using code CB. Each call-back is recorded separately.

5.3 Employees must review their timecards each pay period and report any missing or incorrect on-call or call-back entry to their supervisor or to Payroll as soon as they become aware of it.

5.4 Department directors, or their delegates, must approve timecards, including on-call and call-back entries, by the payroll deadline for each biweekly pay period. Payroll pays stipends and call-back pay in the pay period in which they are approved.

5.5 Payroll corrects any underpayment in the next regular payroll after it is identified. Ridgeline will not make any deduction from wages to recover an overpayment except as permitted by the Illinois Wage Payment and Collection Act.

6. RESPONSIBILITIES

6.1 Department directors maintain on-call schedules, make sure that their schedule templates carry the correct on-call codes, and approve timecards.

6.2 Employees meet the response standards in section 3.3, record call-back time accurately, and review their timecards.

6.3 Payroll processes stipends and call-back pay from approved timecards and answers employees' pay questions.

6.4 Human Resources interprets this policy, approves exceptions under section 7 and reviews the policy at least every three (3) years.

6.5 Information Services maintains the schedule templates and pay code mappings in Kronos and must test them, with Payroll, after any upgrade or change to Kronos and before the first schedule period built on the changed system.

7. EXCEPTIONS

7.1 An exception to this policy requires the written approval of the Chief People Officer, or a delegate, and of the vice president responsible for the department. No exception may reduce an employee's pay below the amount required by law.

8. QUESTIONS

8.1 Questions about this policy should be directed to the employee's HR Business Partner or to Payroll.

9. APPROVAL

This revision 3 of Policy HR-214 was approved by the Chief People Officer on December 11, 2023 and takes effect on January 1, 2024. It replaces revision 2 in full.
