# TOLLING AGREEMENT

This Tolling Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of October 28, 2026 by and between MARIA ORTEGA ("Ms. Ortega") and RIDGELINE HEALTH PARTNERS, INC., an Illinois corporation ("Ridgeline"). Ms. Ortega and Ridgeline are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## RECITALS

A. Ms. Ortega is employed by Ridgeline as a Respiratory Therapist II at Lakeshore Medical Center, Chicago, Illinois.

B. By letter dated October 6, 2026 from her counsel, Reyes & Gold LLC (the "Demand Letter"), Ms. Ortega asserted claims against Ridgeline for unpaid wages, overtime, liquidated damages and related relief arising from her on-call shifts and call-back work, and proposed that the Parties enter into a tolling agreement.

C. Ridgeline denies the claims and denies any liability to Ms. Ortega.

D. The Parties wish to discuss whether the claims can be resolved without litigation, and to avoid the need for Ms. Ortega to file suit during those discussions solely to preserve her claims.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Tolled Claims" means all claims that Ms. Ortega has or may have against Ridgeline, its parents, subsidiaries, affiliates, officers, directors and employees for wages, on-call pay or stipends, overtime, call-back pay, liquidated damages, statutory damages, penalties, interest, attorneys' fees or costs, whether under the Fair Labor Standards Act, the Illinois Minimum Wage Law, the Illinois Wage Payment and Collection Act, any other federal, state or local law, or contract, arising out of or relating to her compensation for on-call shifts or call-back work during her employment with Ridgeline (together, her "wage claims").

1.2 "Tolling Period" means the period from October 6, 2026 through January 31, 2027, inclusive, unless it is ended earlier under section 3.2.

2. TOLLING OF LIMITATIONS PERIODS

2.1 All statutes of limitations, statutes of repose and other time-based defenses, including laches, that apply to the Tolled Claims are tolled and suspended during the Tolling Period. The Tolling Period will not be counted in determining whether any Tolled Claim, or any part of a Tolled Claim, is timely.

2.2 Without limiting section 2.1, the Parties agree that the Tolling Period will be excluded in calculating the two-year and three-year periods under 29 U.S.C. § 255(a) and the three-year period under the Illinois Minimum Wage Law for each of Ms. Ortega's wage claims.

2.3 This Agreement does not revive any claim that was already time-barred on October 6, 2026.

2.4 Ms. Ortega agrees not to file any lawsuit, arbitration or administrative charge asserting a Tolled Claim during the Tolling Period.

3. TERM AND EARLY TERMINATION

3.1 Unless the Parties extend it by a written agreement signed by both of them, the Tolling Period ends at 11:59 p.m. Central Time on January 31, 2027.

3.2 Either Party may end the Tolling Period earlier by giving the other Party fifteen (15) days' written notice. If a Party gives notice, the Tolling Period ends at 11:59 p.m. Central Time on the fifteenth (15th) day after the day on which the notice is given.

3.3 A notice under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by email and by overnight courier:

(a) to Ms. Ortega, care of Daniel Gold, Reyes & Gold LLC, 233 South Wacker Drive, Suite 4100, Chicago, IL 60606 (dgold@reyesgold.example); and

(b) to Ridgeline, attention Andrea Lim, Vice President & Associate General Counsel, Ridgeline Health Partners, Inc., 1900 West Belmont Avenue, Chicago, IL 60657 (alim@ridgelinehealth.example), with a copy, which does not constitute notice, to Sofia Marchetti, Calder & Wynne LLP, 70 West Madison Street, Suite 3100, Chicago, IL 60602 (smarchetti@calderwynne.example).

A notice is given on the day it is sent by email, provided that a copy is sent by overnight courier on the same day.

4. NO ADMISSION

4.1 This Agreement is not an admission by Ridgeline of any liability, wrongdoing or violation of law, or of the validity of any claim or of any amount claimed in the Demand Letter. It is not an admission by Ms. Ortega that any of her claims lacks merit.

4.2 Neither this Agreement nor its negotiation may be offered or used as evidence in any proceeding, except to enforce this Agreement or to establish that the Tolled Claims were tolled. The Parties' settlement discussions are subject to Federal Rule of Evidence 408 and Illinois Rule of Evidence 408.

5. SCOPE AND RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

5.1 This Agreement applies only to Ms. Ortega's individual claims. It does not toll the claims of any other current or former employee of Ridgeline, and it is not consent by Ridgeline to, or a waiver of any defense to, any collective or class action.

5.2 Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, nothing in it waives or limits any right, claim or defense of either Party, all of which are reserved.

5.3 Ridgeline will not retaliate against Ms. Ortega for asserting the Tolled Claims or for entering into this Agreement.

6. GENERAL

6.1 This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

6.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement of the Parties about its subject matter. It may be amended or extended only by a written agreement signed by both Parties.

6.3 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original. A signature delivered by email in PDF form is effective as an original signature.

6.4 The person signing on behalf of Ridgeline represents that she is authorized to do so. Ms. Ortega acknowledges that she has been represented by counsel of her choice, Reyes & Gold LLC, in negotiating this Agreement.
