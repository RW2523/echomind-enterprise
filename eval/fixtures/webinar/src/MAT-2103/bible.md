## 2. Economics (all documents agree unless noted)

- Series A amount **$12,000,000**; **pre-money valuation $48,000,000** on a fully diluted basis, including an
  unallocated option pool equal to **12% of the post-money** fully diluted capitalization; **post-money $60,000,000**.
- **Original Issue Price $1.20 per share**; **10,000,000** shares of Series A Preferred Stock.
- Fully diluted shares: pre-money **40,000,000**; post-money **50,000,000**; Series A = 20% post-money.
- Pre-money cap table: Priya Raman 15,000,000 common; Owen Fitzgerald 11,000,000 common; Series Seed Preferred
  5,600,000 (Juniper Seed Partners 4,000,000; other seed holders 1,600,000); options granted 2,400,000; unallocated pool
  6,000,000 (after a 3,200,000-share increase approved for this financing). Total 40,000,000.
- **Liquidation preference: 1x the Original Issue Price, non-participating**; dividends 6% non-cumulative when and if
  declared; conversion 1:1; broad-based weighted-average anti-dilution.
- **Board: five (5) directors** — two elected by common (Priya Raman, Owen Fitzgerald), one designated by Granite Peak
  (Rafael Mendes), one designated by Halcyon (Simone Archer), one independent director acceptable to the others.
- **Major Investor** (IRA): holder of at least **1,000,000** shares of Series A — so Juniper (833,333) is NOT a Major
  Investor; Granite Peak and Halcyon are.
