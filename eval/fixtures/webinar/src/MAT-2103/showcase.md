## 1. The economics, and Granite Peak's stake

**Question:** "Walk me through the economics of the Northlake Series A. What does Granite Peak end up owning?"

**A correct answer contains:**
- $12,000,000 for 10,000,000 shares of Series A Preferred at an Original Issue Price of $1.20 per share.
- Pre-money $48,000,000 fully diluted (40,000,000 shares), including an unallocated pool of 12% of the post-money
  (6,000,000 shares, after a 3,200,000-share increase); post-money $60,000,000 (50,000,000 shares); Series A = 20%.
- Granite Peak: 6,666,667 shares = 13.33% fully diluted post-money (Halcyon 2,500,000 = 5.00%; Juniper 833,333 = 1.67%,
  9.67% with its 4,000,000 Series Seed shares).
- 1x non-participating liquidation preference; 6% non-cumulative dividends only if declared.

**Cite:** Series A term sheet (Price Per Share; Fully Diluted Basis; Liquidation Preference); Capitalization table
(November 9, 2026), tables 1 and 3; SPA Draft 3, Schedule A.

## 2. Counsel fee cap: term sheet against SPA

**Question:** "How much of Pell Strand's fees has Northlake agreed to pay? Do the term sheet and the SPA draft agree?"

**A correct answer contains:**
- They do not agree. The signed term sheet: the Company pays one investor counsel's reasonable fees and expenses at
  the Closing **up to $50,000** (and the Expenses section is one of the binding provisions).
- SPA Draft 3, Section 6.9: **not to exceed $45,000**, payable at the Initial Closing.
- Once signed, the SPA supersedes the term sheet (6.14); expect Pell Strand to ask for $50,000. Raise it with the client.

**Cite:** Series A term sheet (Expenses); SPA Draft 3, Section 6.9 (and 6.14).

## 3. Closing timing: December 15 or December 31?

**Question:** "Rafael wants to close by December 15. Does our SPA draft line up with that?"

**A correct answer contains:**
- Term sheet: "Closing expected on or before December 15, 2026."
- SPA Draft 3, Section 6.4: the Company or the Lead Investor may terminate if the Initial Closing has not occurred by
  **December 31, 2026** - a later date.
- Rafael Mendes's email of November 13, 2026 asks to keep the December 15 target and to work back from it; the actual
  closing date is not yet fixed.
- Bonus: the same email asks for a board observer seat for Daniel Kwan, which is not in the term sheet or the drafts.

**Cite:** Series A term sheet (Closing); SPA Draft 3, Section 6.4; Email from Granite Peak (November 13, 2026).

## 4. Open closing conditions

**Question:** "Which conditions to the Initial Closing are still open, and who is on each?"

**A correct answer contains:**
- PIIAs (SPA 4.7): 7 of 9 employees signed; the 2 new hires who started November 2 still to sign (Company, Laura Chen).
- Stockholder consent: circulating (C&W); the Restated Certificate (SPA 4.2) is in final form but can be filed in
  Delaware only on closing day, after the stockholder consent.

- Wire instructions: call-back verification pending (Company).
- Done or settled: board consent signed November 12; Management Rights Letter in agreed form (SPA 4.6); no legal opinion
  required. IRA, Voting and ROFR/Co-Sale (SPA 4.3) are still drafts.

**Cite:** SPA Draft 3, Section 4; Closing checklist (as of November 13, 2026); Disclosure Schedule 2.11.1; Board consent
(November 12, 2026).

## 5. Definitions check on the SPA

**Question:** "Run a definitions check on SPA Draft 3 before it goes to the client."

**A correct answer contains:**
- "Knowledge" is defined twice, inconsistently: Section 1.3 - actual knowledge of Priya Raman, Owen Fitzgerald and
  Laura Chen, **after reasonable inquiry**; Section 2 lead-in - actual knowledge of Priya Raman and Owen Fitzgerald,
  **without inquiry**.
- The Section 2 version governs the representations and is narrower (no Laura Chen, no inquiry); keep one definition.
- Optional follow-up: "Is Juniper a Major Investor?" - No: 833,333 Series A shares against the 1,000,000 threshold
  (IRA 1.8; Series Seed does not count), so no information or pro rata rights.

**Cite:** SPA Draft 3, Section 1.3 and the lead-in to Section 2; (follow-up) Investors' Rights Agreement 1.8 and
Schedule A.
