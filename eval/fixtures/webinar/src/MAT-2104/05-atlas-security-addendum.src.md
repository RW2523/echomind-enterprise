# INFORMATION SECURITY ADDENDUM

Third-Party Vendor Information Security Requirements

Vendor: Brightline Software, Inc.
Agreement: Master Subscription Agreement for the Brightline Lending Cloud service

This Information Security Addendum (this "Addendum") is entered into by Atlas Federal Credit Union ("Atlas") and the vendor named above ("Vendor") and supplements the agreement between them for the products or services named above (the "Agreement"). Vendor agrees to comply with this Addendum for as long as it has access to Atlas Data or Atlas Systems.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 Atlas is a federally insured credit union subject to supervision and examination by the National Credit Union Administration. Atlas is required by applicable law and regulatory guidance to oversee the third parties that provide it with services and to ensure that they protect the security and confidentiality of member information.

1.2 This Addendum sets out the minimum information security requirements that Vendor must meet in providing products or services to Atlas. The requirements apply to Vendor and to each subcontractor of Vendor that has access to Atlas Data.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Atlas Data" means all data and information, in any form, that Vendor or its subcontractors receive, create, store or process on behalf of Atlas, including Member Information.

2.2 "Atlas Systems" means the computer systems, networks, applications and facilities owned or operated by or for Atlas.

2.3 "Member Information" means any record containing nonpublic personal information about a member or prospective member of Atlas, in paper, electronic or any other form.

2.4 "Security Incident" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, modification or destruction of, Atlas Data, and any actual or reasonably suspected compromise of the systems that Vendor uses to store or process Atlas Data.

## 3. Information Security Program

3.1 Vendor shall maintain a comprehensive written information security program that includes administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the services and the sensitivity of Atlas Data, and that is designed to protect against unauthorized access to or use of Atlas Data.

3.2 Vendor shall designate a qualified individual responsible for its information security program, carry out a documented information security risk assessment at least annually, and update its safeguards to address the risks identified.

3.3 Vendor shall conduct background checks, to the extent permitted by law, on all personnel with access to Member Information before granting access, and shall provide security awareness training to all such personnel at least annually.

## 4. Assurance Reports

4.1 Vendor shall obtain, at its own expense, a SOC 2 Type II report annually from an independent certified public accounting firm covering the systems and services used to provide services to Atlas, addressing at least the Security, Availability and Confidentiality trust services criteria.

4.2 Vendor shall deliver each SOC 2 Type II report to Atlas promptly after it is issued and, where the report period ends more than three months before Atlas's request, shall provide a bridge letter covering the gap.

4.3 Vendor shall remediate any exception identified in a SOC 2 Type II report that is relevant to Atlas Data within a reasonable time and shall inform Atlas of its remediation plan.

## 5. Technical Controls

5.1 Encryption in Transit. Vendor shall encrypt all Atlas Data transmitted over any public or untrusted network using Transport Layer Security (TLS) version 1.2 or higher.

5.2 Encryption at Rest. Vendor shall encrypt all Atlas Data at rest, including in databases, file storage and backups, using AES-256 encryption, and shall protect encryption keys using industry-standard key management practices.

5.3 Multi-Factor Authentication. Vendor shall require multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all administrative access to systems that store or process Atlas Data, for all remote access to Vendor's networks, and for all access by Vendor personnel to Atlas Data.

5.4 Access Control. Vendor shall grant access to Atlas Data on the principle of least privilege, review access rights at least quarterly, and revoke the access of any person who no longer requires it promptly and in any event on termination of that person's employment or engagement.

5.5 Logging and Monitoring. Vendor shall log security-relevant events on systems that store or process Atlas Data, monitor those logs for suspicious activity, and protect them against unauthorized alteration.

5.6 Vulnerability Management. Vendor shall scan the systems used to provide services to Atlas for vulnerabilities at least monthly and shall remediate identified vulnerabilities within timeframes appropriate to their severity, consistent with industry standards.

5.7 Penetration Testing. Vendor shall engage a qualified independent third party to perform a penetration test of the applications and infrastructure used to provide services to Atlas at least annually. Vendor shall provide Atlas with a summary of the results of each penetration test, including the number and severity of the findings and the status of their remediation, and shall remediate all critical and high findings promptly.

## 6. Security Incidents

6.1 Vendor shall notify Atlas of any Security Incident within forty-eight (48) hours after Vendor discovers it. Notice shall be given by telephone and by email to Atlas's Chief Information Security Officer at security@atlasfcu.example, with a copy to Atlas's Director, Vendor Management.

6.2 The notice shall describe, to the extent known, the nature and scope of the Security Incident, the Atlas Data affected, the members likely to be affected, and the steps Vendor has taken to contain it. Vendor shall provide updates at least daily until the Security Incident has been contained, and a written root-cause report once its investigation has been completed.

6.3 Vendor shall cooperate fully with Atlas in investigating and responding to any Security Incident, including by preserving relevant logs and evidence, and shall take all steps reasonably necessary to contain and remediate the Security Incident and prevent its recurrence.

6.4 Vendor shall not notify any Atlas member, regulator or other third party of a Security Incident involving Atlas Data without Atlas's prior written consent, unless Vendor is required by law to do so. Where a Security Incident results from Vendor's breach of this Addendum, Vendor shall reimburse Atlas for the reasonable costs of notifying affected members and of providing them with credit monitoring services.

## 7. Right to Audit

7.1 Atlas, its internal and external auditors, and its regulators may audit Vendor's compliance with this Addendum, including by on-site inspection of the facilities, systems, records and personnel used to provide services to Atlas, on reasonable prior written notice and during normal business hours.

7.2 Nothing in this Addendum limits the right of the National Credit Union Administration or any other regulator having jurisdiction over Atlas to examine Vendor's performance of services for Atlas to the extent permitted by law.

## 8. Subcontractors

8.1 Vendor shall not permit any subcontractor to access Atlas Data unless the subcontractor is bound by written obligations no less protective of Atlas Data than this Addendum. Vendor remains fully responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

8.2 On request, Vendor shall provide Atlas with a current list of the subcontractors that have access to Atlas Data.

## 9. Business Continuity

Vendor shall maintain business continuity and disaster recovery plans for the services provided to Atlas, test those plans at least annually, and provide Atlas with a summary of the test results on request.

## 10. Return and Destruction of Atlas Data

10.1 On termination or expiration of the Agreement, or at Atlas's earlier request, Vendor shall return Atlas Data to Atlas in a format reasonably requested by Atlas and shall then securely destroy all Atlas Data in its possession or control, in accordance with recognized media sanitization standards.

10.2 Vendor shall provide Atlas with a written certificate of destruction signed by an officer of Vendor.

## 11. Order of Precedence

If there is any conflict or inconsistency between this Addendum and the Agreement, including any data processing addendum or schedule to the Agreement, the provision that is more protective of Atlas Data and Member Information prevails.

## 12. Survival

Vendor's obligations under this Addendum survive the termination or expiration of the Agreement for as long as Vendor or any of its subcontractors retains any Atlas Data.
