# MAT-2104 - live-demo candidate questions

Brightline Software / Atlas Federal Credit Union - enterprise SaaS agreement (we act for Brightline, the vendor).
Ask in the MAT-2104 matter. "MSA" = "Master Subscription Agreement - Brightline / Atlas" (the product shows the newest
version, Atlas's markup v2; v1 is its earlier version).

## 1. What did Atlas change in the liability provisions?

**Ask:** "What did Atlas change in the limitation of liability section of the MSA?"

A correct answer contains:
- 11.3 data-breach super-cap raised from two times (2x) the annual Subscription Fees ($960,000) to five times (5x)
  ($2,400,000), for breach of Section 8 (Security) or confidentiality involving Customer Data;
- Atlas's comment on 11.3: "Required by NCUA vendor guidance per our CISO.";
- 11.2 general cap unchanged (Subscription Fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the event); 11.1 unchanged;
- good extra: the client's limit is 3x ($1,440,000).

Cite: MSA (v2, 11.3 with the v1 wording as the earlier version); Brightline GC email of November 9, 2026.

## 2. The security-incident notice conflict

**Ask:** "How fast do we have to notify Atlas of a security incident? Do the documents agree?"

A correct answer contains:
- 72 hours after Brightline *confirms* the incident: MSA v1 8.3 and the DPA 5.1 (Personal Information Breach);
- 24 hours after *discovery*: Atlas's markup of the MSA (v2 8.3);
- 48 hours after discovery: Atlas's own Information Security Addendum 6.1;
- the client will give 48 hours (matching Atlas's addendum) but not 24;
- good extra: the precedence clauses pull different ways (DPA 8.1: the DPA prevails on Personal Information; Addendum
  section 11: the provision more protective of Atlas Data prevails).

Cite: MSA (v2 8.3); Data Processing Addendum 5.1; Atlas Information Security Addendum 6.1; Brightline GC email.

## 3. A service-credit calculation

**Ask:** "If Availability comes in at 99.3% in a month, what service credit does Atlas get?"

A correct answer contains:
- 99.3% is in the "< 99.5% but ≥ 99.0%" band of the Schedule 2 table: 10% of the Monthly Fees for that month;
- Monthly Fees = $40,000 (one twelfth of $480,000); credit = **$4,000**;
- within the 25%-of-Monthly-Fees cap; applied against the next invoice;
- good extra: Atlas's markup raises the commitment to 99.95%, but the Schedule 2 table (keyed to 99.9%) was not marked
  up.

Cite: SLA Schedule (Schedule 2) 3.1-3.3; MSA 5.2.

## 4. The client's negotiating limits against Atlas's asks

**Ask:** "Compare what Atlas asked for in its markup with Brightline's negotiating limits."

A correct answer contains (Atlas's markup / Brightline's instructions):
- uptime: 99.95% / acceptable only if service credits are capped at 15% of the Monthly Fees (Schedule 2 caps them at
  25%);
- super-cap: 5x ($2,400,000) / never above 3x ($1,440,000); our form offered 2x ($960,000);
- termination for convenience: after the first 12 months, on 90 days' notice, with a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees
  (10.4) / only after 24 months, on 180 days' notice, with no refund of the current year's fees;
- incident notice: 24 hours from discovery / 48 hours (matching Atlas's own addendum);
- sole-remedy deletion (5.3) and on-site audit right (8.6, 30 days' notice): no instructions yet - the client wants our
  recommendation;
- signature wanted by December 18, 2026 (quarter end).

Cite: MSA (v2 5.1, 5.3, 8.3, 8.6, 10.4, 11.3); Brightline GC email of November 9, 2026; Atlas Information Security
Addendum 6.1.

## 5. The cover email's description against the markup

**Ask:** "Monica Sheridan says Atlas accepted our liability cap as drafted and only tightened the uptime. Is that
accurate?"

A correct answer contains:
- no: 11.2 is unchanged, but the 11.3 super-cap went from 2x ($960,000) to 5x ($2,400,000);
- the markup also makes other changes the email does not mention: credits no longer the sole remedy (5.3), 24-hour
  notice from discovery (8.3), new on-site audit right (8.6), termination for convenience after 12 months (10.4);
- the uptime change itself is as described: 99.9% to 99.95%.

Cite: Email from Atlas (Monica Sheridan), November 6, 2026; MSA (v2 11.3 and the other marked sections).
