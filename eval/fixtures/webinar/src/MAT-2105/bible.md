## 2. Facts

**Warning letter (November 6, 2026)** — states:
- During the inspection, FDA collected finished-product samples of Harvest Grain Crackers, lots HG2608B and HG2609A;
  analysis found **sesame** (14 ppm and 38 ppm).
- The label declares "**Contains: Wheat, Soy**" and does not declare sesame, a major food allergen → the product is
  **misbranded under section 403(w)** of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.
- The firm's food allergen preventive controls did not adequately prevent allergen cross-contact (shared Line 3 with a
  sesame product; changeover cleaning not verified) — significant violations of the preventive-controls requirements.
- **"Please respond in writing within fifteen (15) working days of receipt of this letter"**, describing the specific
  steps taken to correct the violations and prevent recurrence, with documentation; if corrections cannot be completed
  within 15 working days, state the reason and the time within which they will be completed.
- Failure to correct may result in regulatory action without further notice, including seizure and injunction.
- Received by Meadowgate on **November 10, 2026** (stamped on the scanned copy: RECEIVED NOV 10 2026 MEADOWGATE
  REGULATORY).

**QA memo (November 12, 2026, Hannah Brooks to Celeste Moreau, privileged, prepared at the request of counsel):**
- Root cause: Line 3 is shared with "Everything Seed Crisps" (contains sesame). The validated changeover clean (SSOP-L3-
  07) was **skipped on three occasions** (July 22, August 5 and August 19, 2026) because of a staffing shortage on
  second shift.
- **Affected: 9 lots, HG2607A through HG2609C, about 41,800 cases, distributed to retailers in six states (OH, PA, MI,
  IN, KY, WV).** No consumer illness complaints received as of November 12.
- Prairie Mills' seasoning blend certificate says the blend contains no sesame but is produced in a facility that also
  processes sesame — a second possible source, being tested.
- Corrective actions under way: Line 3 dedicated to non-sesame products from November 16; label revised to "Contains:
  Wheat, Soy, Sesame" (print order placed November 11); retraining; cleaning verification by ATP and allergen swab on
  every changeover.
- **Response due by December 1, 2026** ("15 working days from receipt on November 10, counted conservatively without
  excluding the Veterans Day and Thanksgiving holidays").

**Label proof (current label, scan):** ingredients list (enriched wheat flour [wheat flour, niacin, reduced iron,
thiamine mononitrate, riboflavin, folic acid], whole grain oats, sunflower oil, cane sugar, sea salt, rosemary
seasoning [salt, rosemary, garlic powder, natural flavor], soy lecithin, baking soda); **"CONTAINS: WHEAT, SOY."**;
net wt 7 oz (198 g); Distributed by Meadowgate Foods, Inc., Wooster, OH 44691. A handwritten note may appear ("add
SESAME — HB 11/12") but no fact relies on it alone.

**Supplier allergen certificate (Prairie Mills, RS-440, dated March 3, 2026):** "This product does not contain sesame.
This product is manufactured in a facility that also processes sesame, milk and soy." Signed by the QA manager.

**Draft response memo (Calder & Wynne, November 14, 2026, Kenji Arai to Celeste Moreau, privileged):**
- Recommends: (1) a **voluntary recall of the 9 lots** at the retail level, coordinated with FDA (expected Class II —
  state this as counsel's expectation, not a certainty); (2) a written response by **December 1, 2026** with the root
  cause, corrective actions and documentation (revised label, SSOP, training records, swab results); (3) testing of the
  RS-440 blend; (4) a supplier verification update.
- Open points for the client: confirm the case count by lot; confirm whether any product went to food service; decide
  on press release wording.
