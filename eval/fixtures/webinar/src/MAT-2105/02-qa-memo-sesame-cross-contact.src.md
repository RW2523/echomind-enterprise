# MEMORANDUM

**PRIVILEGED & CONFIDENTIAL - ATTORNEY-CLIENT COMMUNICATION**
Prepared at the request of counsel for the purpose of obtaining legal advice. Do not forward.

To: Celeste Moreau, General Counsel
From: Hannah Brooks, Director of Food Safety & Quality
cc: Gary Lindqvist, Plant Manager, Wooster
Date: November 12, 2026
Re: Sesame cross-contact on Line 3 - Harvest Grain Crackers, Rosemary & Sea Salt, 7 oz (UPC 0 41620 77310 4) - FDA warning letter CMS # 712044

## 1. Purpose

You asked me on November 10, the day we received FDA's warning letter of November 6, 2026, to investigate how sesame got into Harvest Grain Crackers and to tell you which product is affected. This memo sets out what the Food Safety & Quality team has found so far: the root cause, the affected lots, a second possible source of sesame that we are still testing, the corrective actions under way, and the date by which we need to respond to FDA.

## 2. Summary

**Root cause.** Line 3 is shared with "Everything Seed Crisps", which contains sesame. The validated changeover clean between the two products (SSOP-L3-07) was skipped on three occasions, on July 22, August 5 and August 19, 2026, because of a staffing shortage on second shift.

**Affected product.** 9 lots, HG2607A through HG2609C, about 41,800 cases, distributed to retailers in six states (OH, PA, MI, IN, KY, WV).

**Complaints.** No consumer illness complaints have been received as of November 12, 2026.

**Second possible source.** Prairie Mills' certificate for the RS-440 rosemary seasoning blend says the blend contains no sesame but is produced in a facility that also processes sesame. We are testing the blend.

**Response.** Response due by December 1, 2026.

## 3. Background

Line 3 at Wooster sheets, cuts, bakes, seasons and packs crackers. Everything Seed Crisps were moved onto Line 3 from Line 1 on July 6, 2026 to free capacity on Line 1. Since then, Line 3 has run Everything Seed Crisps at the start of the week and changed over to Harvest Grain Crackers on Wednesday, second shift.

Our food safety plan identifies sesame cross-contact as a hazard for Harvest Grain Crackers on Line 3 and controls it with SSOP-L3-07, the validated allergen changeover clean: full disassembly and wet clean of the topping applicator, seasoning drum and slitter, followed by a visual inspection and sign-off by the sanitation lead. Until the inspection, the visual inspection was the only check. We did not use ATP or allergen swabs on Line 3 changeovers.

## 4. Root cause

We reviewed the sanitation logs, production records and shift rosters for Line 3 from July 6 to September 18, 2026, and interviewed the second-shift sanitation crew and line leads between November 10 and November 12.

On July 22, August 5 and August 19, 2026, second shift was short of sanitation staff. On each of those days the line lead started the Harvest Grain run after a dry clean (scraping and vacuuming) instead of the full SSOP-L3-07 clean. The SSOP-L3-07 sign-off boxes for those three changeovers are blank. Nobody escalated the missed cleans to Food Safety & Quality, and nobody reviewed the logs in time to catch them. The crew told us they understood that the dry clean was "good enough" when the line was behind schedule. That understanding was wrong, and our procedures did not say clearly that the line must stop if the validated clean cannot be done.

FDA's two samples fit this picture only in part. Lot HG2608B, in which FDA found sesame at 14 ppm, was produced on August 19, immediately after the third skipped clean. Lot HG2609A, in which FDA found sesame at 38 ppm, was produced on September 2, after a changeover that is signed off as completed. Because no changeover clean was ever verified, we cannot rule out that the September 2 clean was ineffective. But we also cannot rule out that sesame came in with an ingredient (section 6).

## 6. Second possible source: Prairie Mills RS-440

Harvest Grain Crackers are seasoned with rosemary seasoning blend RS-440 from Prairie Mills Ingredients, LLC of Kansas. Prairie Mills' allergen certificate dated March 3, 2026 states that the blend does not contain sesame but that it is manufactured in a facility that also processes sesame, milk and soy. Our supplier approval file accepted that statement as it stood. We have not audited Prairie Mills, and we have not tested RS-440 for sesame.

The RS-440 blend is therefore a second possible source, and it could explain the HG2609A result. We have sent retained samples of the RS-440 lots used in the nine affected lots, and samples from two unopened totes in the warehouse, to an outside accredited laboratory for sesame testing. Results are expected next week. We have also asked Prairie Mills for its allergen control program and any sesame test data it holds. Until the results are in, I would not tell FDA that the skipped cleans are the only cause.

## 7. Corrective actions under way

(a) **Line 3 dedicated.** Line 3 will be dedicated to non-sesame products from November 16, 2026. Everything Seed Crisps move back to Line 1.

(b) **Label.** The Harvest Grain Crackers label has been revised to "Contains: Wheat, Soy, Sesame". The print order was placed on November 11, 2026. Until the RS-440 results are in, we do not want to ship product under a label that could be wrong again.

(c) **Retraining.** All sanitation staff and line leads on all shifts are being retrained on SSOP-L3-07, including a new stop-the-line rule: no run starts unless the validated clean has been completed and verified.

(d) **Verification.** Cleaning is verified by ATP and allergen swab on every changeover. We began this on September 21, 2026 as an interim control, together with a hold-and-test of finished product, and we are writing it into SSOP-L3-07 as a permanent requirement.

Gary Lindqvist has also changed the second-shift roster so that the sanitation crew cannot be moved to production during a changeover.
