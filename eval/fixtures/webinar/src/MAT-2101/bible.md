## 2. The Agreement

"Temperature-Controlled Warehousing and Distribution Services Agreement" between Hollis (Customer) and Brennan, dated
and effective **March 1, 2024** ("Effective Date"). Initial Term three (3) years; renews yearly unless 90 days' notice.
"Contract Year" = each 12-month period beginning March 1. Signed by Marcus Bell (Hollis) and Gregory Halvorsen
(Brennan) on March 1, 2024.

Key sections (use these numbers and words):
- **1. Definitions.** "Business Day" (Monday–Friday, not a federal holiday); "Goods"; "Temperature-Controlled Goods"
  (Goods to be kept at 2 °C to 8 °C); "Excursion" (any period of more than thirty (30) consecutive minutes during which
  Temperature-Controlled Goods are outside 2 °C to 8 °C); "Shipment"; "Pickup Time"; "Delivery Window"; "Replacement
  Cost" (Customer's documented cost to replace Goods that cannot be used); "Logger Data".
- **4. Services.** Storage at Brennan's Eddystone facility; refrigerated transport to the Newark DC and to Customer's
  customers within the Territory (Delaware, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Maryland).
- **5. Fees.** 5.1 Storage $42.00 per pallet per month; 5.2 Handling $18.00 per pallet in and $18.00 per pallet out;
  5.3 Transport per Exhibit B (refrigerated truckload Eddystone–Newark DC: $1,150.00); 5.4 invoices monthly, net 30.
- **6. Temperature Control.** 6.1 Brennan keeps Temperature-Controlled Goods at 2 °C to 8 °C at all times in its
  custody; 6.2 continuous data loggers on every Shipment; **6.3 Brennan shall provide Logger Data within two (2)
  Business Days after Customer's request.**
- **7. Delivery.** **7.1 Delivery Window: each Shipment within the Territory shall be delivered within twenty-four (24)
  hours after its Pickup Time.** 7.2 proof of delivery signed by Customer's receiving staff; **7.4 Late Delivery
  Credit: for each Shipment delivered after the Delivery Window, Brennan shall credit Customer $1,250.00 for each full
  day (24 hours) of delay, up to a maximum of ten (10) days per Shipment.**
- **9. Claims.** **9.3 "Customer shall give Brennan written notice of any claim for loss of or damage to Goods within
  five (5) Business Days after Customer becomes aware of the Excursion or damage, and shall submit its full claim with
  supporting documents within sixty (60) days after that notice."**
- **10. Liability for Goods.** Brennan is liable for loss of or damage to Goods in its custody, including Goods that
  cannot be used because of an Excursion, measured by Replacement Cost, plus Customer's reasonable expedited
  replacement freight.
- **12. Limitation of Liability.** 12.1 no consequential damages (except 12.3); **12.2 (original): "Brennan's aggregate
  liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000) in
  any Contract Year."** 12.3 the limits do not apply to gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of Section 6.
  (Note: 12.3 carve-out for Section 6 breaches is real and matters; keep it.)
- **15. Term and Termination.** termination for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days after notice.
- **18. Governing Law; Forum.** Delaware law; exclusive forum the Superior Court of Delaware (CCLD); jury trial waived.
- **19. Notices.** to Hollis: Attn: General Counsel; to Brennan: Attn: Chief Operating Officer; by email with
  confirmation copy by courier.
- **Exhibit B** rate schedule (a short table).

## 5. Planted issues (what the demo shows)

1. **Delivery-date conflict.** Brennan's Incident Report (Kim Delacroix, June 4, 2026) says the Shipment was "delivered
   to Hollis Newark DC on June 3, 2026 at 2:15 p.m." The Hollis receiving log, the Elena Ruiz interview memo and the
   excursion report all say it arrived **June 6, 2026 at 9:40 a.m.** Brennan's response letter repeats June 3.
2. **Misquoted cap.** Brennan's response letter quotes Section 12.2 as "shall not exceed Five Hundred Thousand Dollars
   ($500,000) in any Contract Year" and says liability is capped at $500,000 — that is the ORIGINAL text, replaced by
   the First Amendment ($1,500,000). Also 12.3 removes the cap for breach of Section 6 anyway.
3. **Misquoted notice clause.** Brennan's response letter says Section 9.3 requires notice "within forty-eight (48)
   hours of delivery" and that Hollis's June 9 notice was late. The Agreement says five (5) Business Days after Customer
   becomes aware.
4. **Logger Data late:** requested July 15, provided July 24 (Section 6.3: two Business Days).
5. **Credit memo short:** Brennan credited $1,150.00 (the freight charge) — not the Late Delivery Credit, not the Goods.
6. **Calculation:** Late Delivery Credit for 3 full days = $3,750.00 (Section 7.4).
