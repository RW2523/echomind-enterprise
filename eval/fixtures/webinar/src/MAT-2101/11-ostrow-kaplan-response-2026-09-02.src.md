This firm represents Brennan Freight Lines, LLC ("Brennan"). We write in response to your letter of August 18, 2026 to Gregory Halvorsen, which demands $1,228,900.00 on behalf of your client, Hollis Medical Supply, Inc. ("Hollis"). Please direct all further communications concerning this matter to the undersigned and not to Brennan.

Brennan denies that it is liable to Hollis in the amount demanded, or in any amount approaching it, for the reasons summarized below.

**1. Delivery of the Shipment**

Brennan's records show that Shipment HMS-26-0588 was delivered to Hollis's Newark DC on June 3, 2026 at 2:15 p.m., following the transfer of the load to trailer BFL-R-217 after an alarm on the reefer unit of trailer BFL-R-204 on June 2. Brennan's dispatch supervisor recorded the delivery in Incident Report IR-26-0412 on June 4, 2026. On those facts the Shipment was delivered less than one full day after the end of the Delivery Window, and no Late Delivery Credit is due under Section 7.4 of the Agreement. Hollis's internal receiving log, prepared by its own employees, does not displace Brennan's business records, and we note that your letter does not enclose the proof of delivery on which it relies.

**2. Hollis's notice was untimely**

Section 9.3 of the Agreement requires the customer to give notice of any claim "within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery." Hollis did not give notice until June 9, 2026, six days after the Shipment was delivered on June 3. Hollis's claim is therefore barred by the plain terms of the Agreement. Brennan's acknowledgment of Ms. Lowell's email of June 9 was expressly made with a reservation of all of Brennan's rights and does not waive this defense.

**3. Brennan's liability is capped**

Even if Hollis could establish liability, which Brennan denies, Section 12.2 of the Agreement provides that Brennan's aggregate liability "shall not exceed Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000) in any Contract Year." Brennan's liability for all claims arising out of the Agreement in the current Contract Year is accordingly capped at $500,000, and Hollis's demand for more than twice that amount has no contractual basis.

**4. Damages**

Hollis's claimed damages are, in any event, overstated:

(a) Hollis destroyed all 22 pallets on the basis of its own desk review of manufacturer guidance, without independent stability testing and without any attempt to segregate product lines that may have tolerated a short temperature deviation. Brennan is not responsible for losses that Hollis could reasonably have avoided.

(b) The claimed expedited replacement freight of $38,750.00 is consequential in nature and is excluded by Section 12.1 of the Agreement.

(c) Brennan has already credited Hollis $1,150.00 for this Shipment by credit memo BFL-CM-26-019, in recognition of the service interruption.

**5. Logger Data**

Brennan provided the Logger Data on July 24, 2026, as soon as its reefer service vendor had retrieved the unit file for trailer BFL-R-204. The timing of that production caused Hollis no loss and is irrelevant to the merits of its claim.

**6. Settlement proposal**

Without prejudice to the foregoing, and solely in the interest of avoiding the cost of litigation and preserving the parties' commercial relationship, Brennan is prepared to pay Hollis $150,000 in full and final settlement of all claims arising out of or relating to Shipment HMS-26-0588, in exchange for a mutual release in a form acceptable to both parties. This offer is made for settlement purposes only, is subject to Delaware Rule of Evidence 408 and Federal Rule of Evidence 408, and may not be used for any other purpose. It will remain open for fourteen (14) days from the date of this letter.

Brennan has preserved its documents relating to the Shipment and expects Hollis to do the same, including all records relating to the quarantine, testing and destruction of the Goods. Brennan expressly reserves all of its rights, remedies and defenses under the Agreement and at law, including its right to recover its attorneys' fees and costs to the extent permitted.

Nothing in this letter is intended as a complete statement of the facts or of Brennan's defenses.
