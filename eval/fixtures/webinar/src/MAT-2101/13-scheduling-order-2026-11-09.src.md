# IN THE SUPERIOR COURT OF THE STATE OF DELAWARE

HOLLIS MEDICAL SUPPLY, INC.,
Plaintiff,

v.

BRENNAN FREIGHT LINES, LLC,
Defendant.

C.A. No. N26C-09-117 CCLD

# SCHEDULING ORDER

WHEREAS, Plaintiff Hollis Medical Supply, Inc. ("Plaintiff") filed its Complaint on September 21, 2026;

WHEREAS, Defendant Brennan Freight Lines, LLC ("Defendant") filed its Answer on October 26, 2026;

WHEREAS, the parties have conferred and submitted a proposed schedule for the Court's consideration; and

WHEREAS, the parties have waived trial by jury in Section 18.3 of the agreement on which this action is based;

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED this 9th day of November, 2026, that the following schedule shall govern this action:

1. Initial disclosures. The parties shall exchange initial disclosures identifying the persons likely to have discoverable information, describing by category the relevant documents in their possession, custody or control, and setting out a computation of any damages claimed, by November 30, 2026.

2. Confidentiality and electronically stored information. The parties shall meet and confer regarding a stipulated confidentiality order and a protocol for the production of electronically stored information, and shall submit them for the Court's approval in advance of the first document production.

3. Fact discovery. All fact discovery shall be initiated so that it is completed by May 28, 2027. Unless the Court orders otherwise for good cause, each side is limited to thirty (30) interrogatories, including discrete subparts, and ten (10) fact depositions of no more than seven (7) hours each.

4. Plaintiff's expert reports. Plaintiff shall serve the reports of any expert witnesses on issues on which it bears the burden of proof by June 25, 2027.

5. Defendant's expert reports. Defendant shall serve the reports of its expert witnesses, including any rebuttal reports, by July 30, 2027.

6. Expert discovery. All expert discovery, including the depositions of expert witnesses, shall be completed by September 3, 2027.

7. Mediation. The parties shall complete mediation before a mediator agreed by them, or appointed by the Court if they cannot agree, by September 30, 2027. The parties shall promptly report the outcome of the mediation to the Court by letter.

8. Dispositive motions. All case-dispositive motions, and any motion to exclude expert testimony, shall be filed by October 15, 2027. Briefing shall proceed in accordance with the Superior Court Civil Rules and the Complex Commercial Litigation Division guidelines unless the parties stipulate to a different briefing schedule approved by the Court.

9. Pretrial conference. The Court will hold a pretrial conference on January 10, 2028 at 10:00 a.m. The parties shall file a joint proposed pretrial stipulation and order, motions in limine and their exhibit lists in advance of the conference in accordance with the Court's guidelines.

10. Trial. A bench trial, the parties having waived trial by jury, shall begin on February 7, 2028 at 9:30 a.m. and is scheduled for four (4) days.

11. Modification. The dates in this Order may be modified only by order of the Court upon a showing of good cause. A stipulation of the parties to extend any date must be submitted to the Court for approval and is not effective until approved.

12. Discovery disputes. Before filing any discovery motion, counsel shall meet and confer in good faith. Disputes that are not resolved shall be presented to the Court by letter of no more than three (3) pages, and the Court will schedule a teleconference.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
