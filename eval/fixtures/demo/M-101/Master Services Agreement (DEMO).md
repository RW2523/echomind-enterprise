## Schedule 2 — Service Levels and Service Credits

1. Availability Target. Provider shall ensure that Availability in each calendar month is at least 99.5% (the "Availability Target").

2. Calculation. Availability (%) = ((Total Minutes − Downtime) ÷ Total Minutes) × 100, where "Total Minutes" is the total number of minutes in the calendar month and "Downtime" is the total number of minutes in that month during which the production environment of the Services is unavailable or materially degraded for Client, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and any unavailability caused by Client, the Client Products or a force majeure event under Clause 15. Availability is rounded to two decimal places.

3. Service Credits. If Availability in a calendar month is below the Availability Target, Provider shall give Client a Service Credit equal to the following percentage of the Monthly Subscription Fee for that month:

| Availability in the calendar month | Service Credit (% of the Monthly Subscription Fee) |
|---|---|
| 99.0% or more but below 99.5% | 5% |
| 98.0% or more but below 99.0% | 10% |
| 95.0% or more but below 98.0% | 20% |
| Below 95.0% | 30% |

4. Cap. The total Service Credits for any calendar month shall not exceed thirty percent (30%) of the Monthly Subscription Fee for that month.

5. Reporting and credit. Provider shall deliver to Client a service level report within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar quarter, showing Availability for each month of the quarter. Service Credits are credited against the next invoice issued after the report is delivered. Client does not need to claim a Service Credit.

6. Chronic failure. If Availability is below 97.0% in any two (2) consecutive calendar months, or in any three (3) calendar months in any rolling period of twelve (12) months, Client may terminate this Agreement for cause under Clause 12.4 by written notice given within thirty (30) days after receiving the service level report that shows the failure. No early termination fee is payable on such a termination.

7. Remedy. Service Credits are Client's sole financial remedy for a failure to meet the Availability Target, without prejudice to Client's rights under paragraph 6 of this Schedule and under Clauses 12.2 and 12.4.
