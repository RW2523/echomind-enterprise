## Background

The Client's Master Services Agreement with Harrowgate Systems Inc. comes up for renewal on March 2, 2027. The Client wishes to engage the Consultant to give it independent technical support for the renewal and to prepare a plan for moving Lindqvist Flow to another platform if the renewal does not go ahead (the "Project").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

"Background IP" means Intellectual Property Rights owned by or licensed to the Consultant before the date of this Agreement, or developed by the Consultant independently of the Services.

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday in Sweden.

"Day" means one consultant working day of eight (8) hours.

"Deliverables" means all reports, plans, analyses, data models, scripts and other materials prepared by the Consultant for the Client in performing the Services.

"Fee Cap" has the meaning given in Clause 4.3.

"Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, copyright, database rights, design rights, trade marks, rights in know-how and all similar rights anywhere in the world, whether registered or not.

"Key Personnel" means Linnea Sjöberg (Principal Consultant) and Oskar Lund (Senior Analyst).

"Services" means the services described in Schedule 1.

## 2. Services

2.1 The Consultant shall perform the Services for the Project with the skill, care and diligence expected of an experienced provider of similar services.

2.2 The Services start on October 26, 2026 and end on March 31, 2027, unless this Agreement is terminated earlier.

2.3 The Consultant shall perform the Services through the Key Personnel, Linnea Sjöberg (Principal Consultant) and Oskar Lund (Senior Analyst). The Consultant shall not replace either of the Key Personnel without the Client's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

2.4 The Consultant shall deliver each Deliverable by the date set out in Schedule 1.

## 3. Client Obligations

3.1 The Client shall give the Consultant access to the information reasonably needed for the Services, including the Client's agreement with Harrowgate Systems Inc., Harrowgate's service level reports and the Client's platform monitoring data.

3.2 The Client's contact for the Project is Petra Voss, General Counsel. Technical questions shall be directed to Jonas Ekberg, Head of Platform Engineering.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 The Client shall pay the Consultant for each Day worked at the following day rates: Principal Consultant, SEK 14,500 per Day; Senior Analyst, SEK 10,800 per Day. Part of a Day is charged in half-Day units.

4.2 The Consultant shall invoice monthly in arrears for the Days worked in the previous month, with timesheets. Each invoice is payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.3 The total fees payable under this Agreement shall not exceed SEK 1,200,000 excluding VAT (the "Fee Cap"). The Consultant shall not perform work that would take the fees above the Fee Cap unless the Client has first agreed a written variation under Clause 13.2.

4.4 The Consultant shall notify the Client in writing when the fees invoiced reach eighty percent (80%) of the Fee Cap.

4.5 The Client shall reimburse reasonable travel expenses approved in advance in writing, up to a total of SEK 40,000 for the Project. Expenses do not count towards the Fee Cap.

4.6 All fees are exclusive of value added tax, which is payable in addition.

## 5. Consultant Personnel

5.1 The Consultant is an independent contractor. The Consultant is responsible for the salaries, taxes and social security contributions of its personnel.

5.2 The Consultant's personnel shall comply with the Client's information-security and site rules when they have access to the Client's systems or premises.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All Intellectual Property Rights in the Deliverables vest in the Client on creation. To the extent they do not, the Consultant assigns to the Client, with full title, all present and future Intellectual Property Rights in the Deliverables, effective as each Deliverable is created.

6.2 The Background IP remains the property of the Consultant. The Consultant grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use any Background IP incorporated in the Deliverables for the Client's business purposes, including the transfer of Lindqvist Flow to another platform.

6.3 The Consultant shall procure that its personnel waive their moral rights in the Deliverables to the extent permitted by law.

6.4 The Consultant warrants that the Deliverables and the Client's use of them in accordance with this Agreement will not infringe the Intellectual Property Rights of any third party, and shall indemnify the Client against any third-party claim that they do.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Consultant shall keep confidential all information received from the Client in connection with the Project, including the terms of the Client's agreement with Harrowgate Systems Inc. and any proposal Harrowgate makes, and shall use it only to perform the Services. This obligation continues for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

8.1 The Consultant's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid under this Agreement.

8.2 Neither party is liable for indirect loss or loss of profits.

8.3 Clauses 8.1 and 8.2 do not apply to liability for wilful misconduct, gross negligence, breach of Clause 7, or under the indemnity in Clause 6.4.

## 9. Insurance

9.1 The Consultant shall maintain professional liability insurance with cover of at least SEK 10,000,000 per claim during the term of this Agreement and for two (2) years after it ends.

## 10. Conflicts of Interest

10.1 The Consultant confirms that it does not act for Harrowgate Systems Inc. and shall not accept any engagement from Harrowgate Systems Inc. before the Services end.

10.2 The Consultant has disclosed to the Client that one of its employees is a family member of a director of the Client. That employee shall not work on the Project.

## 11. Data Protection

11.1 The Consultant does not expect to process personal data for the Client. If it needs to do so, the parties shall first sign a data processing agreement.

## 12. Termination

12.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience on fifteen (15) Business Days' written notice to the Consultant.

12.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied within fifteen (15) Business Days of written notice.

12.3 On termination the Client shall pay for the Days worked up to the termination date, and the Consultant shall deliver all Deliverables, whether finished or not.

13.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties about the Project.

13.2 Any variation of this Agreement, including of the Services or the Fee Cap, must be in writing and signed by both parties.
