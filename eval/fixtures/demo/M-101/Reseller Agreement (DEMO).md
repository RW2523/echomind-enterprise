# RESELLER AGREEMENT

This Reseller Agreement (the "Agreement") is dated April 20, 2026 (the "Effective Date") and is made between:

(1) Voss-Lindqvist AB, a Swedish limited company (corporate identity number 556812-4471) with its registered office at Kvarnhamnsgatan 14, SE-417 56 Gothenburg, Sweden (the "Supplier"); and

(2) Fjellstad Distribution AS, a Norwegian limited company (organisation number 921 447 380) with its registered office at Kaiveien 18, 0151 Oslo, Norway (the "Reseller").

## Background

(A) The Supplier develops Lindqvist Flow, a warehouse-orchestration application that the Supplier makes available as a subscription service hosted on a third-party platform.

(B) The Supplier wishes to appoint the Reseller to resell subscriptions to Lindqvist Flow in the Territory, and the Reseller wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday in Sweden or Norway.

"Contract Year" means each period of twelve (12) months starting on the Effective Date or on an anniversary of it.

"End Customer" means a customer in the Territory that buys a Flow Subscription from the Reseller.

"End Customer Terms" means the Supplier's standard end-user terms for Lindqvist Flow, as notified to the Reseller from time to time.

"Flow Subscription" means a subscription to Lindqvist Flow for one warehouse site, as described in Schedule 1.

"List Price" means the Supplier's list price for a Flow Subscription or service set out in Schedule 1, as changed under Clause 5.5.

"Minimum Annual Commitment" means net purchases by the Reseller of at least EUR 250,000 in each Contract Year.

"Platform Provider" means Harrowgate Systems Inc., or any replacement provider of the platform on which Lindqvist Flow is hosted.

"Territory" means Norway, Denmark and Iceland.

"Trade Marks" means the LINDQVIST FLOW name and logo and any other trade marks of the Supplier notified to the Reseller.

## 2. Appointment

2.1 The Supplier appoints the Reseller as its non-exclusive reseller of Flow Subscriptions to End Customers in the Territory.

2.2 The Reseller buys Flow Subscriptions from the Supplier and resells them in its own name and for its own account. The Reseller is not the Supplier's agent and has no authority to bind the Supplier.

2.3 The Reseller shall not actively market Flow Subscriptions outside the Territory without the Supplier's prior written consent.

2.4 The Reseller shall meet the Minimum Annual Commitment in each Contract Year.

## 3. Term

3.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months, ending on April 19, 2028.

3.2 This Agreement then renews for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives the other at least three (3) months' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. Orders and Activation

4.1 The Reseller shall place orders for Flow Subscriptions through the Supplier's partner portal. Each order is subject to acceptance by the Supplier.

4.2 The Supplier shall activate each accepted Flow Subscription within five (5) Business Days of acceptance.

4.3 Each Flow Subscription has a minimum subscription period of twelve (12) months from activation.

4.4 The Reseller shall ensure that each End Customer accepts the End Customer Terms before its Flow Subscription is activated.

## 5. Prices and Payment

5.1 The Reseller shall pay the List Price less a discount of twenty-five percent (25%) for each Flow Subscription and service it buys.

5.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Reseller monthly in arrears for all Flow Subscriptions active during the previous month. Invoices are payable net forty-five (45) days from the invoice date.

5.3 Late payments accrue interest at ten percent (10%) per annum from the due date until paid.

5.4 If an undisputed invoice remains unpaid twenty (20) days after its due date, the Supplier may suspend the activation of new Flow Subscriptions until it is paid.

5.5 The Supplier may change the List Prices on at least ninety (90) days' written notice to the Reseller, no more than once in any Contract Year, and by no more than five percent (5%) on each occasion.

5.6 The Reseller is free to set its own resale prices to End Customers.

5.7 All prices are in euros and exclude value added tax, which is payable in addition where applicable.

## 6. Liability

6.1 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to EUR 400,000.

6.2 Neither party is liable for loss of profits.

6.3 Clauses 6.1 and 6.2 do not apply to liability for fraud, gross negligence or wilful misconduct, to breach of Clause 10, or to the Reseller's obligation to pay invoices.

## 7. Supplier Obligations

7.1 The Supplier shall make each activated Flow Subscription available to the End Customer in accordance with the Supplier's service description for Lindqvist Flow.

7.2 On each Business Day, the Supplier shall provide second-line support in English to the Reseller between 08:00 and 17:00 Central European Time.

7.3 The Supplier shall maintain in force throughout the term of this Agreement an agreement with a Platform Provider sufficient to make the Flow Subscriptions available to End Customers, and shall give the Reseller not less than three (3) months' written notice of any change of Platform Provider.

7.4 If the Supplier receives a service credit from the Platform Provider for unavailability that affected End Customers, the Supplier shall pass a fair share of that credit to the Reseller, in proportion to the subscription fees of the affected End Customers.

## 8. Reseller Obligations

8.1 The Reseller shall use reasonable efforts to promote Flow Subscriptions in the Territory and shall provide first-line support to End Customers in the local language.

8.2 The Reseller shall deliver the quarterly report described in Schedule 2.

8.3 The Reseller shall comply with all laws applicable to its activities under this Agreement, including anti-bribery, sanctions and export-control laws.

## 9. Trade Marks

9.1 The Supplier grants the Reseller a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use the Trade Marks in the Territory during the term of this Agreement, only to promote and resell Flow Subscriptions and in accordance with the Supplier's brand guidelines.

9.2 The Reseller shall not register any trade mark or domain name that includes or is confusingly similar to the Trade Marks.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party under this Agreement and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, during the term of this Agreement and for three (3) years after it ends.

## 11. Data Protection

11.1 For personal data processed in Lindqvist Flow, the End Customer is the controller and the Supplier is the processor under the End Customer Terms. The Reseller processes only the business contact details of End Customers' staff.

11.2 The Supplier shall notify the Reseller of any personal data breach affecting End Customer data without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

11.3 The Reseller shall pass any notice under Clause 11.2 to the affected End Customers without delay.

## 12. Change of Control

12.1 The Reseller shall tell the Supplier within fifteen (15) days of any change of control.

12.2 The Supplier may terminate this Agreement on forty-five (45) days' written notice if the Reseller undergoes a change of control.

12.3 In this Clause 12, a change of control occurs when any person or group of persons acting together acquires more than fifty percent (50%) of the shares or voting rights in the Reseller, or the right to appoint a majority of its board.

## 13. Termination

13.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach of this Agreement that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to be remedied.

13.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, enters into bankruptcy or reconstruction proceedings, or makes an arrangement with its creditors generally.

13.3 The Supplier may terminate this Agreement on three (3) months' written notice if the Reseller fails to meet the Minimum Annual Commitment in any Contract Year.

## 14. Consequences of Termination

14.1 On termination, each Flow Subscription then active continues until the end of its current subscription period, and the Supplier may contract directly with the End Customer for any renewal.

14.2 The Reseller shall pay all invoices for Flow Subscriptions supplied up to the end of their current subscription periods, and shall stop using the Trade Marks within thirty (30) days.

14.3 Clauses 5, 6, 10, 14 and 19 survive termination.

## 15. Assignment

15.1 Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that the Supplier may assign it to an affiliate of the Supplier on written notice.

## 16. Force Majeure

16.1 Neither party is liable for any failure or delay caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control. Failure of the Platform Provider is not such a circumstance unless the Platform Provider's failure was itself caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control.

## 17. Notices

17.1 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent by courier or email to: for the Supplier, the General Counsel, Kvarnhamnsgatan 14, SE-417 56 Gothenburg, Sweden (legal@voss-lindqvist.example); and for the Reseller, the Managing Director, Kaiveien 18, 0151 Oslo, Norway (post@fjellstad-distribution.example).

## 18. General

18.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.

18.2 Nothing in this Agreement creates an exclusive distributorship, partnership or agency.

## 19. Governing Law and Disputes

19.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Sweden.

19.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be decided by the Swedish courts, with Gothenburg District Court as the court of first instance.
