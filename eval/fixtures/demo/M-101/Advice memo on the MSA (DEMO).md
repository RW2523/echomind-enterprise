## 1. Summary

1.1 The Master Services Agreement dated March 2, 2026 (the "MSA") renews automatically for twelve months on March 2, 2027 unless either party gives notice of non-renewal by December 1, 2026.

1.2 Without a signed amendment, the MSA renews on its current terms. Harrowgate's proposal of October 14, 2026 would raise the Monthly Subscription Fee by about 6.8% to USD 19,750 and remove the USD 250,000 minimum from the liability cap. We recommend that the client reject the change to the cap and accept a fee increase above CPI only in exchange for a longer term.

1.4 The client's Reseller Agreement with Fjellstad Distribution AS runs until April 19, 2028, so the client needs the platform to continue. The main risk is that Harrowgate itself serves notice of non-renewal to press its terms.

## 3. Term and Renewal

3.1 Under Clause 2.1, the MSA continues "for an initial term of twelve (12) months" from the Effective Date of March 2, 2026. The Initial Term therefore ends on March 1, 2027.

3.2 Clause 2.2 provides that the MSA "renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month renewal terms (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term." Ninety days before March 1, 2027 is December 1, 2026, which is the last day on which either party can give notice of non-renewal.

3.3 A notice must be addressed to the other party's General Counsel at the address in Schedule 4 (Clause 22.1). For Harrowgate that is Miriam Achterberg in Wilmington, Delaware. A notice of non-renewal is not valid if it is sent by email only (Clause 22.2), and a courier delivery is deemed received on the delivery date recorded by the courier (Clause 22.3).

3.4 Because either party may give notice, Harrowgate could serve notice of non-renewal by December 1, 2026 to force a negotiation. If it did, the MSA would end on March 1, 2027, subject to exit assistance for up to ninety (90) days under Clause 13.2.

3.5 The client does not need to serve notice to protect itself against Harrowgate's proposed terms. The Monthly Subscription Fee can be increased only by a signed amendment (Clause 20.3) or by a CPI increase under Clause 4.4. No early termination fee is payable on a termination for convenience that takes effect after the Initial Term, so the client can leave a Renewal Term on ninety (90) days' notice (Clause 12.1). We therefore recommend that the client does not serve notice of non-renewal, but we will keep a notice ready in case its instructions change.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 Clause 4.1 sets the Monthly Subscription Fee at USD 18,500 per month, or USD 222,000 a year.

4.2 Clause 4.2 provides that Harrowgate shall invoice monthly in advance and that "Invoices are payable net sixty (60) days from the invoice date." We see no reason to change the payment mechanics in the renewal.

4.3 Under Clause 4.3, undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month. The client should therefore dispute any invoice it does not accept in writing within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date, as Clause 4.5 requires, and pay the undisputed part on time.

4.4 Under Clause 4.4, Harrowgate may increase the Monthly Subscription Fee for a Renewal Term only by written notice given at least one hundred and twenty (120) days before the Renewal Term starts, which for the Renewal Term starting on March 2, 2027 means by November 2, 2026, and only by the percentage increase in CPI. Harrowgate's letter states that its proposal is not a notice under that clause. The CPI figure for the relevant period is not stated in Harrowgate's letter, and we have not been given it.

4.5 The proposed fee of USD 19,750 is an increase of USD 1,250 per month, or about 6.8%, and would raise the annual fee to USD 237,000. In return Harrowgate offers to raise the Named Users included in the fee from 400 to 500 (Clause 3.4 and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3). At the Schedule 3 rate of USD 35 per additional Named User per month, those 100 users are worth up to USD 3,500 per month, but only if the client would otherwise need them.

## 5. Liability

5.1 Clause 8.1 currently provides: "Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of USD 250,000 or the Fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim." At the current fee the Fees paid in any twelve months are about USD 222,000, so the USD 250,000 minimum is the cap that applies today.

5.2 Harrowgate's draft amendment would delete the USD 250,000 minimum. The cap would then be the Fees paid in the preceding twelve months only: about USD 222,000 for a claim early in the Renewal Term, rising to no more than USD 237,000 after twelve months at the proposed fee. The change lowers the cap for both parties, but it matters more to the client, because Harrowgate is the party more likely to cause a loss.

5.3 The proposal does not affect the exclusions in Clause 8.3 (fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of Clause 5, the indemnities under Clause 9 and the payment of Fees), the separate cap of USD 1,000,000 for breach of Clause 6 in Clause 8.4, or Clause 8.5, under which Service Credits do not count towards the cap.

5.4 We recommend that the client reject the change to Clause 8.1. If the client is willing to trade, it should ask for a minimum of at least USD 400,000.

## 6. Service Levels and Service Credits

6.1 The Availability Target is 99.5% in each calendar month (paragraph 1 of Schedule 2). The Q3 2026 report shows Availability of 99.82% in July, 98.71% in August and 99.64% in September 2026.

6.2 Availability in August 2026 was below the Availability Target, so a Service Credit is due under paragraph 3 of Schedule 2. It is credited against the next invoice issued after the report (paragraph 5 of Schedule 2). Harrowgate issues invoices on the first Business Day of each month (paragraph 5 of Schedule 3), so the credit should appear on the invoice issued on November 2, 2026. Northbridge Analytics AB will check the August figure against the client's own monitoring data by November 10, 2026.

6.3 The report also gives a figure of 99.39% for the whole quarter. That figure is for information only: Availability and Service Credits are measured by calendar month.

6.4 The chronic-failure right in paragraph 6 of Schedule 2 requires Availability below 97.0% in two consecutive months, or in three months in any rolling twelve months. The Q3 figures do not meet that test, so no termination right arises. Under paragraph 7 of Schedule 2, Service Credits are the client's sole financial remedy for missing the Availability Target.

## 7. Other Points

7.1 Data protection. Harrowgate must notify the client of a Personal Data Breach "within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of it" (Clause 6.3). The proposal does not change this, and the client should not agree to any change.

7.2 Change of Control. Under Clause 14.2, Harrowgate must give the client no less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any Change of Control. If it does not, the client may terminate for cause within ninety (90) days of becoming aware of the Change of Control (Clause 14.3). We have seen no such notice.

7.3 Termination for convenience. Only the client may terminate for convenience, on ninety (90) days' written notice (Clause 12.1). During the Initial Term an early termination fee of up to three months' Monthly Subscription Fees applies. Harrowgate has no right to terminate for convenience.

7.4 Reseller dependency. The Reseller Agreement has an initial term of twenty-four (24) months ending on April 19, 2028 (its Clause 3.1). Its Clause 7.3 requires the client to keep a platform agreement in force throughout that term and to give Fjellstad Distribution AS at least three (3) months' written notice of any change of Platform Provider. If Harrowgate served notice of non-renewal, the client would have very little time to give that notice and to move Lindqvist Flow.
