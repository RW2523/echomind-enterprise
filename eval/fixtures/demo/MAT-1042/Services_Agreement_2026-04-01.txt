2. SCOPE AND SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 Scope. The Supplier shall manufacture, test, pack and deliver the Units, and perform the Services, in accordance with this Agreement, the Specifications and good industry practice.

2.2 Quantity. The Buyer shall purchase, and the Supplier shall supply, forty (40) Units.

2.3 Services. The Services comprise:
(a) factory acceptance testing of each Unit at the Supplier's plant before dispatch, including a run test and a pressure and leak test of each refrigerant circuit, and the issue of a signed test certificate for each Unit;
(b) packing of the Units in crates suitable for road transport, two (2) Units to a crate;
(c) transport of the Units to, and unloading at, the Delivery Point;
(d) supervision of the Buyer's installation contractor during commissioning, for up to ten (10) technician days; and
(e) supply of operation and maintenance manuals in English.

2.4 Specifications. Each Unit shall conform to the Specifications. The Supplier shall not change the design, materials or principal components of the Units without the Buyer's prior written consent.

2.5 Order of precedence. If there is a conflict between this Agreement, the Specifications and the Purchase Order, this Agreement prevails, then the Specifications, then the Purchase Order.

3. PRICE AND PAYMENT

3.1 Purchase Order. The Buyer shall issue the Purchase Order within five (5) Business Days after the Effective Date. The Purchase Order is issued under, and is governed by, this Agreement.

3.2 Unit Price. The price of each Unit is USD 31,000 (the "Unit Price").

3.3 Total Contract Price. The total price for the Units and the Services is USD 1,240,000 (the "Total Contract Price"), being forty (40) Units at the Unit Price.

3.4 Inclusions and taxes. The Total Contract Price is fixed. It includes packing, transport, insurance in transit, unloading at the Delivery Point, the Services and all duties. It excludes any sales or use tax, which the Buyer shall pay, if any applies, against a valid invoice.

3.5 Deposit. The Supplier may invoice a deposit of twenty percent (20%) of the Total Contract Price, being USD 248,000 (the "Deposit"), on or after the Effective Date. The Buyer shall pay the Deposit within ten (10) Business Days after receiving the invoice.

3.6 Balance. The Supplier may invoice the remaining eighty percent (80%) of the Total Contract Price, being USD 992,000 (the "Balance"), on or after the date on which Delivery is complete.

3.7 Payment terms. Except as stated in clause 3.5, the Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receiving it. Payments shall be made in US dollars by wire transfer to the bank account stated in the invoice.

3.8 Late payment. The Supplier may charge interest on any undisputed amount that the Buyer does not pay when due at the rate of one percent (1%) per month, from the due date until the date of payment, calculated daily.

3.9 Disputed invoices. If the Buyer disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier in writing of the disputed amount and its reasons, and shall pay any undisputed part of the invoice when due.

3.10 Set-off. The Buyer may set off any liquidated damages payable under clause 7 against any amount due to the Supplier under this Agreement.

4. DELIVERY

4.1 Dispatch notice. The Supplier shall notify the Buyer's Project Contact in writing, at least three (3) Business Days before dispatch, of the planned dispatch date, the carrier, the tracking number, the number of crates and the estimated time of arrival at the Delivery Point.

4.2 Delivery Date. The Supplier shall deliver all Units to the Delivery Point on or before May 15, 2026.

4.3 Delivery hours and delivery note. Deliveries shall be made between 07:00 and 17:00 on a Business Day, at a time booked in advance with the Buyer's Project Contact. Each delivery shall be accompanied by a delivery note stating the Purchase Order number, the serial number of each Unit and the number of crates. Signature of a delivery note by the Buyer's personnel confirms receipt of the number of crates stated on it only. It is not Acceptance and does not waive any right of the Buyer.

4.4 Completion of Delivery. Delivery is complete only when all forty (40) Units have been delivered to the Delivery Point. A Unit rejected under clause 6.2 is treated as not delivered until a conforming replacement Unit has been delivered to the Delivery Point.

4.5 Information about delays. The Supplier shall tell the Buyer's Project Contact of any circumstance likely to delay Delivery as soon as it becomes aware of it. Information given under this clause 4.5 is not a notice under clause 8.2.

5. RISK AND TITLE

5.1 Risk. Risk of loss of or damage to each Unit passes to the Buyer when that Unit has been unloaded at the Delivery Point.

5.2 Title. Title to each Unit passes to the Buyer on Delivery of that Unit, free of all liens and encumbrances.

5.3 Insurance. Until risk in a Unit passes to the Buyer, the Supplier shall keep that Unit insured for its full replacement value.

6. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

6.1 Inspection. The Buyer may inspect and test the Units within three (3) Business Days after the day on which they arrive at the Delivery Point (the "Inspection Period").

6.2 Rejection. The Buyer may reject any Unit that does not conform to the Specifications, or that is damaged, by written notice to the Supplier given within the Inspection Period. The notice shall state the serial number of each rejected Unit and the reasons for its rejection.

6.3 Replacement. The Supplier shall, at its own cost, remove each rejected Unit and deliver a conforming replacement Unit to the Delivery Point within ten (10) Business Days after the notice of rejection. A replacement Unit may be inspected and rejected under clauses 6.1 and 6.2 as if it were a Unit delivered under clause 4.

6.4 Acceptance. A Unit is Accepted on the earlier of:
(a) the Buyer's issue of an Acceptance Certificate for that Unit; and
(b) the end of the Inspection Period for that Unit without a notice of rejection under clause 6.2.

6.5 Effect of Acceptance. Acceptance does not limit the Supplier's obligations under clause 9.

7. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES FOR LATE DELIVERY

7.1 Rate. If Delivery is not complete by the Delivery Date, the Supplier shall pay the Buyer liquidated damages of one percent (1%) of the Total Contract Price for each week of delay, from the day after the Delivery Date until the day on which Delivery is complete, calculated pro rata on a daily basis for any part of a week.

7.2 Cap. The total liquidated damages payable under clause 7.1 shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the Total Contract Price.

7.3 Genuine pre-estimate. The Parties agree that the Buyer's losses from late Delivery would be difficult to estimate at the Effective Date, that the amounts in clause 7.1 are a reasonable pre-estimate of those losses and not a penalty, and that the Buyer is not required to prove its loss.

7.4 Sole financial remedy for delay. Liquidated damages under this clause 7 are the Buyer's sole financial remedy for late Delivery, without prejudice to its rights under clauses 6, 9 and 11.

7.5 Payment. Liquidated damages are payable within thirty (30) days after the Buyer's written demand, or may be set off under clause 3.10.

8. FORCE MAJEURE

8.1 Definition. A "Force Majeure Event" means an event beyond the reasonable control of the Party affected by it (the "Affected Party") that prevents or delays the performance of its obligations, including flood, fire, earthquake, storm, epidemic, war, terrorism, riot, government order and industry-wide strikes, but excluding the matters listed in clause 8.4.

8.2 Notice. The Affected Party may claim relief under this clause 8 only if it gives the other Party written notice, in accordance with clause 14, within five (5) Business Days after it first becomes aware of the Force Majeure Event. The notice must state that it is given under this clause 8.2 and must describe:
(a) the Force Majeure Event and the date on which it began;
(b) the obligations whose performance it prevents or delays;
(c) its expected duration; and
(d) the steps the Affected Party is taking to mitigate its effects.

8.3 Relief. Subject to clause 8.2, the Affected Party is not in breach of this Agreement, and is not liable for liquidated damages under clause 7, for any failure or delay in performance caused by the Force Majeure Event, for as long as the Force Majeure Event continues. No relief is available for any period before a notice that complies with clause 8.2 is given.

8.4 Exclusions. None of the following is a Force Majeure Event:
(a) lack of funds;
(b) a delay or failure of any subcontractor or supplier of the Affected Party, unless it is caused by an event that would be a Force Majeure Event if it affected the Affected Party directly;
(c) a strike or labor dispute involving only the Affected Party's own workforce; and
(d) breakdown of the Affected Party's own plant or equipment.

8.5 Mitigation and updates. The Affected Party shall use all reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects of the Force Majeure Event and shall give the other Party a written update at least every ten (10) Business Days while it continues.

8.6 Prolonged Force Majeure. If a Force Majeure Event prevents performance for more than sixty (60) consecutive days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

SCHEDULE 1 - SPECIFICATIONS

1. Model. Apex IC-450 industrial process chiller, air-cooled, packaged for outdoor installation.
2. Cooling capacity. Not less than 60 kW at 7 degrees C leaving water temperature and 35 degrees C ambient air temperature.
3. Refrigerant circuits. Two independent refrigerant circuits using refrigerant R-513A, each driven by one scroll compressor module.
4. Electrical supply. 480 V, three-phase, 60 Hz.
5. Controls. Microprocessor controller with a Modbus TCP interface and a local display.
6. Dimensions and weight. Approximately 2.1 m long, 1.1 m wide and 1.9 m high; approximately 640 kg per Unit.
7. Factory acceptance test. Each Unit to be run-tested for not less than four (4) hours, and each refrigerant circuit pressure-tested and leak-tested, at the Supplier's plant before dispatch.
8. Serial numbers. Each Unit to carry a unique serial number in the form IC450-11XX.
9. Documentation. Operation and maintenance manual, wiring diagrams and a signed factory test certificate for each Unit.
