# BOARD PROCEDURES POLICY OF ALDER FINCH LTD

Version 2.0. Adopted by the Board on 20 January 2026 with effect from 1 February 2026. Owner: Company Secretary.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy sets out how the Board of Alder Finch Ltd (the "Company") meets and takes decisions. It supplements the Company's Articles of Association adopted on 14 June 2023 (the "Articles").

1.2 If this policy conflicts with the Articles or the Companies Act 2006, the Articles or the Act prevail.

## 2. Composition

2.1 The Board shall consist of not less than two (2) directors and not more than seven (7) directors, as the Articles provide.

2.2 At the date of this policy the Board has four directors: Catherine Osei (Chair, non-executive director), Amélie Moreau (Chief Executive Officer), Benedikt Lind (Chief Technology Officer) and Owen Achebe (Investor Director).

2.3 The Chair is appointed by the Board. The Chair does not have a casting vote.

## 3. Meetings and Notice

3.1 The Board shall meet at least six (6) times a year.

3.2 Board meetings may be called on seven (7) days' notice by the Chair, or by any director through the Company Secretary.

3.3 Where the Chair considers a matter urgent, a meeting may be convened at shorter notice if all directors agree.

3.4 The agenda and board papers shall be circulated at least three (3) days before each meeting.

3.5 Directors may attend by video conference or telephone and are then counted as present.

## 4. Quorum

4.1 A board meeting is quorate when two (2) directors are present, one of whom must be the Investor Director if one is in office.

4.2 If a meeting is not quorate because the Investor Director is absent, it is adjourned to the same time and place seven (7) days later, and at the adjourned meeting any two directors form a quorum.

4.3 The Company Secretary shall record in the minutes the directors present and whether the meeting was quorate.

## 5. Decisions

5.1 Decisions are taken by a majority of the votes of the directors present and entitled to vote.

5.2 A written resolution is valid if it is signed by all the directors who would have been entitled to vote on the matter at a meeting.

## 6. Conflicts of Interest

6.1 Each director shall declare the nature and extent of any interest in a matter on the agenda at the start of the meeting, as section 177 of the Companies Act 2006 requires.

6.2 A director with a declared interest may take part in the discussion and vote only if the other directors authorise it under the Articles. The minutes shall record the declaration and the decision taken on it.

6.3 The Company Secretary keeps a register of directors' interests, which is reviewed by the Board each year.

## 7. Minutes

7.1 The Company Secretary shall prepare minutes of each meeting recording attendance, quorum, declarations of interest, the resolutions passed and the votes cast.

7.2 Draft minutes shall be circulated to the directors within ten (10) Business Days after the meeting, approved at the next meeting and signed by the Chair.

## 8. Matters Reserved for the Board

8.1 The following matters are reserved for the Board and may not be delegated:
(a) the allotment of shares and the grant of options, within the limits of the Articles;
(b) the annual budget and business plan;
(c) contracts and commitments above the limits in the Delegation of Authority;
(d) borrowing, guarantees and security of any amount;
(e) starting or settling litigation where more than GBP 100,000 is at stake;
(f) the appointment and removal of the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the Company Secretary; and
(g) the approval of this policy and of the Delegation of Authority.

8.2 Some reserved matters also require Investor Consent under Article 17 of the Articles. The Company Secretary shall check whether Investor Consent is needed before any such matter is put to the Board.

## 9. Committees

9.1 The Audit and Risk Committee consists of Catherine Osei (chair) and Owen Achebe. It meets at least twice a year.

9.2 The Remuneration Committee consists of Owen Achebe (chair) and Catherine Osei. It approves the remuneration of executive directors and any hire with a base salary above GBP 120,000.

## 10. Information for Investors

10.1 The Investor Director may share board papers and minutes with the managers of Brightwater Ventures Fund II LP, as Article 16.3 of the Articles permits, subject to their duty of confidentiality.

## 11. Review

11.1 The Board shall review this policy each year. The next review is due in January 2027.
