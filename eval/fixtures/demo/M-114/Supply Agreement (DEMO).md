## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a bank holiday in England.

"Change of Control" means any person, alone or together with persons acting in concert with it, acquiring more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting shares of the Supplier.

"Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by one party to the other under or in connection with this Agreement.

"Contract Year" means each period of twelve (12) months starting on the Commencement Date or on an anniversary of it.

"Delivery Location" means the Customer's distribution centre at Wakefield Gate Distribution Centre, Wakefield WF2 0XQ, or any other location in England that the Customer notifies to the Supplier at least ten (10) Business Days in advance.

"Forecast" means the Customer's non-binding forecast of its requirements for Products, given under clause 4.2.

"Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, trade marks, design rights, copyright, database rights and all similar rights anywhere in the world, whether registered or not.

"Order" means a written order for Products issued by the Customer under clause 4.

"Price" means the price for each Product set out in Schedule 2.

"Products" means the wireless sensors and gateways described in Schedule 1.

"Specification" means the specification for each Product set out in Schedule 1.

"Territory" means England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

1.2 Headings do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement. References to clauses and Schedules are to clauses of and Schedules to this Agreement.

## 2. Supply

2.1 The Supplier shall supply, and the Customer shall buy, Products in accordance with each Order accepted under clause 4.

2.2 This Agreement does not oblige the Customer to place any minimum quantity of Orders. Each Forecast is for planning purposes only.

2.3 The Supplier shall supply Products that conform to the Specification.

## 3. Term

3.1 This Agreement starts on the Commencement Date and continues for thirty-six (36) months, unless terminated earlier under clause 11.

3.2 After the end of the first Contract Year, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other not less than six (6) months' written notice.

## 4. Orders and Forecasts

4.1 The Customer shall place each Order through the Supplier's online ordering portal or by email. The Supplier shall accept or reject each Order within three (3) Business Days of receipt, and an Order that is not rejected within that period is accepted.

4.2 The Customer shall give the Supplier a Forecast covering the following six (6) months by the fifth Business Day of each month.

4.3 The Supplier shall not reject an Order for quantities up to one hundred and twenty percent (120%) of the most recent Forecast for the month concerned.

## 5. Price and Payment

5.1 The Customer shall pay for each Product at the price stated in the relevant Order (the "Price").

5.2 The Price excludes VAT, which the Customer shall pay in addition, and includes delivery to the Delivery Location.

5.3 The Supplier shall invoice the Customer on delivery of each Order.

5.4 The Customer shall pay each invoice within sixty (60) days after the end of the month in which it receives the invoice.

5.5 If the Customer fails to pay any amount when due, it shall pay interest on that amount at four percent (4%) a year above the Bank of England base rate, from the due date until payment.

5.6 The Supplier may increase the Price of any Product once in each Contract Year, with effect from an anniversary of the Commencement Date, by no more than three percent (3%), by giving the Customer at least sixty (60) days' written notice.

## 6. Delivery, Risk and Title

6.1 The Supplier shall deliver each Order to the Delivery Location within fifteen (15) Business Days after accepting it.

6.2 Products are at the Customer's risk from delivery. The Customer becomes the owner of Products when the Supplier has received payment for them in full.

6.3 If the Supplier delivers an Order more than five (5) Business Days late, the Customer may cancel the undelivered part of that Order without liability.

## 7. Quality and Warranty

7.1 The Supplier warrants that for twenty-four (24) months from delivery the Products will conform to the Specification and be free from defects in design, materials and workmanship.

7.2 If any Products do not comply with clause 7.1, the Supplier shall, at the Customer's option, repair or replace them, or refund the Price paid for them.

7.3 The Customer shall notify the Supplier of any defect within thirty (30) days after discovering it.

## 8. Intellectual Property

8.1 The Supplier and its licensors retain all Intellectual Property Rights in the Products and in the software embedded in them.

8.2 The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use the software embedded in the Products, and to use the Supplier's trade marks to promote the Products in its stores and online.

## 9. Liability

9.1 Each party's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed GBP 750,000. This limit does not apply to the Customer's obligation to pay for Products delivered.

9.2 Neither party is liable for any loss of profit or any indirect or consequential loss.

9.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for:

(a) death or personal injury caused by negligence;

(b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; or

(c) breach of the terms implied by section 12 of the Sale of Goods Act 1979.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement, during its term and for three (3) years after it ends.

## 11. Termination

11.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days after written notice of the breach.

11.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent or enters administration or liquidation.

11.3 The Customer may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice if the Supplier undergoes a Change of Control. The Supplier shall notify the Customer in writing within ten (10) Business Days after any Change of Control.

11.4 Termination does not affect the rights of either party accrued before it takes effect. Clauses 9, 10 and 13 survive termination.
