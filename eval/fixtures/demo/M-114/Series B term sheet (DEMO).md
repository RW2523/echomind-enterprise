# SERIES B TERM SHEET — ALDER FINCH LTD

Date: 9 October 2026

This term sheet summarises the principal terms of a proposed investment in Alder Finch Ltd. Except for paragraphs 20 to 24, it is not legally binding, and no party is obliged to complete the investment until the Investment Agreement and the New Articles have been signed.

## Parties

1. Company: Alder Finch Ltd, company number 13248871, registered office Unit 4, Harbourside Works, Cumberland Road, Bristol BS1 6XG.

2. Lead Investor: Larchmont Growth Partners LP, acting by its manager Larchmont Capital LLP.

3. Other Investor: Brightwater Ventures Fund II LP, an existing holder of Series A Preferred Shares (together with the Lead Investor, the "Investors").

4. Founders: Amélie Moreau and Benedikt Lind.

## Investment

5. Security: Series B preferred shares of GBP 0.0001 each in the capital of the Company (the "Series B Shares").

6. Amount: GBP 12,600,000 in aggregate, subscribed as follows: Larchmont Growth Partners LP, GBP 10,500,000 for 3,000,000 Series B Shares; Brightwater Ventures Fund II LP, GBP 2,100,000 for 600,000 Series B Shares.

7. Price: GBP 3.50 per Series B Share.

8. Valuation: a pre-money valuation of GBP 38,500,000 on a fully diluted basis, calculated on 11,000,000 shares, being the 10,520,000 shares in issue and the 480,000 Ordinary Shares under outstanding options. The post-money valuation is GBP 51,100,000.

9. Capitalisation after Completion: 14,120,000 issued shares, made up of 8,520,000 Ordinary Shares, 2,000,000 Series A Preferred Shares and 3,600,000 Series B Shares. The Lead Investor will hold approximately 21.2% of the issued shares and approximately 20.5% on a fully diluted basis.

## Rights of the Series B Shares

10. Liquidation preference: on a liquidation or an Exit, each Series B Share receives the greater of its issue price and its share of the proceeds as if converted, ranking ahead of the Series A Preferred Shares and the Ordinary Shares (one times, non-participating).

11. Conversion: each Series B Share converts into one Ordinary Share at the holder's option, and automatically on an IPO.

12. Anti-dilution: broad-based weighted average protection for issues below GBP 3.50 per share, with customary exceptions, including options under the Share Option Plan.

13. Voting: one vote per Series B Share, voting with the Ordinary Shares and the Series A Preferred Shares as one class.

## Governance

14. Board: the Board will have up to five directors. The Lead Investor may appoint one director (the "Series B Director"). Brightwater Ventures Fund II LP keeps its right to appoint the Investor Director.

15. Consent matters: the matters in Article 17 of the current Articles will require the consent of the holders of a majority of the Series B Shares as well as Investor Consent.

16. Information rights: monthly management accounts within twenty (20) days after month end; annual budget at least thirty (30) days before the start of each financial year; audited accounts within one hundred and twenty (120) days after year end.

17. Founders: each Founder will enter into a new service agreement with a twelve (12) month non-compete. The Founders' Ordinary Shares will be subject to customary leaver provisions in the New Articles.

## Conditions

18. Completion is conditional on:
(a) legal, financial and technical due diligence satisfactory to the Lead Investor;
(b) an Investment Agreement containing customary warranties from the Company and the Founders;
(c) the adoption of new articles of association (the "New Articles") creating the Series B Shares;
(d) delivery of a confirmatory IP assignment deed from Benedikt Lind in a form agreed with the Lead Investor; and
(e) shareholder resolutions authorising the directors to allot the Series B Shares and disapplying pre-emption rights for the investment.

19. Warranty limits: the Company's liability under the warranties is limited to the amount invested, and each Founder's liability is limited to GBP 150,000.

## Binding Provisions

20. Exclusivity: for forty-five (45) days from the date of this term sheet, until 23 November 2026, the Company and the Founders shall not solicit, encourage or negotiate any other equity investment in, or sale of, the Company.

21. Confidentiality: the parties shall keep the existence and terms of this term sheet confidential, except for disclosure to their professional advisers and existing investors.

22. Costs: the Company shall pay the Lead Investor's reasonable legal and due diligence costs, up to GBP 60,000 plus VAT, on Completion. If the investment does not complete, each party bears its own costs.

23. Timetable: the parties aim for Completion on 15 December 2026.

24. Governing law: this term sheet is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
