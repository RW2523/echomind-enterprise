### Warranty 5 — Material contracts

3.4 Northwind Components Ltd. The Company buys sensor modules and hub boards under a supply agreement with Northwind Components Ltd dated 29 January 2026, which runs until 31 January 2028 and contains a minimum purchase commitment of 50,000 NW-T4 sensor modules and 2,400 NW-H1 hub boards, with a value of GBP 864,000. By 30 September 2026 the Company had bought 21,000 NW-T4 sensor modules and 1,050 NW-H1 hub boards under it. Northwind may terminate on ninety (90) days' notice if the Company comes under the control of a competitor of Northwind (clause 10.3).

3.5 Tidewater Retail Ltd. The Company supplies FinchSense S2 sensors and FinchSense Hub gateways to Tidewater Retail Ltd under a supply agreement dated 16 February 2026 with a term of thirty-six (36) months. Tidewater may terminate on thirty (30) days' written notice if the Company undergoes a change of control, meaning the acquisition by any person of more than fifty percent (50%) of the Company's voting shares (clauses 1.1 and 11.3). The Warrantors do not consider that the investment will be a change of control under that agreement.

3.6 In May 2026, 312 FinchSense S2 sensors delivered to Tidewater Retail Ltd failed calibration checks. The Company replaced them under clause 7.2 of the Tidewater agreement at a cost of GBP 12,012, being 312 sensors at GBP 38.50 each. Tidewater has made no further claim.
