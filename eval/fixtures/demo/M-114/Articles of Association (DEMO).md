# ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF ALDER FINCH LTD

Company number 13248871 (England and Wales)

Private company limited by shares

Adopted by special resolution passed on 14 June 2023

## Part 1 — Interpretation

### 1. Model Articles

1.1 The model articles for private companies limited by shares in Schedule 1 to the Companies (Model Articles) Regulations 2008 (the "Model Articles") apply to the Company, except where they are excluded or modified by these Articles.

1.2 Model Articles 11(2), 13, 14 and 44(2) do not apply to the Company.

### 2. Definitions

2.1 In these Articles:

"Act" means the Companies Act 2006.

"Articles" means these articles of association, as amended from time to time.

"Board" means the board of directors of the Company.

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a bank holiday in England.

"Exit" means a sale of all the issued shares of the Company, a sale of all or substantially all of its business and assets, or an IPO.

"Founders" means Amélie Moreau and Benedikt Lind.

"Investor Consent" means the prior written consent of the Investor Majority.

"Investor Director" means the director appointed under Article 14.2.

"Investor Majority" means the holders of more than fifty percent (50%) of the Series A Preferred Shares in issue.

"IPO" means the admission of any of the Company's shares to trading on a recognised investment exchange.

"Ordinary Shares" means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company.

"Original Issue Price" means GBP 2.00 for each Series A Preferred Share.

"Series A Preferred Shares" means the series A preferred shares in the capital of the Company.

"Share Option Plan" means the Alder Finch Ltd EMI Share Option Plan, under which options over up to 600,000 Ordinary Shares may be granted to employees and officers of the Company.

2.2 Words and expressions defined in the Act have the same meanings in these Articles, unless the context requires otherwise.

## Part 2 — Share Capital and Share Rights

### 3. Share Capital

3.1 The share capital of the Company is divided into Ordinary Shares and Series A Preferred Shares.

3.2 Each share has a nominal value of GBP 0.0001.

3.3 For the purposes of section 551 of the Act, the directors are authorised to allot Ordinary Shares up to a maximum of 8,500,000 Ordinary Shares in aggregate, including the Ordinary Shares in issue on the date these Articles are adopted and any Ordinary Shares allotted on the exercise of options under the Share Option Plan, but excluding Ordinary Shares allotted on the conversion of Series A Preferred Shares. This authority may be increased only by special resolution passed with Investor Consent, and expires on 13 June 2028.

3.4 For the purposes of section 551 of the Act, the directors are authorised to allot up to 2,000,000 Series A Preferred Shares.

3.5 Except as these Articles provide, the Ordinary Shares and the Series A Preferred Shares rank equally in all respects.

### 4. Dividends

4.1 Any dividend declared shall be distributed among the holders of the Ordinary Shares and the Series A Preferred Shares pro rata to the number of shares held, as if they were one class.

4.2 No dividend may be declared or paid without Investor Consent.

### 5. Return of Capital and Exit

5.1 On a return of capital on liquidation or otherwise, or on an Exit, the proceeds available for distribution to shareholders shall be applied: (a) first, in paying to each holder of Series A Preferred Shares, for each Series A Preferred Share held, the greater of the Original Issue Price and the amount it would receive if all the Series A Preferred Shares had been converted into Ordinary Shares immediately before the distribution; and (b) second, in distributing the balance among the holders of the Ordinary Shares pro rata to the number of Ordinary Shares held.

### 6. Conversion

6.1 A holder of Series A Preferred Shares may at any time, by notice to the Company, convert all or any of its Series A Preferred Shares into Ordinary Shares on the basis of one Ordinary Share for each Series A Preferred Share.

6.2 All Series A Preferred Shares convert automatically into Ordinary Shares on the same basis immediately before an IPO.

### 7. Anti-dilution

7.1 If the Company issues new shares at a price per share below the Original Issue Price, other than shares excluded under Article 10.4, the conversion rate in Article 6 shall be adjusted for the holders of Series A Preferred Shares on a broad-based weighted average basis, unless the Investor Majority waives the adjustment in writing.

### 8. Voting

8.1 Each Ordinary Share and each Series A Preferred Share carries one vote on a show of hands, on a poll and on a written resolution.

### 9. Variation of Class Rights

9.1 The rights attached to the Series A Preferred Shares may be varied only with Investor Consent. The rights attached to the Ordinary Shares may be varied only with the written consent of the holders of more than seventy-five percent (75%) of the Ordinary Shares in issue.

## Part 3 — Issue and Transfer of Shares

### 10. Pre-emption on New Issues

10.1 Unless the shareholders by special resolution and with Investor Consent decide otherwise, any new shares, or rights to subscribe for or convert into shares, that the Company proposes to issue shall first be offered to the shareholders pro rata to their holdings of shares, the Ordinary Shares and the Series A Preferred Shares being treated as one class.

10.2 The offer shall be made by notice stating the number and price of the shares offered and shall remain open for not less than fourteen (14) days. Shares not accepted shall be offered to the shareholders who accepted in full, pro rata to their holdings, before they may be offered to any other person.

10.3 Sections 561 and 562 of the Act do not apply to the Company.

10.4 Article 10.1 does not apply to Ordinary Shares allotted on the exercise of options granted under the Share Option Plan, or to Ordinary Shares allotted on the conversion of Series A Preferred Shares.

### 11. Transfers of Shares

11.1 No shareholder may transfer any share except as a permitted transfer under Article 11.2, in accordance with Article 11.3, or under Article 12 or Article 13.

11.2 A shareholder may transfer shares without restriction: (a) in the case of a Founder, with Investor Consent, to a trust for the benefit of that Founder or members of the Founder's family; and (b) in the case of a shareholder that is an investment fund, to another fund managed or advised by the same manager.

11.3 A shareholder who wishes to transfer shares in any other case shall give a transfer notice to the Company stating the number of shares and the price. The Company shall offer those shares to the other shareholders pro rata to their holdings, and the offer shall remain open for twenty-one (21) days. If the price is not agreed, it shall be the fair value certified by an independent accountant appointed by the Board. Shares not taken up within the offer period may be sold to the proposed buyer within sixty (60) days at no lower price.

11.4 The directors shall refuse to register any transfer that does not comply with these Articles.

### 12. Drag-along

12.1 If the holders of more than sixty percent (60%) of the shares in issue, including the Investor Majority, agree to sell all their shares to a bona fide buyer, they may require all the other shareholders to sell their shares to that buyer on the same terms, subject to Article 5.

### 13. Tag-along

13.1 No sale of shares that would result in a buyer, together with persons acting in concert with it, holding more than fifty percent (50%) of the shares in issue may be completed unless the buyer has offered to buy all the other shares in issue at the same price per share and on the same terms.

## Part 4 — Directors

### 14. Number and Appointment of Directors

14.1 Unless otherwise determined by ordinary resolution with Investor Consent, the number of directors shall not be less than two (2) and not more than seven (7).

14.2 The Investor Majority may appoint one director (the Investor Director), and may remove and replace that director, by notice to the Company.

14.3 The Board shall appoint one of the directors as chair of the Board.

### 15. Board Meetings

15.1 Not less than seven (7) days' notice shall be given of every board meeting, unless all the directors agree to shorter notice.

15.2 The quorum for board meetings shall be two (2) directors, one of whom must be the Investor Director if one is in office.

15.3 If a meeting is not quorate because the Investor Director is absent, it shall be adjourned to the same time and place seven (7) days later, and at the adjourned meeting any two (2) directors shall be a quorum.

15.4 Questions arising at a board meeting are decided by a majority of votes. The chair of the Board does not have a casting vote.

15.5 A decision of the directors may also be taken by a written resolution signed by all the directors who would have been entitled to vote on it at a board meeting.

15.6 A director may take part in a board meeting by video conference or telephone, and is then counted in the quorum.

### 16. Directors' Conflicts of Interest

16.1 The directors may, in accordance with section 175 of the Act, authorise any matter that would otherwise involve a director in a breach of the duty to avoid conflicts of interest. A director who has an interest in the matter does not count in the quorum or vote on the authorisation.

16.2 A director who has declared an interest in a proposed transaction or arrangement with the Company under section 177 of the Act may, if the other directors so authorise, take part in the discussion and vote on it.

16.3 The Investor Director may disclose information about the Company to the Investor Majority and to the managers of any fund that holds Series A Preferred Shares.

### 17. Matters Requiring Investor Consent

17.1 The Company shall not do any of the following without Investor Consent:
(a) alter its share capital or the rights attached to any shares, or allot any shares other than under the Share Option Plan;
(b) amend these Articles;
(c) sell, transfer or dispose of all or a material part of its business or assets;
(d) borrow, or incur any indebtedness, of more than GBP 500,000 in aggregate;
(e) change the nature of its business;
(f) appoint or remove the chief executive officer; or
(g) declare or pay any dividend.

## Part 5 — General Meetings and Administration

### 18. General Meetings

18.1 The quorum for a general meeting is two (2) persons entitled to vote, present in person or by proxy, one of whom must be a holder of Series A Preferred Shares or its proxy if any Series A Preferred Shares are in issue.

18.2 A poll may be demanded by the chair of the meeting or by any shareholder present.

### 19. Communications

19.1 The Company may send any notice or document to a shareholder or director by email or by publishing it on a website, in accordance with the Act.

### 20. Indemnity and Insurance

20.1 Subject to the Act, each director and officer of the Company may be indemnified out of the Company's assets against any liability incurred in that capacity, and the Company may buy and maintain insurance against such liability.
