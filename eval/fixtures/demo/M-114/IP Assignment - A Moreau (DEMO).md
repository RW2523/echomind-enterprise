# DEED OF CONFIRMATORY ASSIGNMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

This deed is dated 2 February 2026 and is made between:

(1) Amélie Moreau of 22 Clifton Vale, Bristol BS8 4PQ (the "Assignor"); and

(2) Alder Finch Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13248871, whose registered office is at Unit 4, Harbourside Works, Cumberland Road, Bristol BS1 6XG (the "Company").

## Background

(A) The Assignor is a founder, a director and the Chief Executive Officer of the Company.

(B) Before the Company was incorporated on 12 March 2021, and since then, the Assignor has created inventions, software, designs and other materials for the FinchSense wireless sensor products and monitoring service that the Company develops and sells.

(C) The Board of the Company resolved on 20 January 2026 that the Company should enter into a confirmatory assignment of intellectual property with each founder. This deed confirms and, so far as necessary, makes the assignment to the Company of the Assignor's intellectual property relating to the Business.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this deed:

"Assigned IP" means the Intellectual Property Rights listed in the Schedule and all other Intellectual Property Rights created by the Assignor, alone or with others, before the date of this deed that relate to the Business.

"Business" means the design, development, manufacture and sale of wireless environmental sensors, gateways and related monitoring software, as carried on by the Company at the date of this deed.

"Consideration" means the sum of GBP 1 (one pound).

"Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, rights to inventions, copyright and related rights, trade marks, rights in get-up, rights in designs, database rights, rights in confidential information and know-how, and all similar rights anywhere in the world, whether registered or unregistered, including all applications for them and all rights to claim priority from them.

## 2. Assignment

2.1 In consideration of the payment of the Consideration by the Company to the Assignor, receipt of which the Assignor acknowledges, Amélie Moreau, as Assignor, assigns to the Company absolutely, with full title guarantee, all her right, title and interest in the Assigned IP, including the right to sue and recover damages for any past infringement.

2.2 So far as any Assigned IP will come into existence after the date of this deed as a result of work done before it, the Assignor assigns that Assigned IP to the Company by way of present assignment of future rights.

2.3 The Assignor confirms that all Intellectual Property Rights created by her in the course of her employment with the Company already belong to the Company, and that nothing in this deed limits the Company's rights under section 11 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 or section 39 of the Patents Act 1977.

## 3. Moral Rights

3.1 The Assignor unconditionally and irrevocably waives all moral rights under Chapter IV of Part I of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, and all similar rights anywhere in the world, in the works included in the Assigned IP.

## 4. Further Assurance

4.1 The Assignor shall, at the Company's request and cost, sign all documents and do all things reasonably required to vest the Assigned IP in the Company and to register the Company as owner of the registrable Assigned IP, including the patent application and the trade mark application listed in the Schedule.

4.2 By way of security for her obligations under clause 4.1, the Assignor irrevocably appoints the Company as her attorney to sign any such document in her name if she has not done so within fourteen (14) days of the Company's written request.

## 5. Warranties

5.1 The Assignor warrants that:
(a) she is the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Assigned IP listed in the Schedule;
(b) she has not assigned, licensed or charged any of the Assigned IP to any person other than the Company;
(c) she is not aware of any claim by any person that any of the Assigned IP infringes the rights of a third party; and
(d) she has not disclosed any confidential know-how forming part of the Assigned IP to any person except in confidence for the purposes of the Business.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Assignor shall keep confidential all confidential information and know-how forming part of the Assigned IP, except as required for her duties to the Company or by law.

## 7. Costs

7.1 The Company shall pay all costs of preparing and registering the assignment of the Assigned IP, including official fees.

## 8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This deed and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
