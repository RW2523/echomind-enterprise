# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement is dated 29 January 2026 and is made between:

(1) Northwind Components Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 06120934, whose registered office is at 3 Foundry Way, Swindon SN2 8BA (the "Supplier"); and

(2) Alder Finch Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13248871, whose registered office is at Unit 4, Harbourside Works, Cumberland Road, Bristol BS1 6XG (the "Customer").

## Background

The Supplier manufactures printed circuit board assemblies. The Customer designs and sells FinchSense wireless sensors and wishes to buy sensor modules and hub boards from the Supplier for use in the FinchSense devices it sells.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a bank holiday in England.

"Commencement Date" means 1 February 2026.

"Contract Year" means each period of twelve (12) months starting on the Commencement Date or on an anniversary of it.

"Minimum Purchase Commitment" means the quantities of Products set out in clause 4.1.

"Order" means a written order for Products placed by the Customer under clause 3.

"Prices" means the prices for the Products set out in Schedule 2, as adjusted under clause 5.2.

"Products" means the NW-T4 sensor modules and the NW-H1 hub boards described in Schedule 1.

"Specification" means the specification for each Product referred to in Schedule 1.

"Term" means the period described in clause 2.1.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement starts on the Commencement Date and continues for twenty-four (24) months, until 31 January 2028, unless terminated earlier under clause 10.

2.2 The parties may extend this Agreement by written agreement signed by both of them.

## 3. Orders and Forecasts

3.1 The Customer shall place each Order in writing at least twenty (20) Business Days before the delivery date it requests. Each Order shall be for at least 2,000 NW-T4 sensor modules or 100 NW-H1 hub boards.

3.2 The Supplier shall accept or reject each Order within two (2) Business Days of receipt. The Supplier may reject an Order only if it exceeds the Customer's most recent forecast for that month by more than twenty-five percent (25%).

3.3 The Customer shall give the Supplier a rolling non-binding forecast of its requirements for the following six (6) months by the tenth Business Day of each month.

## 4. Minimum Purchase Commitment

4.1 The Customer shall buy at least 50,000 NW-T4 sensor modules and 2,400 NW-H1 hub boards during the Term.

4.2 The total contract value under this Agreement is GBP 864,000, being the Minimum Purchase Commitment at the Prices in Schedule 2.

4.3 If at the end of the Term the Customer has bought less than the Minimum Purchase Commitment, the Customer shall pay the Supplier, within thirty (30) days of the Supplier's invoice, an amount equal to thirty percent (30%) of the Prices of the quantities not bought.

## 5. Prices and Payment

5.1 The Prices are set out in Schedule 2. They exclude VAT and include delivery to the Customer's premises at Unit 4, Harbourside Works, Cumberland Road, Bristol BS1 6XG.

5.2 The Prices are fixed for the first Contract Year. With effect from 1 February 2027, the Supplier may increase the Prices by no more than three percent (3%) by giving the Customer at least sixty (60) days' written notice.

5.3 The Supplier shall invoice the Customer on dispatch of each Order.

5.4 The Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of the date of the invoice.

5.5 If the Customer pays late, the Supplier may claim interest and compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

## 6. Delivery, Risk and Title

6.1 The Supplier shall deliver each accepted Order to the Customer's premises on the delivery date stated in the Order.

6.2 Risk in the Products passes to the Customer on delivery. Title to the Products passes to the Customer when the Supplier has received payment for them in full.

6.3 If the Supplier delivers an Order more than ten (10) Business Days late, the Customer may cancel the undelivered part of that Order, and the cancelled quantities count towards the Minimum Purchase Commitment.

## 7. Quality and Warranty

7.1 The Supplier warrants that for eighteen (18) months from delivery the Products will conform to the Specification and be free from defects in materials and workmanship.

7.2 The Customer may reject a delivered batch of Products if more than one half of one percent (0.5%) of the units tested at incoming inspection fail to conform to the Specification.

7.3 The Supplier shall, at the Customer's option, repair or replace any non-conforming Products, or refund the Prices paid for them, within fifteen (15) Business Days of notice.

## 8. Liability

8.1 Each party's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed GBP 500,000.

8.2 Neither party is liable for any loss of profit or any indirect or consequential loss.

8.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or under Part I of the Consumer Protection Act 1987.

## 9. Insurance

9.1 The Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of at least GBP 5,000,000 for each claim during the Term and for three (3) years after it ends.

## 10. Termination

10.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days after written notice of the breach.

10.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent or enters administration or liquidation.

10.3 The Supplier may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days' written notice if the Customer comes under the control of a competitor of the Supplier.

10.4 Termination does not affect the rights of either party accrued before it takes effect. Clause 4.3 does not apply if the Customer terminates under clause 10.1 or 10.2.

## 11. Assignment

11.1 Neither party may assign or subcontract this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each party shall keep the contents of this Agreement and all confidential information received from the other party confidential during the Term and for three (3) years after it ends.

## 13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
