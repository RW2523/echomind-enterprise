# DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY OF ALDER FINCH LTD

Version 3.0. Adopted by the Board on 20 January 2026 with effect from 1 February 2026. Replaces version 2.1. Owner: Chief Financial Officer.

## 1. Principles

1.1 The Board of Alder Finch Ltd (the "Company") delegates the authorities below to the officers named. Any matter not delegated is reserved to the Board under the Board Procedures Policy.

1.2 All values exclude VAT. The value of a contract is its total value over its full term, including any minimum commitment and any renewal that the Company cannot refuse. Related contracts with the same counterparty are added together.

1.3 Where an authority below also requires Investor Consent under Article 17 of the Articles of Association, that consent must be obtained before the contract is signed.

1.4 An officer may not approve a matter in which he or she has a personal interest.

## 2. Purchase and Supply Contracts

2.1 The Head of Operations may sign purchase orders and contracts with a total value of up to GBP 25,000.

2.2 The Chief Financial Officer may sign contracts with a total value of up to GBP 150,000.

2.3 The Chief Executive Officer may sign contracts with a total value of up to GBP 1,000,000, provided that a contract with a total value above GBP 500,000, or with a term longer than twenty-four (24) months, must first be approved by the Board.

2.4 Contracts with a total value above GBP 1,000,000 must be approved by the Board and signed by two directors.

## 3. Customer Contracts

3.1 Customer contracts on the Company's standard terms of sale require approval by the Head of Sales for an expected annual value up to GBP 100,000, and approval by the Chief Executive Officer for an expected annual value up to GBP 1,000,000.

3.2 Customer contracts with an expected annual value above GBP 1,000,000, with a term longer than twenty-four (24) months, or on terms other than the Company's standard terms of sale, require Board approval and signature by two directors.

## 4. Capital Expenditure

4.1 Capital expenditure within the approved annual budget requires approval by the Chief Financial Officer for items up to GBP 50,000, and approval by the Chief Executive Officer for items up to GBP 250,000.

4.2 Capital expenditure above GBP 250,000, or outside the approved annual budget, requires Board approval.

## 5. People

5.1 The Chief Executive Officer may approve hires within the approved headcount budget.

5.2 Any hire with a base salary above GBP 120,000, and the remuneration of the executive directors, require the approval of the Remuneration Committee.

## 6. Banking and Payments

6.1 Payments of up to GBP 25,000 require one authorised signatory on the bank mandate.

6.2 Payments above GBP 25,000 require two authorised signatories, one of whom must be the Chief Executive Officer or the Chief Financial Officer.

6.3 Payments above GBP 250,000 require two authorised signatories, at least one of whom must be a director.

## 7. Borrowing, Guarantees and Security

7.1 Only the Board may approve borrowing, guarantees or security. Borrowing of more than GBP 500,000 in aggregate also requires Investor Consent.

## 8. Litigation

8.1 The Chief Executive Officer may start, defend or settle claims where up to GBP 100,000 is at stake. Larger claims are reserved to the Board.

## 9. Shares and Options

9.1 Only the Board may allot shares or grant options, and only within the authority to allot in the Articles of Association and with Investor Consent where the Articles require it.

## 10. Review

10.1 The Chief Financial Officer shall review this Delegation of Authority each year and propose any changes to the Board. The next review is due in January 2027.
