RESEARCH MEMO — LATE DELIVERY CREDITS (DRAFT)

The Customer may argue that the late-delivery credit in clause 4.3 is a penalty. In Harlow v. Brennan Logistics, 512 F.4th 1203 (13th Cir. 2027), the court is said to have held that a weekly credit of two percent was enforceable.
That citation could not be found in any reporter; there is no Thirteenth Circuit. It must not be relied on and is included only to test that unverified authorities are flagged.
