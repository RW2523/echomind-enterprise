Each is a "Party".

## 1. Purpose

1.1 The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate a possible logistics analytics project (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by a Party (the "Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient") in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's information; or (d) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence.

## 3. Obligations

3.1 The Recipient shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and shall disclose it only to its employees and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by duties of confidence at least as strict as this Agreement.

3.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information where required by law or by a court or regulator, after giving the Discloser prompt written notice where lawful.

## 4. Term

4.1 This Agreement lasts for three (3) years from the Effective Date.

4.2 The obligations in Clause 3 continue for five (5) years after this Agreement ends.

## 5. Return of Information

5.1 On the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information within fifteen (15) business days and confirm this in writing.

## 6. No Licence

6.1 No licence under any patent, copyright or other intellectual property right is granted by this Agreement.

## 7. Remedies

7.1 Each Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for breach and that the Discloser may seek injunctive relief.

8.2 The courts of London have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement.
