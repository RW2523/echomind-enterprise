# LEASE OF WAREHOUSE PREMISES

> FICTIONAL EVALUATION FIXTURE. Every party, address and amount is invented. Not a real lease and not legal advice.

This Lease is made on 20 March 2025 between **Crestmoor Estates Ltd** (the "Landlord") and **Voss-Lindqvist Logistics Ltd** (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises

1.1 The Landlord lets to the Tenant Unit 7, Harbour Road Trading Estate, Southampton SO14 3XY (the "Premises").

## 2. Term

2.1 The term is ten (10) years starting on 1 April 2025 and ending on 31 March 2035 (the "Term").

## 3. Rent

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of GBP 84,000 per year, exclusive of VAT, by equal quarterly payments in advance on the usual quarter days.

3.2 The rent shall be reviewed on 1 April 2030 to the open market rent. The rent payable after a review shall not be less than the rent payable immediately before it (upward only).

## 4. Deposit

4.1 On the date of this Lease the Tenant shall pay a rent deposit of GBP 21,000, to be held by the Landlord until the end of the Term.

## 5. Use

5.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only for storage and distribution within Use Class B8.

## 6. Repairs and Insurance

6.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good and substantial repair.

6.2 The Landlord shall keep the structure and roof of the Premises in repair.

6.3 The Landlord shall insure the building and the Tenant shall reimburse the Landlord for the insurance premium within twenty-one (21) days of demand.

## 7. Alienation

7.1 The Tenant may assign the whole of the Premises with the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. The Tenant shall not assign or underlet part only of the Premises.

## 8. Break Clause

8.1 The Tenant may end this Lease on 31 March 2030 by giving the Landlord at least six (6) months' prior written notice, provided that the Tenant has paid all rent due up to that date.

## 9. Force Majeure

9.1 Neither party is liable for any delay or failure in performing its obligations caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

9.2 If the Premises are unfit for use for more than thirty (30) consecutive days because of such an event, the rent shall be suspended until the Premises are fit for use again.

## 10. Governing Law

10.1 This Lease is governed by the law of England and Wales.

*Signed for Crestmoor Estates Ltd and for Voss-Lindqvist Logistics Ltd on 20 March 2025 (fictional).*
